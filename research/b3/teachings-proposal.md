# Teachings & Laws

Draft proposal only. No category, record or route is added to the app.
All 21 requested units remain held. The older Exodus hold remains separate.

A new Explore card could open a collection called **Teachings & Laws**.
Its blurb: “Explore teachings, laws, instructions, and lists in their written setting.”
Stories would keep their present scope. Instructions would never imply that an action happened.

Each card would name its form: teaching, law, ritual instructions, symbolic instructions, family list, or work list.
The visible form label matters. A family list is not a command or a story.
The collection introduction would explain that it also holds lists that help readers understand these texts.
The alternative is a broader “Texts & Teachings” title; the owner should decide before implementation.

Cards would show a title, form, passage range, short summary and “Read passage” action.
“Context” and “Sources” would open native disclosure controls below the summary.
There would be no event date, completed-action field, evidence verdict or historical accuracy badge.
A command in a text would not become advice to the reader.
Present-day application would need a separately attributed interpretation, with its limits visible.

The draft record fields would be: stable ID, held-unit ID, title, form, passage, summary, context and sources.
A review status would remain required. Optional links could connect existing Stories and Commandments records.
Links would not duplicate evidence, change a verdict, or imply that a command was followed.
Multiple literary forms could share a range. Reviewers could split long units while keeping the original hold ID.
A split must preserve all supplied verses and explain overlaps.

On phones, the collection would use one column, readable Inter text and 48-pixel controls.
Readers could filter by form and book, then open the passage without losing their place.
Images would be optional. Any imagined scene would say “Illustration” and would never serve as evidence.
Reduced motion, visible focus and browser back would follow the existing app behavior.
Method & Sources would remain in the footer. Deferred Explore cards would remain deferred.

## Three Example Cards

These are draft card concepts based only on supplied unit descriptions. No quotations are proposed.

### Offerings at the Tabernacle

- Hold: F01
- Form: Ritual instructions
- Passage: Leviticus 1:1–7:38
- Blurb: “Explore rules for offerings at the tent used for worship.”
- Summary: “This section gives rules for offerings at the tent used for worship. It describes rules without claiming that anyone followed them.”
- Context: “Read the full passage to compare the rules. Instructions alone do not show when or whether people followed them.”
- Action: Read passage
- Sources: Packaged KJV passage; supplied F01 unit and its B2 hold. Further source review remains open.

## Gates Before Implementation

The owner must approve the collection scope and how its title covers family and work lists.
Researchers must revise summaries that turn commands into completed actions, including F17.
F20’s wording must pass the KJV checks. No quotation or corrected number is invented here.
Each final record must retain its form, source range, uncertainty and links to its original hold.
New copy must pass sentence limits, jargon review and the collection’s grade 6–8 target.
All units stay drafts until their exact wording is reviewed and accepted.
