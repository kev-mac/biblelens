Joshua 8, 18, and 24 inline depictions have limited facial detail or rear views, so identity is uncertain rather than established. The farewell Story depicts an older Joshua with a white beard, consistent with elapsed years. No new scene uses the ambiguous Numbers 14 inline image as an identity reference.
