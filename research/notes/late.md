# Job through Malachi annotation expansion

## What was completed?

### Takeaway
27 original contextual findings cover all 22 assigned books through precise verse ranges. This is an expansion of selected passages, not a claim of exhaustive chapter review. `late.json` is the integration artifact.

### Cited Findings
- Apparent contradiction must preserve context: adjacent advice in [Proverbs 26](https://ebible.org/engwebp/PRO26.htm), inherited suffering in [Lamentations 5](https://ebible.org/engwebp/LAM05.htm), individual responsibility in [Ezekiel 18](https://ebible.org/engwebp/EZK18.htm), and the Tyre qualification in [Ezekiel 29](https://ebible.org/engwebp/EZK29.htm).
- Independent evidence supports a limited historical claim: Hezekiah tribute is recorded in the [British Museum prism catalog](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1855-1003-1); the [Metropolitan Museum Cyrus exhibition](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2013/cyrus-cylinder) supports Cyrus and a restoration policy but explicitly says Jews are not named on the cylinder.
- Cosmic poetry requires genre awareness: [Job 26](https://ebible.org/engwebp/JOB26.htm) and [Habakkuk 3](https://ebible.org/engwebp/HAB03.htm). No manufactured scientific error is assigned to a simile.
- All remaining note-level primary sources are included with each finding in late.json. Their claims are bounded interpretations of the primary text rather than external archaeological findings.

### Inferences
- The directly overlapping peace texts support a textual relationship but do not settle dependence: [Micah 4](https://ebible.org/engwebp/MIC04.htm), [Isaiah 2](https://ebible.org/engwebp/ISA02.htm).
- Conditional warnings require interpretation before classifying an averted disaster as a failed prediction: [Jeremiah 18](https://ebible.org/engwebp/JER18.htm), [Jonah 3](https://ebible.org/engwebp/JON03.htm).

### Gaps
- This file does not certify entire Bible or entire book review.
- No independent archaeological sources were sought for every historical setting; those notes explicitly identify their evidence as primary literary context.
