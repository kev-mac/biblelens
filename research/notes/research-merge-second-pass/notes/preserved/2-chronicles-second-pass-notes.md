## Considered and ruled out

- 1 Chronicles 13:1-5 beside 2 Samuel 6:1 (plan lead): belongs to the 1 Chronicles unit (D6), not looked at here.
- 2 Chronicles 7:10 (twenty-third day) beside 1 Kings 8:66: already issued as `dedication-feast-people-sent-away-eighth-day-or-twenty-third`.
- 2 Chronicles 1:14, 1:17 (chariots, horsemen, 600 and 150 shekels) beside 1 Kings 10:26, 10:29: same numbers in the Hebrew. The Greek of 1 Kings 10:29 prints 100 and 50; that is a 1 Kings verse, not issued here.
- 2 Chronicles 18 beside 1 Kings 22: "after certain years" (18:2) beside "in the third year" (1 Kings 22:2) is not a conflict; the lying spirit (18:21-22) is covered by `god-that-cannot-lie-or-the-lord-hath-put-a-lying-spirit-...`; Ahab's death and Joash the king's son agree.
- 2 Chronicles 10 beside 1 Kings 12 (Hadoram and Adoram, Jeroboam "in Egypt"): a name spelling and the same events; Kings' own 12:2-3 and 12:20 are a Kings question.
- 2 Chronicles 11:20-21 and 13:2: Maachah and Michaiah already issued (`abijam-mother-maachah-of-abishalom-or-michaiah-of-uriel`).
- 2 Chronicles 12:9-10 (gold shields taken, brass made): no conflict with 9:16; the gold shields were already considered in the first session.
- 2 Chronicles 12:13 (forty-one years, seventeen reigned) beside 1 Kings 14:21: same figures.
- 2 Chronicles 25:4 (children not put to death for fathers): obeys Deuteronomy 24:16, quoted; 25:5-13 numbers add up; 25:23 (400 cubits) beside 2 Kings 14:13 same; 25:24 "Obededom" is an addition, not a contrary.
- 2 Chronicles 26:2 "Eloth" and 2 Kings 14:22 "Elath": a spelling.
- 2 Chronicles 26:16-21 (Uzziah burning incense): a breach rebuked and punished in the passage; not V.
- 2 Chronicles 27:2 and 27:8: the reign figures (25, 16) are repeated and agree with 2 Kings 15:33; the 2 Kings 15:30 "twentieth year" question has `hoshea-began-in-jotham-twentieth-year-yet-jotham-reigned-sixteen`.
- 2 Chronicles 29:21 (seven rams and lambs "for a sin offering") against the sin-offering rules of Leviticus 4: the law there names bulls, goats and a lamb for a person; I did not find a verse that forbids a ram as a sin offering for the kingdom, so no conflict is stated.
- 2 Chronicles 29:34 (priests too few to flay) beside Leviticus 1:6 (the offerer flays): the Leviticus verse does not say priests flay, so no contrast.
- 2 Chronicles 30:2-3, 30:17-20 (second-month Passover, the unclean eat "otherwise than it was written", Hezekiah prays): the law's provision for the second month is Numbers 9:10-11; the passage reports the breach and the pardon (V requires it to be unchecked); not issued.
- 2 Chronicles 30:26 ("since the time of Solomon … not the like in Jerusalem") against 35:18 ("no passover like to that" since Samuel): they speak of different things (joy, passover); no direct clash.
- 2 Chronicles 32:21 (angel cut off the mighty men) beside 2 Kings 19:35 (185,000): no number in Chronicles, so no arithmetic conflict.
- 2 Chronicles 32:21 "bowels" (Hebrew מִיציאי מעיו): the KJV is literal.
- 2 Chronicles 32:24, 32:31 (Hezekiah's sickness and the Babylonian envoys) beside 2 Kings 20: the order of events in 2 Chronicles is not time-stamped; no conflict stated.
- 2 Chronicles 32:30 (upper watercourse of Gihon) beside 2 Kings 20:20: complementary.
- 2 Chronicles 6, 7, 19, 31: no wording contrast or arithmetic found; 6:36 and 1 Kings 8:46 agree.
