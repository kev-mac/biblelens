- 135:14 "he will repent himself": the same wording as Deuteronomy 32:36; the God-repents threads are covered elsewhere.
- 100:3 "and not we ourselves": the WLC text marks a written (לא, "not") and read (לו, "his") form; the KJV margin and the existing literature on this are not opened. **Lead for a later pass**; needs a second source before a T record.

- 136:13, 15 "Red sea": a sibling of the published Red Sea / Sea of Suph record; not checked whether that record lists these verses.

- 53 beside 14: 53:5 against 14:5–6 is a larger rewrite; Psalm 14 has a record on 14:3 only. **Lead.**
- 108 beside 57:7–11 and 60:5–12: same pattern as 18/2 Samuel 22; the Judah/lawgiver pair is already a published record (`judah-is-my-lawgiver-...`). The rest not compared line by line. **Lead.**
- 99:6 "Samuel among them that call upon his name" with "among his priests": no conflict stated.
- 37:25 "not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his seed begging bread": a speaker's statement, not set against another verse here.
- 143:2 "no man living be justified" beside 18:20–24 and 26:1 (the speaker's own righteousness): thematic, not issued.
