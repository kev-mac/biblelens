## Considered and ruled out

- 1 Chronicles 4:1 (sons of Judah include Hezron and Hur) against Genesis 46:12: "sons" in a genealogy can mean descendants; not stated as a contrast.
- 4:27, 4:41-43 (Shimei's sons; Hezekiah's days; 500 men): no figure to compare.
- 15:1-2, 15:15 (only Levites carry the ark, staves) and 15:13 (breach "not after the due order"): agree with Numbers 4 and 7; the breach is rebuked in the passage; not V.
- 15:26 (seven bullocks and seven rams) beside 2 Samuel 6:13 (oxen and fatling after six paces): different sacrifices, not stated as the same act.
- 15:19-21 Alamoth and Sheminith: the KJV transliterates them; they are musical terms of unknown sense, so the ordinary-word-name ruling was not applied.
- 15:29 and 2 Samuel 6:16: same event.
- 16:7-36 beside Psalms 105, 96 and 106: wording differences only, with no figure or contrast; Psalm parallels belong to the Psalms pass.
- 16:37-42 (ark in the tent, tabernacle at Gibeon): consistent within the book.
- 17:10 and 2 Samuel 7:11 ("will build thee an house"): same sense.
- 17:11-13 beside 2 Samuel 7:12-15 ("If he commit iniquity" absent from Chronicles): omission, not a contrary; no argument from silence.
- 23:4-5 (24,000 + 6,000 + 4,000 + 4,000 = 38,000): adds up.
- 24:4 (16 + 8 = 24 courses): adds up with the lots in 24:7-18.
- 26:4-8 (Obededom's 8 sons, 62 in all) and 26:9, 26:11: adds up as far as the text lets it be checked; the 62 includes "brethren" without names.
- 26:30, 26:32 (1,700; 2,700): no parallel.
- 27:1-15 (12 x 24,000): adds up; 27:2 Zabdiel beside 11:11 Hachmonite: a name only.
- 27:23-24 beside 21:5-6: "finished not" agrees with Levi and Benjamin left uncounted in 21:6.
- 29:4, 29:7 beside 22:14: further gifts, not a conflict.
- 29:22 "the second time" beside 23:1: consistent.
- 29:27 (7 + 33 = 40): adds up with 2 Samuel 5:5 allowing for the months.
- 29:7 "drams": KJV for a coin name; dating the daric needs a primary publication, none opened. Not issued.
- No V: the breaches in the book are rebuked in the passage.
