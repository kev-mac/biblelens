# Ezekiel second pass (workstream D10), 2026-10-20

First-time reading of the 15 Ezekiel chapters with no published record: 2, 3, 6, 7, 12, 13, 15, 22, 24, 26, 33, 35, 42, 44, 47. KJV and Brenton's English were screened verse by verse (low word overlap or length mismatch), then the Hebrew (WLC) and Brenton's Greek were fetched by script (bolls.life, ebible.org) and compared at each flagged verse. KJV quotes in `sides` were checked as substrings of the fetched KJV text. All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## Ledger
11 chapters records-draft (6, 7, 12, 13, 22, 24, 26, 33, 35, 44, 47); 4 reviewed-none (2, 3, 15, 42). KJV-level read against Brenton's English plus Hebrew/Greek at flagged verses; not a full Hebrew read.

## Considered and ruled out
- Brenton's Greek of 7:3-11 is shorter and its verses are out of step with the Hebrew numbering (the Greek 7:5-8 carry material the Hebrew has at 7:3-4 and 7:8-9); the shift is a numbering and order difference, and the wording itself repeats. Held as one whole-passage item for Codex if wanted.
- 7:24 ("worst of the heathen... possess their houses" not printed in Greek): same kind as the issued omissions, held to keep the batch small.
- 24:9 ("Woe to the bloody city" not printed): clause omission, held.
- 2:1, 2:4, 2:9-10, 3:2, 3:9, 3:13, 3:27: verse division, synonyms ("adamant / rock", "touched / clapping") or short clause omissions.
- 6:8, 6:10, 6:14, 12:5, 12:28, 13:3, 13:6-7, 13:11-12, 13:23, 15:2-8, 22:7-8, 22:28-31, 24:4-13, 26:2, 26:18, 26:21, 33:15-19, 33:32-33, 35:11, 44:8, 44:13-14, 44:25-31, 47:5, 47:11, 47:15-20: wording, transliteration (the border place names in 47:15-20 are spelled differently in Greek), or clause-level omission. 22:25 and 35:5 were issued; the others did not meet the "meaning differs" test.
- Chapters 42: Greek and Hebrew temple measures differ in several verses (42:1-12 chambers), but this needs a measurement-by-measurement table, and Ezekiel 40-48 measurements are already covered by published records (40:14, 40:49, 41:4, 45:1, 48:9). Held for a Codex-guided round.
- 15 (vine, fire): no qualifying difference.

## For Codex
- 7:13 sentence is hard in Hebrew; record sets out the two printed subjects only.
- 7:23: "chain" is rare in Hebrew; the scope says so.
- 33:25-26: Brenton's Greek chapter has 32 verses; the record notes the missing verse 26.
- 35:5, 47:8: the Hebrew words allow more than one rendering; scopes say the record does not claim the KJV is wrong.
