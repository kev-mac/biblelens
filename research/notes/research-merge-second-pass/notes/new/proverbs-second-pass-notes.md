# Proverbs second pass (workstream D), 2026-10-20

First-time reading of the 17 Proverbs chapters that had no published record: 2, 5, 7, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 19, 20, 21, 24, 25, 27. The KJV and Brenton's Greek were read side by side for every chapter; Hebrew (WLC) was fetched by script for all 17 and compared only at the verses that raised a question. All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## Issued (20 records, `proverbs-second-pass-records.json`), all T, witness Septuagint

**Plainly different word, name or person (16)**
- 2:7 "sound wisdom" against "salvation".
- 2:18, 9:18, 21:16 (one record): Hebrew רְפָאִים, KJV "the dead" each time; Greek "the earth-born" (2:18, 9:18) and "giants" (21:16). No claim about who they are; Isaiah 14:9 (already drafted) is a separate record.
- 5:19 "pleasant roe" against "colt".
- 7:22-23 "fool" against "dog"; the arrow strikes the man in the KJV, a deer in the Greek.
- 10:10 second half: the KJV repeats 10:8's "a prating fool shall fall"; the Greek has "he who reproves openly makes peace". (10:8 is quoted for contrast.)
- 12:9 "hath a servant" against "serving himself".
- 12:28 "no death" against "the ways of the revengeful are to death".
- 13:17 "wicked messenger" against "bold king".
- 14:32 "hope in his death" against "trusts in his own devoutness" (no death in the Greek clause).
- 16:15 "king's countenance" against "king's son".
- 17:2 "a son that causeth shame" against "foolish masters".
- 19:13 "contentions of a wife" against "prayers from a courtesan's hire are not pure".
- 21:4 "plowing" against "lamp".
- 21:18 "ransom" against "offscouring".
- 24:20 "reward" against "offspring".
- 25:1 "men of Hezekiah" against "friends of Hezekiah".
- 25:2 "search out" against "honours".

## Ruled out or not issued
- Greek pluses 9:12a-c, 17:6a, 17:16a, 12:11a, 12:13a, 13:9a, 13:13a, 15:18a, 15:27a-29b, 20:9a-c, 25:10a, 25:20a, 27:20a, 27:21a: they restate sentiments the same chapter or book already has (15:27a beside 16:6, 20:9c beside 20:22) or were held back to keep the batch small; none checked for a clean "KJV says nothing" test.
- 5:4 wormwood/gall, 12:4 rottenness/worm, 14:30 envy/sensitive heart, 14:34 reproach/diminish, 15:4, 15:15, 16:26, 19:6, 19:15, 19:18, 19:19, 19:27, 19:28, 20:4, 20:25, 25:23 (north wind drives away rain / stirs up clouds), 25:27, 27:8, 27:9, 27:24: real differences but either a range-of-senses case where the Hebrew word is disputed (20:25, 25:23, 19:18, 14:34) or too loose a paraphrase to state as a noun-for-noun difference. Candidates if the owner wants a larger Proverbs set.
- 24:21-22 ("them that are given to change" against "disobey neither"): the WLC text as served is garbled at 24:21-22 (`עִםשֹׁ־ונִים`, `נֵיהֶם`), so no exact quotation could be given. Not issued.
- 16:1, 16:3, 16:4, 16:6, 19:1-2, 20:14-22, 21:5: Brenton's Greek prints no label at these numbers. Not issued as omissions because the Greek chapters 16, 19, 20, 24 are arranged differently from the Hebrew (Brenton's chapter 24 carries the sayings of Agur and Lemuel at 22a-62; chapter 16 omits 1, 3, 4, 6), so the gap may be a relocation. 16:4 ("the wicked for the day of evil") is the only one with a clear theological weight; Brenton's 16:9 says the ungodly is "kept for an evil day". Held for a later look.
- Brenton's chapter 24 (22a-62) is the Greek form of Proverbs 30:1-31:9 and is covered by the existing Agur and Lemuel records.
- Nothing in chapters 20 and 27 qualified (logged reviewed-none).
