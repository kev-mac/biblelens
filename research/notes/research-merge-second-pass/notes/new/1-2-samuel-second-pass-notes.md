# 1 and 2 Samuel second pass (workstream D8), 2026-10-20

First-time reading of the 13 chapters with no published record: 1 Samuel 2, 5, 6, 20, 23, 24, 26, 29 and 2 Samuel 7, 9, 11, 12, 22. KJV and Brenton's Greek were read side by side for every verse; the Hebrew (WLC) and Greek were fetched by script (bolls.life, ebible.org) and compared at each verse that raised a question. KJV quotes in `text` and `sides` were checked against packaged `data/chapters.json` by script (the packaged text uses straight apostrophes and "Lord"). All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## Considered and ruled out
- 1 Sam 2:22 (clause "how they lay with the women that assembled at the door" is not printed in Brenton's Greek 2:22): a clause omission inside a verse, held back as in earlier batches (rule 3 covers whole verses). Quotes can be sourced if Codex wants a second round.
- 1 Sam 2:10 (Greek adds a long clause echoing Jeremiah 9:23-24), 2:32 (Greek omits "thou shalt see an enemy in my habitation"), 2:29 ("kick ye" / "look with a shameless eye"), 2:36: real differences or wording only; held for a lower-value round to keep the batch small.
- 5:6 and 6:1 (Greek adds that mice sprang up in the land): the KJV names the mice at 6:4-5 within the same story, so under the Genesis addition ruling this is likely dropped; not issued.
- 6:18 (Greek "village of the Pherezite" / KJV "country villages"), 6:7, 6:12: wording or obscure.
- 20:19 and 20:41 (Greek transliterates ergab / argab for "Ezel" and "toward the south"): both texts leave the word obscure; no demonstrated meaning difference. 20:25 (Greek "he forestalled Jonathan" against "Jonathan arose") needs a pointing reading; not issued. 20:30: wording.
- 23:14-24 place names (Maserem, Messara, Kaine, Iessaimou): transliteration. 24 (whole chapter): the Greek chapter numbering runs one verse behind the Hebrew (Greek 24:1 is Hebrew 23:29); differences found are synonyms (24:3 "cover his feet"), a transliterated place (24:2 "rocks of the wild goats"), and dropped clauses; nothing qualifying.
- 26:5 trench / wagon-circle, 26:6 Ahimelech / Abimelech, 26:1 Hachilah / Echela: spelling or an obscure word.
- 29:9 (Greek omits "as an angel of God"): clause omission, held.
- 2 Sam 7:7 (KJV "tribes", Greek "one tribe") is covered from the Chronicles side in `1-chronicles-second-pass-records.json`; 7:19, 7:23, 7:26 (Greek omits the second half of the verse): obscure or clause omission.
- 9:3 to 9:13: names (Lodabar, Machir) and the count of Ziba's sons agree in both texts.
- 11:3 (Eliam / Eliab) beside 1 Chronicles 3:5 (Ammiel): near-spelling and a family-list question; not issued. 11:21 (Jerubbesheth / Jerobaal son of Ner, Thebez / Thamasi): name forms.
- 22 beside Psalm 18: 22:8 and 22:16 are already in `psalms-second-pass-records.json`; 22:30 (KJV "run through a troop", Greek "monozonos", girded alone) and 22:36 ("gentleness" / "obedience") are real differences held for a lower-value round; the rest are synonyms.

## For Codex
- 2:25, 2:31, 12:30: each rests on Hebrew consonants that also read another way (a verb pointing, "arm"/"seed" as figures, מלכם as "their king" or a god's name); each scope says so and does not claim which is original.
- 22:35: the Hebrew noun נחושה is quoted; the record claims only that the KJV and Greek name different metals.
- 23:12 is a Septuagint page omission under owner ruling 3 of 2026-10-13; the record states the verse numbers the page prints (1-11, 13-28).
- 7:16 sits beside the untagged Chronicles record `mine-house-and-my-kingdom-or-thy-house-and-thy-kingdom-1-chronicles-17-14-and-2-samuel-7-16`; the two are different comparisons and both can stand.
- 5:10 quotes 6:16 as a second place of the same difference (sides on both); 6:17 is cited in reasoning only.
