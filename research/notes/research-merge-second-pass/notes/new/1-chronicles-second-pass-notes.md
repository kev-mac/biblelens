## Considered and ruled out
- 9:1-17 beside Nehemiah 11 (956 and 928; 1,760 and 1,192; 212 and 172; 690 and 468): already ruled out in both earlier notes as lists not stated to be the same count. 9:3 (Ephraim and Manasseh in Jerusalem) beside Neh 11:4: same reason.
- 23:3 beside 23:24, 23:27 (thirty or twenty years): the chapter gives the reason for the change (ruled out in 1-chronicles-1-29-notes.md). 23:3-5 adds up (24,000 + 6,000 + 4,000 + 4,000 = 38,000).
- 27:24 ("neither was the number put in the account of the chronicles of king David") beside 21:5: a different record is named; would need the scope to concede no conflict.
- 15:27 (KJV "ephod of linen", Greek "robe of fine linen" twice), 15:22 and 15:27 "song" for the Hebrew massa, 16:32 "fields" / "tree of the field", 29:11 "exalted as head above all" / "every king and nation is troubled", 29:21 "they sacrificed" / "David sacrificed", 26:14 (lots for Zechariah / "sons of Soaz cast lots for Melchias"; Greek obscure), 23:26 "no more", 4:22 "had the dominion in Moab" / "dwelt in Moab" with "ancient things" / "he changed their names", 4:23 "plants and hedges" / "Ataim and Gadira", 4:41 "habitations" / "Minaeans" (a written/read form, background only), 9:27 omitted clause "lodged round about the house of God", 9:41 omitted "Ahaz": real differences, held back as lower value to keep the batch small; quotes sourced if Codex wants a second round.
- 15:5-10, 15:17, 15:18-21, 16:5-6, 23:12-23, 24:1-31, 26:1-32 and 27:1-34: names, spellings, instrument words (Greek "lutes" for "psalteries"), and transliteration only.
- 16:7 "this psalm": the Hebrew has no noun; a KJV supplied word is not a finding. 16:8-36 beside Psalms 105, 96, 106: 16:13 and 16:27 already in `psalms-second-pass-records.json`; the other differences are synonyms.
- 24:3 and 24:6 (Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, and son of Abiathar): reconcilable by generations; no clash in wording.
- 26:1 (Asaph) beside 9:19 (Ebiasaph), 16:38 and 26:4 (Obededom the Gittite / son of Jeduthun / Korahite): identification of men; not issued.
- 27:7 Asahel as monthly captain: argument from the date of his death (ruled out earlier).
- 13:5, 13:9 (Chidon / Nachon): place-name spellings (ruled out earlier). 13:11 "displeased": near-synonym.
