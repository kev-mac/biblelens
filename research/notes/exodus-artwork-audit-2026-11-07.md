## Scene decisions

The burning bush retains bare feet and sandals set aside. The rescue, well, Passover, sea crossing, manna, water from the rock, Jethro, Sinai, covenant altar and calf scenes retain their chapter/Story roles. The Exodus 33 cleft correction removes both the veil and tablets: the first tablets have broken in chapter 32, the replacement pair is cut in chapter 34, and the veil follows the descent in 34:33. Moses now shields his eyes without representing a visible deity. Exodus 34 keeps two tablets and a lit face.

The retained sanctuary scenes depict the design instructions and subsequent work with the same natural blue, purple, scarlet, linen, wood, gold and bronze materials. Checked the seven-light lampstand, four-horned altar, twelve breastplate stones, shoulder fittings, curtains, winged figures, ark carrying poles, basin and completed camp. These imagined designs are not asserted to be archaeological reconstructions. Colors and light vary naturally across the book rather than receiving a uniform amber grade.
