## Follow-up criteria

Use the stable anchors above as visual inputs before replacing a scene. Resolve which man is Joshua or Caleb in Numbers 14 before using either inline image as a reference. Preserve the five daughters' established faces from the Numbers 27 and 36 opening scenes. Treat Moses in Deuteronomy 3, Aaron in Numbers 16, Eleazar in Numbers 20–27, and Miriam in Numbers 12 as pending continuity review rather than approved identity references. Use versioned replacement assets; keep all existing verse coverage.
