### Cited Findings
- **Two animals or seven pairs?** (Genesis 6:19; Genesis 6:20; Genesis 7:2; Genesis 7:3): Genesis 6 gives a general instruction to bring pairs; Genesis 7 specifies larger numbers of clean animals and birds. [Genesis 6 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/GEN06.htm); [Genesis 7 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/GEN07.htm)
- **Two reasons for the Sabbath** (Exodus 20:11; Deuteronomy 5:15): Exodus grounds Sabbath rest in creation; Deuteronomy grounds it in liberation from slavery. [Exodus 20 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/EXO20.htm); [Deuteronomy 5 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/DEU05.htm)
- **Speaking face to face and seeing God's face** (Exodus 33:11; Exodus 33:20; Exodus 33:23): The chapter describes face-to-face conversation but later says Moses cannot see God's face. [Exodus 33 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/EXO33.htm)
- **Different legal treatment of servants and slaves** (Leviticus 25:39; Leviticus 25:40; Leviticus 25:44; Leviticus 25:45; Leviticus 25:46; Exodus 21:2; Deuteronomy 15:12): Leviticus distinguishes impoverished Israelites from foreign slaves, allowing foreign slaves to be inherited as property. [Leviticus 25 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/LEV25.htm); [Exodus 21 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/EXO21.htm); [Deuteronomy 15 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/DEU15.htm)
- **Where did Aaron die?** (Numbers 20:28; Deuteronomy 10:6): Numbers locates Aaron's death on Mount Hor; Deuteronomy names Mosera. [Numbers 20 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/NUM20.htm); [Deuteronomy 10 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/DEU10.htm)
- **Complete conquest and continuing inhabitants** (Joshua 10:40; Joshua 11:23; Joshua 15:63; Judges 1:19; Judges 1:21; Judges 1:27; Judges 1:29; Judges 1:32): Broad victory summaries in Joshua sit alongside explicit reports that many inhabitants remained. [Joshua 10 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/JOS10.htm); [Joshua 11 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/JOS11.htm); [Joshua 15 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/JOS15.htm); [Judges 1 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/JDG01.htm)
- **A transfer confirmed with a sandal** (Ruth 4:7; Ruth 4:8; Ruth 4:9; Ruth 4:10): Ruth explains a sandal exchange as an earlier custom used to confirm redemption and transfer. [Ruth 4 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/RUT04.htm)
- **Jabesh Gilead and Saul's public legitimacy** (1 Samuel 11:1; 1 Samuel 11:2; 1 Samuel 11:7; 1 Samuel 11:11; 1 Samuel 11:14; 1 Samuel 11:15; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Samuel 10:27; 1 Samuel 31:11; 1 Samuel 31:12): The rescue of Jabesh turns disputed kingship into public recognition; the same town later recovers Saul's body. [1 Samuel 11 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1SA11.htm); [1 Samuel 10 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1SA10.htm); [1 Samuel 31 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1SA31.htm)
- **Saul's death: narration and a claimant's story** (1 Samuel 31:4; 1 Samuel 31:5; 2 Samuel 1:9; 2 Samuel 1:10; 2 Samuel 1:16): The narrator says Saul died after falling on his sword; an Amalekite later claims to have killed him. [1 Samuel 31 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1SA31.htm); [2 Samuel 1 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2SA01.htm)
- **Who prompted David's census?** (2 Samuel 24:1; 1 Chronicles 21:1): Samuel attributes the prompting to the LORD's anger; Chronicles names Satan. [2 Samuel 24 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2SA24.htm); [1 Chronicles 21 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1CH21.htm)
- **Different census totals** (2 Samuel 24:9; 1 Chronicles 21:5; 1 Chronicles 21:6): Samuel reports 800,000 for Israel and 500,000 for Judah; Chronicles reports 1,100,000 and 470,000. [2 Samuel 24 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2SA24.htm); [1 Chronicles 21 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1CH21.htm)
- **Seven or three years of famine?** (2 Samuel 24:13; 1 Chronicles 21:12): In the packaged KJV text, Samuel offers seven years of famine while Chronicles offers three. [2 Samuel 24 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2SA24.htm); [1 Chronicles 21 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1CH21.htm)
- **Solomon's horse stalls** (1 Kings 4:26; 2 Chronicles 9:25): Kings lists 40,000 horse stalls; Chronicles lists 4,000, while both list 12,000 horsemen. [1 Kings 4 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1KI04.htm); [2 Chronicles 9 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2CH09.htm)
- **Ahaziah's accession age** (2 Kings 8:26; 2 Chronicles 22:2): Kings gives Ahaziah's accession age as 22; the packaged KJV Chronicles gives 42. [2 Kings 8 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2KI08.htm); [2 Chronicles 22 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2CH22.htm)
- **Assyrian evidence for the attack on Lachish** (2 Kings 18:13; 2 Kings 18:14): An Assyrian palace relief depicts Sennacherib watching the capture of Lachish, corroborating that specific campaign setting. [2 Kings 18 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2KI18.htm); [British Museum · Lachish relief](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1856-0909-14_2)
- **Cyrus and restoration of local worship** (Ezra 1:1; Ezra 1:2; Ezra 1:3; Ezra 1:7): The Cyrus Cylinder describes restoration of sanctuaries and displaced peoples, providing context for Ezra's account of return. [Ezra 1 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/EZR01.htm); [British Museum · Cyrus Cylinder](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1880-0617-1941)
- **Two totals for the descendants of Arah** (Ezra 2:5; Nehemiah 7:10): Ezra lists 775 descendants of Arah; Nehemiah lists 652. [Ezra 2 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/EZR02.htm); [Nehemiah 7 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/NEH07.htm)
- **Two totals for Pahathmoab** (Ezra 2:6; Nehemiah 7:11): Ezra records 2,812 for Pahathmoab's group; Nehemiah records 2,818. [Ezra 2 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/EZR02.htm); [Nehemiah 7 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/NEH07.htm)
- **The listed vessels and the stated total** (Ezra 1:9; Ezra 1:10; Ezra 1:11): Ezra's itemized figures add to 2,499, while the stated total is 5,400. [Ezra 1 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/EZR01.htm)
- **Susa as a Persian royal setting** (Esther 1:2; Esther 1:5; Esther 1:6): The Persian palace remains from Susa provide a real archaeological setting for Esther's royal court. [Esther 1 · primary text](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/EST01.htm); [Louvre · Palace of Darius I in Susa](https://presse.louvre.fr/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/373c2545f0326e58c68488f608936318.pdf)
