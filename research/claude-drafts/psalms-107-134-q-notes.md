# Psalms 107–134 — Q notes (2026-10-13)

3 Q records in `psalms-107-134-q-records.json`. All are drafts until Codex accepts them. Verse numbers: the Hebrew and the KJV agree in the verses quoted (no numbering differences at Psalms 109, 110 and 126). All three records are KJV-only; no Hebrew or Greek is quoted in them.

1. `who-speaks-the-curses-of-psalm-109-6-19-the-psalmist-or-his-adversaries` (109:6; also 109:20) — the plural “they” of 109:2–5, the singular “him” of 109:6–19 and the plural “mine adversaries” of 109:20 leave open whose words the curses are. Three readings are named as possible readings and none is decided. The published Q on Satan’s standing cites 109:6 as context only; this question is about the speaker.
2. `the-lord-said-unto-my-lord-who-is-my-lord-in-psalm-110-1` (110:1) — the psalm verses quoted do not name “my Lord”. Matthew 22:43–45 and Acts 2:34–36 are quoted as context; the record says Acts applies the verse to Jesus and does not say whether that settles the psalm’s own setting. 110:4 is quoted as context only; the published Melchizedek question is separate. **Matthew 22:42–45 and Acts 2:34–36 are therefore covered here: do not re-issue this question in the Matthew, Mark, Luke or Acts sessions.**
3. `when-the-lord-turned-again-the-captivity-of-zion-which-turning-does-psalm-126-1-recall` (126:1; also 126:4) — the past “turned again” of 126:1–3 against the request of 126:4. Limited to the four verses; no other passage on a return is used.
