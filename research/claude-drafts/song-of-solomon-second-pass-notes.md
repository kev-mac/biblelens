# Song of Solomon second pass (workstream D9), 2026-10-18

Scope: the 2 chapters with no published record: 5 and 8. Method: KJV and Brenton's English read side by side for both chapters in full; Hebrew (bolls.life WLC) and Brenton's Greek (ebible.org) read at every verse that raised a question. All records are drafts until Codex accepts them. Verse numbering matches in chapters 5 and 8.

## Issued (5 records, `song-of-solomon-second-pass-records.json`), all T, witness Septuagint, status Context
1. `let-my-kinsman-come-down-into-his-garden-first-clause-greek` (5:1): Greek has an opening invitation clause the KJV/Hebrew lack.
2. `i-charge-you-that-ye-tell-him-or-i-have-charged-you-by-the-powers-and-forces-of-the-field-greek` (5:8): Greek adds the oath "by the powers and the forces of the field".
3. `i-would-instruct-me-in-my-mothers-house-or-into-the-chamber-of-her-that-conceived-me-greek` (8:2): "who would instruct me" against "chamber of her that conceived me".
4. `i-charge-you-that-ye-stir-not-up-or-by-the-forces-of-the-field-that-ye-stir-not-up-greek` (8:4): the same added oath phrase.
5. `who-is-this-that-cometh-up-from-the-wilderness-or-who-is-this-that-comes-up-all-white-greek` (8:5): "from the wilderness" against "all white".
