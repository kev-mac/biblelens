## Records

1. `tower-of-meah-or-tower-of-the-hundred-hebrew-greek` (H, G; 3:1, 12:39) — the Hebrew is the article and the ordinary number word, which the KJV itself renders “an hundred” at 7:24; the KJV keeps it as a name. Issued under the proper-names rule for H: ordinary words whose meaning the KJV’s name does not carry, and the record says the place may be the same. The Greek translates the word at 3:1.
2. `horses-and-mules-of-the-return-not-in-the-hebrew-or-greek-of-nehemiah` (H, G; 7:68) — the KJV verse is not in the WLC text of Nehemiah 7 and Brenton’s Greek page prints no verse 68. The same sentence is in all three at Ezra 2:66.
3. `camels-and-asses-of-the-return-greek-has-no-camels-and-2700-asses` (G; 7:69) — the Greek verse is “asses, two thousand seven hundred” and nothing else.
4. `gifts-to-the-work-in-nehemiah-greek-thirty-garments-and-other-silver` (G; 7:70–72) — 30 garments for 530, 2,300 for 2,200 and 2,200 for 2,000 pound of silver; “to Nehemiah” where the KJV has “The Tirshatha gave”.
5. `register-in-nehemiah-five-entries-differ-in-the-greek` (G; 7:11, 7:24, 7:26, 7:33, 7:34) — four different figures and one added entry in the Greek list.
6. `appointed-a-captain-in-their-rebellion-or-in-egypt-greek` (G; 9:17) — the Greek has “in Egypt” and no “in their rebellion”.
7. `ten-times-and-four-times-not-in-the-greek-of-nehemiah` (G; 4:12, 6:4) — neither count is in the Greek; 4:12 is also worded differently.
8. `dwellers-in-jerusalem-and-the-villages-nine-verses-not-in-the-greek` (G; 11:16, 20, 21, 28, 29, 32–35) — nine KJV verses with no printed counterpart on the Greek page.
9. `second-company-on-the-wall-and-nehemiah-after-them-not-in-the-greek` (G; 12:38, 12:40, 12:41) — the verses that send out the second company and bring both to the house of God are not printed.
10. `booths-not-done-since-jeshua-the-son-of-nun-or-feast-of-tabernacles-kept` (untagged; 8:17 against Ezra 3:4) — a clash as worded, on one reading of “done so”; the scope names the readings on which both stand.
11. `joiada-begat-jonathan-or-johanan-after-joiada-high-priests` (untagged; 12:11 against 12:22) — the name between Joiada and Jaddua differs between two lists in one chapter; the scope says verse 22 is not stated to be a line of fathers and sons.

## Points for Codex

- **Record 2 rests on a verse being absent, not on a word being rendered differently.** The H test asks for a shown difference of meaning; here the KJV states a count that the Hebrew edition does not state in this book. I judged that this meets the standing test, and tagged G as well because the Greek agrees with the Hebrew against the KJV. If you find that an absent verse needs a decision you have not been given, hold the H and keep the G; the scope already says other Hebrew copies were not examined.
- **Record 1** carries both tags on a record with two verses. H applies at both; G applies at 3:1. At 12:39 the Greek page prints no second tower; the scope says so. Narrow `appliesTo` for G if your display needs it.
- **Records 8 and 9 are omission records.** To check them, read the printed verse labels of `EZR21.htm` and `EZR22.htm`; the label lists are quoted in the records and asserted by script. The verses printed on either side of each gap are quoted in full.

## How the book was compared

- All 13 chapters were fetched in WLC, Brenton’s Greek and Brenton’s English. The book was not read in full in any of them.
- A script compared the number words of every KJV verse with Brenton’s English at the same verse number; its output was read with the numbering differences above in mind. Records 3, 4, 5 and 7 came from it.
- The printed verse labels of every Greek page were set against the KJV’s verse counts; records 2, 8 and 9 came from that.
- Verses read in KJV, Hebrew and Greek: 1:1, 1:3; 2:1; 3:1, 3:6–8; 4:6, 4:12; 6:4; 7:10–11, 7:22, 7:24–26, 7:33–34, 7:67–73; 8:17; 9:17; 10:34; 11:16, 11:20; 12:10–11, 12:22–23, 12:38–41; 13:31; Ezra 2:66–67, 3:4, 10:6.
- Verses read in the KJV and the Greek: 11:15–17, 11:19–22, 11:27–36; 12:3–7, 12:31, 12:37–42.

## Considered and not issued

- 3:8 “they fortified Jerusalem”: the Greek verb differs, but Brenton’s own English gives a third sense and no source for the meaning of either word was opened. Held back.
- 2:1 in the Greek (“the wine was before me”, “there was not another before him”): sourced, held back to keep the batch small.
- 12:4–6 not printed in the Greek (nine names): noted inside record 9, not tagged.
- 11:3–19 beside 1 Chronicles 9 (468 and 690, 928 and 956, 1,192 and 1,760, 172 and 212): the two lists are not said to be the same count.
- 7:7 beside Ezra 2:2 (eleven names or twelve): names and spelling; the lists are not said to be complete.
- 10:32, a third of a shekel, beside the half shekel of Exodus 30:13: not said to be the same charge.
- 13:1–3 beside Deuteronomy 23:3 and Ruth: the verse quotes the law; nothing new.
- 9:7 “Ur of the Chaldees”, 9:6–31 beside the Pentateuch: not compared verse by verse.
- V: none. The breaches told (usury 5:1–13, the sabbath 13:15–22, Tobiah’s chamber 13:4–9, the marriages 13:23–28) are all rebuked in the passage.
- H beyond records 1 and 2: none found in the verses compared.
