# Zechariah second pass (workstream D11), 2026-10-20

First-time reading of the four Zechariah chapters with no published record: 2, 3, 8, 9. KJV, Brenton's Greek and the Hebrew (WLC) were fetched by script (ebible.org, bolls.life) and compared verse by verse. Brenton's Greek and the WLC number Zechariah 1:18-21 as 2:1-4, so Hebrew and Greek 2:5-17 match KJV 2:1-13 (offset of 4); each record's text says which numbers it quotes. KJV quotes in `sides` were checked as substrings of the fetched KJV. All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## Considered and ruled out
- 3:8 "men wondered at" / τερατοσκόποι (portent-watchers): mentioned inside the 3:8 summary, not a separate record.
- 2:11 "joined to the LORD" / "flee for refuge to the Lord"; 2:12 "in the holy land" / "upon the holy": clause wording, not meaning.
- 3:4 "change of raiment" / ποδήρη (a long robe), 3:7 "places to walk" / "walkers among those standing": same thing, differing vocabulary.
- 8: 8:10 hire for man and beast, 8:6, 8:19, 8:23 and the rest of the chapter: wording only. The four fasts, ten men and other numbers agree.
- 9:2, 9:3, 9:5, 9:7-9, 9:11, 9:13, 9:15, 9:17: synonyms or clause-level wording; 9:9 (the king on an ass and colt) agrees in sense in both.
- Zechariah 9:9 itself is not touched by any wording difference; no Gospel-citation record was issued here.
