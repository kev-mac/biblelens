Quoted records rest on the verses listed below. Verse numbers: KJV, WLC and Brenton agree at every verse quoted. Brenton prints the acrostic letters as part of the verses; they are not quoted. Brenton’s chapter 3 prints no verses 22–24 and 29 (labels 21, 21a–c, 25…); omitted verses are not issued (see README). Chapter 3 of Brenton also carries misplaced letter labels at 3:26–27.
