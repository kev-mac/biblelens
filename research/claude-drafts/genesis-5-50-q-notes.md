## How each was bounded

- Each record names the usual readings as readings and asserts none.
- Each states the check actually made for an answer elsewhere, with verse counts, and calls it a word search. The "us" record says plainly that no word search was possible and that only four passages were read.
- **Sons of God.** The same Hebrew words in Job 1:6 are quoted, with the limit that shared wording does not settle context. Jude 1:6, 2 Peter 2:4 and Matthew 22:30 are quoted as verses readers cite on either side; the record says none of them mentions Genesis 6.
- **Canaan.** The "younger son" wording of 9:24 against the list order Shem, Ham, Japheth is noted and expressly not pressed.
- **"Us".** 1:26 and 3:22 lie in chapters 1–4, whose batch did not propose this. They are placed here with 11:7 as one record so the question is not split in two.
- **Melchizedek.** The scope says the question is taken up in Hebrews 7:3 but not answered there. If Codex or the owner judge Hebrews 7:3 to be an answer, this record should be dropped.
- **Angel of the Lord.** Exodus 3, Judges 13 and Zechariah 1:12 are quoted to show the question recurs, not to settle it. 77 verses matched the search; the record lists which were read in full.
- **Three men.** The record says the link between the two angels of 19:1 and the three men is itself an inference from the order of the story.
- **Shiloh.** The written and read spellings in the Hebrew edition are background only. The Greek is quoted because it has no name; no G tag is proposed.

## Considered and not proposed

- **Enoch, "God took him" (5:24).** Answered elsewhere: Hebrews 11:5.
- **"His days shall be an hundred and twenty years" (6:3).** An ambiguity of meaning, and a conflict with later lifespans only on one reading.
- **Giants "also after that" and Numbers 13:33.** Rests on the spies' report, which Numbers 13:32 calls an evil report; kept as one sentence in the sons-of-God record.
- **Where the flood waters came from and went.** The passage states its own mechanism (7:11; 8:2–3); a science matter, not Q.
- **Nations "after their tongues" (10:5, 20, 31) before one language (11:1).** Arises only if chapter 10 is read as earlier in time than chapter 11.
- **Lot's wife (19:26); the peeled rods (30:37–39).** Unspecified miracle; and 31:9–12 attributes the flocks to God.
- **Who wrestled with Jacob (32:24–30).** Answered elsewhere, at least in part: Hosea 12:3–4.
- **Where Moriah was (22:2).** Answered elsewhere: 2 Chronicles 3:1.
- **What Ishmael's "mocking" was (21:9).** Addressed in Galatians 4:29.
- **Er's wickedness (38:7); why Rachel took the images (31:19); Jacob's "daughters" (37:35); Rebekah's enquiry (25:22).** Omitted detail without consequence for the story.
- **When Abraham took Keturah (25:1).** Narrative order only.
- **"The Canaanite was then in the land" (12:6); kings "before there reigned any king over Israel" (36:31); Dan (14:14).** Dating and authorship arguments; need outside sources.
- **"The Lord rained … from the Lord" (19:24).** A gap only on one reading.

Seen in passing and outside a Q-only session (not examined, not proposed): Terah's age (11:26, 11:32, 12:4; Acts 7:4); Ishmael's age when sent away (16:16; 21:5, 21:14–15); "thine only son" (22:2); Esau's wives (26:34; 28:9; 36:2–3); Benjamin among those "born in Padanaram" (35:24–26 beside 35:16–18); the Greek of 49:10; the Greek of 14:20, which names Abram as the giver of the tenth; the longer Greek of 31:13.
