# Matthew — Q notes (2026-10-14)

All drafts until Codex accepts them. 3 Q records in `matthew-1-28-q-records.json`, each with a side A on the key verse:

- `he-shall-be-called-a-nazarene-which-prophets-and-which-words-matthew-2-23` — “spoken by the prophets, He shall be called a Nazarene” — which prophets, and which words?
- `many-bodies-of-the-saints-which-slept-arose-who-were-they-and-what-became-of-them-matthew-27-52` — “many bodies of the saints which slept arose” — who were they, and what became of them?
- `we-have-seen-his-star-in-the-east-what-was-the-star-matthew-2-2` — “we have seen his star in the east” — what was the star?

Each follows the Q contract: the passage leaves the identity open, readings are named only as readings, and none is chosen. Every absence claim is a whole-word search of the packaged KJV and says so (“Nazarene” in Genesis–Malachi; “slept”, “graves” and “star” in Matthew–John). None repeats an existing Q (checked against `data/evidence.json` and the drafts).

Considered and left out: Matthew 12:31–32 (the unforgivable sin: the verse states its condition); 16:28 and 24:34 (timing sayings: the question depends on an interpretation); 27:9 (already issued untagged); 2:16 (the number of children: ordinary omitted detail); 1:25 (a question about Mary after the birth: it depends on a reading of “till”).
