## Editions and verses compared

- Greek New Testament text is the Textus Receptus (TR) and Tischendorf eighth edition (TISCH) as served by bolls.life books 48–53 (`https://bolls.life/get-text/TR/{book}/{chapter}/`, `…/TISCH/{book}/{chapter}/`). TR is served without accents; TISCH is quoted. All 28 chapters were fetched and every verse where the TR and TISCH word lists differ was read in a full scan (about 300 lines); only differences of meaning were issued.
- Verses compared in detail (KJV, TR and TISCH side by side): Galatians 1:15, 2:6, 3:1, 3:17, 3:19–20, 4:6–7, 4:15, 4:17–18, 4:26, 4:28, 4:31, 5:4, 5:19, 5:21, 5:24, 6:2, 6:5, 6:15, 6:17; Ephesians 1:10–11, 1:18, 3:1, 3:6, 3:9, 3:14, 4:6, 4:9, 4:17, 5:2, 5:9, 5:21–22, 5:30–31, 6:2–3, 6:10, 6:12, 6:16, 6:24; Philippians 1:14, 1:16–17, 1:25, 1:27–28, 2:5, 2:7–8, 2:30, 3:3, 3:16, 4:13, 4:23; Colossians 1:2, 1:4, 1:6, 1:12, 1:14, 2:2, 2:11, 2:13, 2:18, 3:4, 3:6, 3:15–16, 3:22, 4:8, 4:12–13, 4:16; 1 Thessalonians 1:1, 2:7, 2:12, 2:19, 3:2, 3:11, 4:8, 4:13, 5:27; 2 Thessalonians 1:8, 1:10, 2:2–8, 2:12, 3:6, 3:12. The rest of the chapters were scanned by the word-list comparison only and are not claimed to be read in full.
- **Verse numbering:** the TISCH page for Philippians 2 prints “and being found in fashion as a man” at the end of 2:7, the KJV and TR have it at the start of 2:8; no record rests on those verses. The pages do not carry the subscriptions (“written from Rome”) that the KJV prints after Galatians 6:18, Ephesians 6:24, Philippians 4:23, Colossians 4:18 and 1 and 2 Thessalonians; the subscriptions are not issued. At Philippians 1:16–17 the TISCH page prints the two clauses in the other order.
- **KJV that follows Tischendorf, not the TR as served:** Galatians 4:15 (“Where is then the blessedness”: TR prints “What was then”) and Galatians 4:17 (“exclude you”: TR prints “us”). Not issued.
