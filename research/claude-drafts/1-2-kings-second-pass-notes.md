# 1 and 2 Kings second pass (workstream D9), 2026-10-20

First-time reading of the 14 chapters with no published record: 1 Kings 1, 2, 3, 12, 17, 21 and 2 Kings 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11, 19, 21. KJV and Brenton's English were screened verse by verse (low word overlap or length mismatch), then the Hebrew (WLC) and Brenton's Greek were fetched by script (bolls.life, ebible.org) and compared at each flagged verse. KJV quotes in `text` and `sides` were checked against packaged `data/chapters.json` by script; Hebrew and Greek are pulled whole from the fetched pages. All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## Issued (12 records, `1-2-kings-second-pass-records.json`), status Context, T, witness Septuagint

1 Kings: 2:29 Greek prints a Solomon-Joab exchange before Benaiah is sent; 11:26 and 12:24b Jeroboam's mother a widow (Hebrew and Greek 11:26) but "a harlot" in Greek 12:24b; 17:21 "stretched" / "breathed on"; 17:22 "the Lord heard... revived" / "the child cried out"; 21:1 palace / threshing-floor; 21:10 and 21:13 "blaspheme" / "bless"; 21:16 Greek adds Ahab's torn clothes and sackcloth on hearing of Naboth's death.
2 Kings: 4:35 "the child sneezed" / Elisha bent over the child; 4:42 "full ears of corn" / "fig cakes"; 11:6 "gate of Sur" / "gate of the ways"; 19:25 question "heard long ago" / "I formed it, I gathered it"; 21:6 "his son" / "his sons".

Ledger: 8 chapters records-draft (1 Kings 2, 12, 17, 21; 2 Kings 4, 11, 19, 21); 6 reviewed-none (1 Kings 1, 3; 2 Kings 5, 6, 7, 10). KJV-level read against Brenton's English plus Hebrew/Greek at flagged verses; not a full Hebrew read.

## Considered and ruled out
- Brenton's Greek lettered verses at 1 Kings 2:35a-o and 2:46a-l: mostly repeat Solomon material the KJV has in chapters 3-5, 9-10 (burden-bearers, Pharaoh's daughter, daily provision, officers, horses); under the additions ruling these state nothing new. The Shimei "three years" (2:38) is stated by the KJV itself at 2:39.
- 1 Kings 12:24a: Rehoboam sixteen years old and reigns twelve years against KJV 14:21 (41 and 17); already published as `rehoboam-forty-one-and-seventeen-or-sixteen-and-twelve`.
- 1 Kings 12:24b-z other items (Shishak giving Jeroboam Ano, Ahijah's age, Jeroboam's child's illness told before the rebellion, the garment of twelve pieces): a different order and extra details against 11:19-20, 11:29-31 and 14:1-18. Real, but held for a lower-value round to keep the batch small; sources are in the fetched pages if Codex wants a second round.
- 1 Kings 12:3 (Jeroboam not named among those who speak to Rehoboam), 12:18, 12:32 (Greek stops after "like the feast in Judah"): clause-level omissions, held.
- 1 Kings 1:40 pipes / dancing in choirs, 1:9 Zoheleth / Zoelethi, 2:1-7, 3:5 (Gibeon not printed), 3:15 (altar before the ark), 17:9, 17:15 ("many days" not printed): wording, transliteration or clause omission.
- 1 Kings 21:2-29 beyond the issued verses: 21:19 (Greek adds harlots washing in Ahab's blood) sits beside KJV 22:38 and is better treated with that chapter; 21:27, 21:29 wording only.
- Brenton's chapters 20 and 21 are swapped against the Hebrew in 1 Kings. The Naboth records cite Brenton chapter 20 (`1KI20.htm`) and say so.
- 2 Kings 4:24, 4:28, 4:44; 5:1, 5:11, 5:17-19, 5:25-26 (Greek states "now thou hast received silver" where the KJV asks "Is it a time to receive money"): wording or sentence form. 6:6 (stick broken off / cut down), 6:13 (Dothaim): wording or spelling. 7:13: wording.
- 2 Kings 10:21 (Greek adds Jehu's call "whosoever shall be missing, shall not live"): the KJV has the same threat at 10:19. 10:26 images / pillar: plural against singular noun, obscure.
- 2 Kings 11:5: Greek omits most of the KJV's sabbath arrangement; sentence-level difference, held. 11:13: wording.
- 2 Kings 19:13 (Ivah / Aba), 19:11, 19:15, 19:26, 19:30, 19:32; 21:11: transliteration or wording.

## For Codex
- 17:21 and 4:35: each rests on a Hebrew verb the KJV renders with a margin alternative or a rare form; each scope says it does not decide the meaning.
- 21:10 and 21:13: the Hebrew verb is the ordinary verb for "bless"; the record says what each text prints and does not claim why.
- 11:26 and 12:24b: a widow can also have been a harlot; the reasoning says so and the record sets out what each passage prints.
- 21:16 beside 21:27: different moments; scope says both texts print 21:27.
- 11:6 scope names 2 Chronicles 23:5 ("gate of the foundation" in the KJV) only as a parallel treated elsewhere.
