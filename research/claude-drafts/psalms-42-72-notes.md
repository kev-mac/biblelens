# Psalms 42–72 (Book II) — notes

Companion to `psalms-42-72-records.json` (20 records: 20 G, no H, no V, no untagged; all carry `sides`, three with a side B on a New Testament verse). All are drafts until Codex accepts them. Written 2026-10-13. Q records are in `psalms-42-72-q-records.json` with `psalms-42-72-q-notes.md`.

## What was compared

- Every KJV verse of Psalms 42–72 (31 psalms, 465 verses) was set against Brenton’s English of the Greek verse at the matching number by script; each verse with low word overlap, and each verse with a different number word or proper name, was then read in the KJV, the Hebrew (WLC) and Brenton’s Greek. About 90 verses were read in all three.
- Hebrew and Greek were read in full only at the verses listed in the records and in “Considered” below. The psalms were not read in full in the Hebrew.
- Published and drafted records were checked before writing and again just before the files were written: none lists a verse of Psalms 42–72 in its `refs`. One published record, on Psalm 14:3, mentions Psalm 53 in its text. Psalm 14 was not compared with Psalm 53.

## Verse numbers

Greek psalm = KJV psalm − 1 for the whole part (KJV 42 is `grcbrent/PSA041.htm`). Counts, from the fetched pages:

- Hebrew and Greek run **one ahead** of the KJV (title counted as verse 1) in 42, 44–49, 53, 55–59, 61–65, 67–70.
- **Two ahead** in 51, 52, 54 and 60 (title of two verses).
- **No difference** in 43, 50, 66, 71 and 72 (the title is printed inside verse 1).
- Hebrew and Greek offsets agree in every psalm.
- Brenton’s Greek prints no lettered verses in this part. Brenton’s Greek 59:9–10: the sentence “Judas is my king” ends 59:9 in the Greek text and begins 59:10 in Brenton’s English.
- `refs` and `sides` take KJV numbers; each quotation is given at its own Hebrew or Greek number, and each record says so.

## Points for Codex

- **New Testament side B in three records** (51:4, 68:18, 69:22–23): the side B verse words the matter differently from side A. Wait for your view before more are issued.
- 68:18: the Greek psalm, like the KJV psalm and the Hebrew, has “received”; Ephesians has “gave”. The record is a three-way wording comparison and says it does not decide how Paul used the psalm.
- 50:11: the Hebrew of the last clause (the word the KJV prints “wild beasts”) is not quoted, because the digital text served prints it with a letter missing; the clause is quoted from the KJV and the Greek only.
- Four short English quotations from Brenton’s own English pages stand in scopes with their addresses (56:8, 58:8, 63:8, 67:18, 70:15), as in the Job batch.
- Overlap check printed two overlaps, both context verses in the Q record on Psalm 69:28 (Exodus 32:32–33, published `exodus-intercession-relenting`); no G overlaps.

## Considered and not issued

- **No H.** Candidates where the KJV’s English differs from the Hebrew but a meaning difference would need a dictionary source were left out: 72:9 (the Hebrew word the KJV prints “they that dwell in the wilderness”), 60:8 “washpot”, 45:13 “within”. They stand as G only. Do not tag H on a name or a word for the same reason; the earlier ruling (wording-only differences) applies.
- **No V.** Psalms of lament and praise do not tell an act of a person under the Law.
- 60:6 (Shechem and Succoth against Sicima and “valley of tents”): a name against a common noun; not issued.
- 68:12 (“Kings of armies did flee apace”): the Greek differs, but its sentence is too obscure to state a clean difference; 68:15 (“hill of Bashan” against “a rich mountain”); 68:27 and 68:30.
- 69:31 (“ox or bullock” against “young calf”), 69:20, 69:32, 70:1 (“Make haste” against “Draw nigh”), 65:3, 65:10, 55:2, 55:5, 55:7–8, 55:11, 55:20–21 (Greek 54:21 differs markedly: not issued to keep the batch small), 59:3, 59:9–10, 61:3, 62:2, 62:4, 62:9, 63:6, 64:2–3, 56:1–2, 57:4, 44:12, 45:9, 46:4, 49:1, 49:13, 53:5 (the Greek of the page prints no clause for “There were they in great fear, where no fear was” at this number; not examined further).
- 69:9 with John 2:17 and Romans 15:3, 45:6–7 with Hebrews 1:8–9, 44:22 with Romans 8:36: the KJV psalm, the Greek psalm and the New Testament words agree; no difference. 69:25 with Acts 1:20 (“their” against “his”): Greek psalm and KJV psalm agree against Acts; not a Greek–KJV difference; not issued.
- Psalm 53 against Psalm 14: not compared; the Romans 3 additions at 14:3 are already issued in `there-is-none-that-doeth-good-greek-adds-their-throat-is-an-open-sepulchre`.
- Titles are not in the packaged KJV, so nothing in a title was quoted or given a side.
- The number-word script showed the “thousand” and “twenty thousand” differences (50:10, 68:17) and “twice” against “two” (62:11); the name script showed the Esebon, Arabia, Saba, Idumea and Ethiopians differences. Idumea for Edom (60:8–9) was left out as a spelling difference.
