# Jeremiah second pass (workstream D), 2026-10-20

First-time reading of the 24 Jeremiah chapters that had no published record: 4, 5, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 21, 24, 27, 30, 33, 35, 37, 38, 39, 42, 44, 45, 47, 49. The KJV and Brenton's English were read side by side for every chapter. Hebrew (WLC) and Brenton's Greek were fetched by script and compared at the verses that raised a question. Brenton's Greek orders Jeremiah 25-52 differently from the Hebrew, so those chapters were matched by content (Greek pages 34, 42, 44, 45, 46, 49, 29, 30, 37, 40, 51). All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## Considered and ruled out
- 4:7, 4:30, 5:6, 5:16, 5:31, 5:28: Greek wording differs but not plainly in meaning (or an unclear Hebrew word); held.
- 8:11-12 (omitted by Brenton) repeat 6:14-15, which already carries the same words in the KJV; held. 11:7 (omitted) held as a minor omission.
- 9:15 "wormwood" against "trouble", 15:10 "lent on usury" against "helped", 17:16 "pastor" against "weary", 17:17 "terror" against "stranger", 24:1 extra Greek classes of captives: real differences but lower priority than those issued; candidates for a later session.
- 35:3 Jaazaniah against Jechonias, 37:13 Irijah against Saruia, 38:14 "third entry" against "house of Aselisel", 38:6 "son of Hammelech" against "the king's son", 49:13 and 49:22 Bozrah: candidates for a later session.
- 27:6 "Nebuchadnezzar ... my servant" (Greek has no servant) repeats the published 43:10 record; not duplicated.
- Jeremiah 9 verse labels on Brenton's page run one lower than the KJV from 9:2 to 9:26 (the weeping verse of KJV 9:1 stands at the end of Brenton's chapter 8); compared by content.
- Chapters 44 and 45: the Greek follows the Hebrew closely; nothing qualified beyond transliterated names.
