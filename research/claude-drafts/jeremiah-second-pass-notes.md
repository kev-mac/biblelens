- Jeremiah 39:4-13 (Zedekiah's flight, blinding, the princes' names): Brenton prints 39:1-3 and 39:14-18 only. A real Greek omission, but the events are stated at 2 Kings 25 and Jeremiah 52; held under the additions/omissions ruling.

- Jeremiah 49:7-22 beside Obadiah 1-9: only the order of the grape-gatherers and thieves images differs; Obadiah not opened here. Not issued.
- Jeremiah 24:8 (the Egypt remnant already in Zedekiah's time) and 21:1 (Pashur son of Melchiah, 38:1 Pashur son of Malchiah): same name pairs, no conflict.
- Jeremiah 37:13 "Irijah" against Brenton's "Sarouia": a transliteration difference.
- Jeremiah 38:7 (Ebedmelech "one of the eunuchs"; Greek has "in the king's house"): small; not issued.
- Jeremiah 45 and 47: nothing beyond what is published; Brenton for 45 and 47 not read in full.
