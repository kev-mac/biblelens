# Revelation — Q notes (2026-10-14)

All drafts until Codex accepts them. 3 Q records in `revelation-1-22-q-records.json`, each with a side A on the key verse (the 144,000 record also has a side B on 7:9):

- `my-two-witnesses-who-are-they-and-where-and-when-revelation-11-3-12` — who the two witnesses are (Revelation 11:3–12; Zechariah 4:14 as the verse the two olive trees recall).
- `the-hundred-and-forty-and-four-thousand-sealed-who-are-they-revelation-7-4-14-1` — who the sealed are and whether they are the multitude of 7:9 and the company of 14:1–4.
- `the-number-of-the-beast-whose-name-does-it-count-revelation-13-17-18` — the name behind the number (13:17–18).

Each follows the Q contract: the passage leaves the point open, readings are named only as readings that have been held, and none is chosen. The Greek quoted is Tischendorf (eighth edition) as served by bolls.life, checked against the Textus Receptus text served there; both print the same words in every Q quotation.

For Codex: the two-witnesses record cites Zechariah 4:14, which the published Q `these-are-the-two-anointed-ones-who-are-the-two-anointed-ones-zechariah-4-14` also lists; the questions differ (that one asks who the anointed ones are in Zechariah). Fold or keep separate as you judge.

Considered and left out: the seven thunders (10:3–4: the verse says the content was sealed, a stated fact not an open question); the woman and the man child (12:1–5: symbols the chapter does not explain, which would need a reading chosen); Michael and the war in heaven (12:7, covered in part by the published Q on Satan before the Lord); the thousand years (20:2–7: a duration stated; its relation to time is a theological premise); Gog and Magog (20:8, a name given; the link to Ezekiel 38 is a comparison, not a gap); the “first resurrection” (20:5–6); the little book (10:8–11); “the book of life” (covered by the published Q on Psalm 69:28, which lists Revelation 20:12); the new heaven and earth (21:1: stated, not left open).
