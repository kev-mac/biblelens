## Checks made, and their limits

- Record 1: the packaged KJV was searched for `Pharaoh` joined to a further name, and for Shishak, "So king" and Tirhakah. The named kings found are in Kings, Chronicles, Isaiah and Jeremiah. A word search only.
- Record 2: every verse of Genesis and Exodus 1–19 containing "priest" or "priests" was read (Genesis 14:18, 41:45, 41:50, 46:20, 47:22, 47:26; Exodus 2:16, 3:1, 18:1, 19:6, 19:22, 19:24). A word search of those chapters only. Leviticus and Numbers were not searched for a backward reference.
- Record 3: every verse of the packaged KJV containing "Urim" or "Thummim" was read: seven verses, all quoted. Passages about enquiring of the Lord that use neither word (for example with the ephod) were not searched.

## Points for Codex

1. **Record 2 and the published `exodus-priesthood-access`.** That record's scope says the priests who appear before the later appointment texts need separate research. Record 2 is that question, issued as a Q. It does not repeat the published record's finding about access to the mountain.
2. **Record 3 overlaps on verse only** with `exodus-priestly-tribal-memorial` (28:30), `numbers-joshua-priestly-counsel` (Numbers 27:21), `deuteronomy-levi-blessing-and-rebuke` (Deuteronomy 33:8) and `saul-enquired-of-the-lord-or-enquired-not` (1 Samuel 28:6). None of them asks what the Urim and Thummim were.
3. **Record 1** quotes 1 Kings 6:1 and 2 Kings 23:29 as context; the published records at those verses concern other findings.
4. **No Q was issued on Exodus 4:24–26** (whom the Lord sought to kill, and why). The published `exodus-zipporah` already says the account does not explain the offence or name the referent of every pronoun. A second record would repeat it. If the Q tag is wanted on that passage, it belongs on the published record, which is Codex's and the owner's to decide. The Greek differences in those verses are issued as a G record in the main batch.

## Considered and not issued

- By what power the magicians matched the signs (7:11, 7:22, 8:7): the text names "enchantments"; not a gap in the story's own terms.
- What manna was (16:14–31): described in the passage.
- The tent Moses pitched before the tabernacle was made (33:7): issued as a G record instead, since Brenton's Greek states whose tent it was.
- Where Sinai or "the mountain of God" was: not consequential to the reading of the passage.
- Moses' older brother and sister beside the wording of 2:1–2: ordinary narrative brevity.
