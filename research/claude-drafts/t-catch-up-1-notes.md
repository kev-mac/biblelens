## Re-judged under T (entries of `owner-questions.md` decided “no H” before the switch)

Issued: Psalms 22:16, 8:5, 80:15, 84:6, Job 1:11, 2:5, 3:8 (above).

Not issued, under the same T definition:

- **Psalms 95:8 “temptation” (Hebrew `מַסָּה`).** The Hebrew word is the one the KJV prints as the name “Massah” in some verses and renders as a noun in others; the record would have to say which it is, and a transliteration does not settle that. The KJV’s “temptation” is a noun for that word, not a plainly different word. The Greek difference stays with the published G record (witness Septuagint).
- **Psalms 100:3 “and not we ourselves”.** The Hebrew edition prints a written form and a read form (`וְלֹא כ` and `וְלֹו ק`). The KJV renders the written form, so no word of the KJV differs from the text it follows. Written/read forms are background only.
- **Psalms 106:30 “executed judgment”.** The Hebrew verb printed is one whose range of senses the packaged KJV itself spans; showing “executed judgment” to be a plainly different word would need a dictionary. The Greek “made atonement” stays with the published G record.
- **Psalms 74:8 “synagogues” and 75:6 “south”.** Left out as the catch-up list says: they depend on how the Hebrew word is understood.
- **2 Chronicles 34:14 “a book”.** The difference is an indefinite English article against a definite Hebrew phrase. That is a difference of wording, not a plainly different word, name or meaning (as at Deuteronomy 27:26). Left out.
- **Psalms 82:1, 82:6, 97:7, 138:1.** The KJV renders the same Hebrew noun “gods” or “God” in these verses, so no KJV word differs from it.

Each of the affected entries in `owner-questions.md` is marked “re-judged under T”.
