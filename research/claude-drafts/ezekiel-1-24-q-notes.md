# Ezekiel 1–24 — Q notes (2026-10-13)

Drafts until Codex accepts them. File: `ezekiel-1-24-q-records.json` (3 Q records). Each has `mode: "questions"`, `tags: ["Q"]`, `status: "Context"`, a question-shaped title, KJV quotations asserted by script, and a side A.

## Q records

1. `in-the-thirtieth-year-whose-thirtieth-year-ezekiel-1-1` — Ezekiel 1:1 gives “the thirtieth year” with no king, event or era; 1:2 gives a second date by Jehoiachin’s captivity. The record says what the two verses leave open and names three readings without choosing.
2. `three-hundred-and-ninety-days-each-day-for-a-year-which-years-ezekiel-4-5` — 4:4–6 give 390 and 40 “years of their iniquity” with no starting point. A separate T record (`three-hundred-and-ninety-days-or-a-hundred-and-ninety-days-ezekiel-4-5-greek`) compares the Greek number; this Q rests on the KJV only.
3. `i-the-lord-have-deceived-that-prophet-how-ezekiel-14-9` — 14:9 says the Lord has deceived the prophet and will destroy him; the verses do not say by what means. Named readings only.

## Considered and not issued as Q

- Ezekiel 20:25–26 (“statutes that were not good”): the clash with 20:11 is issued as an untagged record instead, and the question of which statutes is inside its scope. A Q on the same verses would repeat it.
- Ezekiel 21:27 (“until he come whose right it is”): already the Shiloh Q (`until-shiloh-come-who-or-what-is-shiloh`, Genesis 49:10 with Ezekiel 21:27). Not re-issued.
- Ezekiel 1:5–14 (what the living creatures are): answered in the book (10:15, 10:20, “the cherubims”).
- Ezekiel 14:14, 14:20 (Noah, Daniel and Job): the three are named and are known from other books; no gap that the text itself leaves.
- Ezekiel 16:3 (“thy father was an Amorite, and thy mother an Hittite”): the sentence is an image addressed to Jerusalem; what it says of her origin is stated by the image itself.
- Ezekiel 24:16–18 (the death of the prophet’s wife): the cause of her death is the Lord’s stroke (24:16); not an unstated cause.
- Ezekiel 8:14 (Tammuz): a named practice; no unstated identity the passage needs.
