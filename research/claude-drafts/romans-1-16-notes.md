# Romans — notes (2026-10-14)

Unit: Romans 1–16 (16 chapters, 433 verses). **All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.**

Counts: 44 records in `romans-1-16-records.json`: 38 T (all witness "Greek New Testament") and 6 untagged; no V; every record carries `sides`. 3 Q in `romans-1-16-q-records.json` (separate notes file).

## Editions and numbering

- Greek text is the Textus Receptus (TR) and Tischendorf eighth edition (TISCH) as served by bolls.life book 45 (`https://bolls.life/get-text/TR/45/{chapter}/`, `…/TISCH/45/{chapter}/`). Verse numbers agree with the KJV. The bolls.life SBLGNT text carries morphology codes and is not quoted.
- The KJV follows the TR in the edition records, so each compares the KJV with Tischendorf’s text; each says the TR text served agrees with the KJV, that the record does not say which reading is original, and that two digital editions are not a survey of manuscripts.
- Every quotation was asserted by script against the packaged KJV and the fetched pages; sides were validated against the packaged KJV; all 55 source addresses answered.
