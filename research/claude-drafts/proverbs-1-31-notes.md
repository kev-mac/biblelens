# Proverbs 1–31 — notes (2026-10-13)

Batch: `proverbs-1-31-records.json` (20 T records, all witness “Septuagint”; no V; no untagged; all carry `sides`, four with a side B on a New Testament verse). The Q batch is `proverbs-1-31-q-records.json` with `proverbs-1-31-q-notes.md`. All are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## What was compared

- Every one of the 915 KJV verses of chapters 1–31 was scanned by script against Brenton’s English of the whole book (best word-overlap verse anywhere in the book, so that reordered verses are caught), and the verses with low overlap were read in the KJV, the Leningrad text and Brenton’s Greek. The chapters were not read in full in Hebrew and Greek; verses that overlap well were not read.
- Brenton’s Greek is much freer than the Hebrew throughout the book, so many verses differ in wording. Only those where a plainly different word or meaning could be stated from the printed words alone were issued.
- Hebrew and KJV verse numbers agree in every verse quoted. Brenton’s Greek page for **chapter 30 does not exist** (`PRO30.htm` answers 404): the Hebrew chapter 30 and 31:1–9 are printed inside Brenton’s chapter 24 (24:22f–24:62). Records on 30:1–4 and 31:1 cite the chapter 24 page and say so. Greek chapter 31 prints verses 10–31 only. The Greek order of chapters 16 and 20 differs from the Hebrew (for example KJV 16:6 is Greek 15:27a; KJV 20:20–22 are Greek 20:9a–c).

## Records

1. `the-fear-of-the-lord-is-the-beginning-of-knowledge-or-the-beginning-of-wisdom-greek` (1:7): “knowledge” against a word Brenton’s English renders “wisdom”, and two added clauses.
2. `even-as-a-father-the-son-in-whom-he-delighteth-or-scourges-every-son-whom-he-receives-greek` (3:12, side B Hebrews 12:6): “delighteth” against “receives”, and the second act of scourging.
3. `he-scorneth-the-scorners-or-the-lord-resists-the-proud-greek` (3:34, side B James 4:6).
4. `the-lord-possessed-me-in-the-beginning-of-his-way-or-the-lord-made-me-the-beginning-of-his-ways-greek` (8:22): “possessed” against “made”.
5. `he-that-winneth-souls-is-wise-or-the-souls-of-transgressors-are-cut-off-before-their-time-greek` (11:30).
6. `the-righteous-shall-be-recompensed-in-the-earth-or-if-the-righteous-scarcely-be-saved-greek` (11:31, side B 1 Peter 4:18; TR and TISCH agree there).
7. `the-ant-provideth-her-meat-in-the-summer-or-go-to-the-bee-greek` (6:8): Greek verses 6:8a–c add the bee; the KJV book of Proverbs has no “bee” (asserted by script).
8. `he-that-soweth-iniquity-shall-reap-vanity-or-god-blesses-a-cheerful-and-liberal-man-greek` (22:8, side B 2 Corinthians 9:7): a Greek plus. KJV 22:9 speaks of blessing and giving; the scope limits the difference to God’s love or blessing of a cheerful giver.
9. `train-up-a-child-in-the-way-he-should-go-brentons-greek-prints-no-verse-22-6` (22:6): **Brenton’s Greek prints no verse 22:6** and no verse of its English contains the instruction (asserted by script). The scope says this is one printed edition.
10. `wisdom-is-the-principal-thing-therefore-get-wisdom-brentons-greek-prints-no-verse-4-7` (4:7): same shape, same limits.
11. `put-a-knife-to-thy-throat-or-apply-thine-hand-knowing-that-it-behoves-thee-to-prepare-such-meats-greek` (23:2).
12. `where-there-is-no-vision-the-people-perish-or-there-shall-be-no-interpreter-to-a-sinful-nation-greek` (29:18).
13. `she-is-not-afraid-of-the-snow-clothed-with-scarlet-or-her-husband-is-not-anxious-about-those-at-home-greek` (31:21): no snow and no scarlet in Brenton’s 31:21 (asserted by script).
14. `the-words-of-agur-the-son-of-jakeh-unto-ithiel-and-ucal-or-these-things-says-the-man-to-them-that-trust-in-god-greek` (30:1, Greek 24:22f): none of the four names occurs anywhere in Brenton’s English of Proverbs (asserted by script). The scope does not say that the Hebrew words are or are not names.
15. `i-neither-learned-wisdom-or-god-has-taught-me-wisdom-greek` (30:3, Greek 24:22h): two negations against two affirmations.
16. `the-words-of-king-lemuel-or-my-words-have-been-spoken-by-god-the-oracular-answer-of-a-king-greek` (31:1, Greek 24:54): no “Lemuel” in Brenton’s English (asserted by script).
17. `the-great-god-that-formed-all-things-rewardeth-the-fool-or-all-the-flesh-of-fools-endures-much-hardship-greek` (26:10).
18. `whoso-findeth-a-wife-findeth-a-good-thing-or-he-that-puts-away-a-good-wife-puts-away-a-good-thing-greek` (18:22, Greek 18:22a, printed in square brackets by Brenton’s English; the KJV chapter has no “put away” and no “adulter”, asserted by script).
19. `the-words-of-a-talebearer-are-as-wounds-or-fear-casts-down-the-slothful-greek` (18:8; 26:22 is context only: the Hebrew of 26:22 has the same consonants as 18:8, asserted by script, and Brenton’s Greek words it differently).
20. `a-man-that-doeth-violence-to-the-blood-of-any-person-shall-flee-or-he-that-becomes-surety-for-a-man-charged-with-murder-greek` (28:17).

## For Codex

- **Records 9 and 10 rest on a verse the Greek page does not print.** The Nehemiah 7:68 record (a verse the Hebrew edition lacks) was accepted; these are the Greek-side equivalent. They may be held if an omitted verse is not a difference of meaning for T. Logged in `owner-questions.md`.
- Records 2, 3, 6 and 8 carry a side B on a New Testament verse; the Greek New Testament words were checked in TISCH and TR at bolls.life. No claim is made about which text the New Testament writer followed.
- Records 14 and 16 compare names in the KJV with Greek wording that prints no name. They do not claim the Hebrew words are or are not names.
- Records 4 and 12 sit beside well-known debates; the scopes state only what each text prints and do not decide the sense.

## Not issued (reasons)

- Many verses where the Greek is a free paraphrase with the same sense (for example 3:9, 3:13, 8:11, 12:10, 14:21, 15:17).
- Animal and plant names (30:26 “conies”, 30:28 “spider”, 30:31 “greyhound”, 7:17 “myrrh, aloes”): naming differences without a lexical source; left out under the rule not to identify across texts.
- 10:10 (“a prating fool shall fall” against “he that reproves boldly is a peacemaker”), 11:16, 13:7, 14:10, 14:34, 25:11 (“pictures of silver” against “a necklace of sardius”), 31:3 (“strength” against “wealth”): differences present but the Hebrew word’s range of senses was judged to leave the KJV’s choice within range, or the difference is lesser than the issued ones. Held back with quotes sourced for a later pass if wanted.
- Further verses the Greek page does not print or prints elsewhere (1:16, 8:33, 13:6, 15:31, 16:1, 16:3, 16:4, 16:6, 18:23–24, 19:1–2, 20:14–19, 21:5): not issued, to keep the batch small and to wait for Codex’s view on records 9 and 10. The Greek of chapters 16 and 20 prints several of these at other numbers.
- Cross-book conflicts: none issued. Romans 12:20 with Proverbs 25:21–22 and 1 Peter 4:8 with Proverbs 10:12 were not compared with the Greek NT wording (not examined).
- V: none. The book states no act told of a person under the law; sayings are not acts.
- Untagged conflicts: 26:4 and 26:5 is already published (`exp-late-19-26-4`); no other direct conflict found.
