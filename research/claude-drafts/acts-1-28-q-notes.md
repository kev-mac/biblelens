# Acts 1–28 — Q notes (2026-10-14)

`acts-1-28-q-records.json`: 3 Q records, all drafts until Codex accepts them. Every KJV quotation and every Greek quotation was asserted by script against the packaged KJV and the fetched Tischendorf (TISCH) page; the Greek is Tischendorf’s eighth edition as served by bolls.life, and where it matters the Textus Receptus text served was compared.

## Records

1. `why-had-the-holy-ghost-not-yet-fallen-on-the-baptized-samaritans-acts-8-16` — Acts 8:12 says the Samaritans believed and were baptized; 8:16 says the Spirit had “as yet … fallen upon none of them”; 8:17 has them receive him when the apostles laid hands on them. The text gives no reason for the interval. Readings are named as possibilities only. Verses quoted and read: 8:12–17.
2. `forbidden-of-the-holy-ghost-to-preach-in-asia-how-acts-16-6-16-7` — the prohibition is attributed to the Holy Ghost and “the Spirit”, and no means is given; the next direction (16:9) is a vision. Verses quoted and read: 16:6–9.
3. `having-shorn-his-head-in-cenchrea-for-he-had-a-vow-whose-vow-acts-18-18` — the verse does not say whether “he” is Paul or Aquila, or what the vow was. Acts 21:23 is quoted only as another place the book mentions a vow. Verses quoted and read: 18:18; 21:23.

## Considered and left out

- **The “other tongues” of Pentecost (2:4–11):** the passage says what the hearers heard (2:6, 2:8); not a gap.
- **Ananias and Sapphira (5:1–11):** the cause of death is not stated, but the passage names the offence and Peter’s words (5:3–9); not a material gap.
- **Philip carried away (8:39):** a miracle with no mechanism; an unspecified miracle is not tested here, and the passage is narrative brevity.
- **“It is his angel” (12:15):** who or what is meant is not stated; the question is a matter of what the speakers believed, which the passage does not say, so it was left out as a claim about the speakers’ minds.
- **Why these four things (15:20, 29):** answered in part by 15:21.
- **The twelve disciples at Ephesus (19:1–7):** who they were is not stated and the question is real, but the passage answers what matters to the account (they knew only John’s baptism, 19:3); the baptism question is treated by the Acts 8:16 record.
- **What became of Paul (28:30–31):** the book ends without an outcome; narrative brevity.
- **Where Paul’s trial would have taken place (25:11–12, 26:32):** answered by 25:12 and 26:32.

The chapters were not read in full for Q; the verses listed above are the ones quoted and read by script. The left-out items were judged from the verses named in each line, not from a full read of the book.
