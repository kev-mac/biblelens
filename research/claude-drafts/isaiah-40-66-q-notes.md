# Isaiah 40–66 — Q notes (2026-10-13)

Three Q records, all drafts until Codex accepts them, all `mode: "questions"`, `tags: ["Q"]`, `status: "Context"`, each with a side A (the first also has a side B at 49:5). All wording is KJV; no Hebrew or Greek is relied on, and no word search is claimed.

1. `thou-art-my-servant-o-israel-and-bring-jacob-again-who-is-the-servant-of-isaiah-49-3-6` — 49:3–6. The servant is called "Israel" (49:3) and is sent to bring Jacob again (49:5, 49:6). The verses quoted do not say whether the servant is the nation, a part of it or one person. Other servant passages (42:1, 52:13, 53) are not examined.
2. `who-is-this-that-cometh-from-edom-with-dyed-garments-from-bozrah-isaiah-63-1` — 63:1–3. The speaker is described, not named.
3. `who-hath-believed-our-report-whose-voice-says-we-in-isaiah-53-1` — 53:1 with 52:13, 52:15, 53:4. Who speaks as "we" is not stated in the verses quoted. The T record on 53:4 and 53:10 compares wording only; the Q does not examine how another book uses the chapter.

## Considered and not issued

- 66:19 (who "those that escape" are, and what the "sign" is): the sign and the escaped are partly set by the verse's own list of nations; left out as ordinary omitted detail.
- 41:2 and 41:25 (the "righteous man from the east", "one from the north"): 45:1 names Cyrus in the same book, and a built-in study already covers 45:1; the gap is answered elsewhere in the book.
- 40:3 (whose voice cries): a voice is not a person the passage sets up as an identity question.
- 7:14-style questions are in the Isaiah 1–27 batch; none repeated.
