# Jeremiah 1–29 — notes (2026-10-13)

All records are drafts until Codex accepts them. 18 records, all tag T: 17 with witness "Septuagint" (Jeremiah 1–29) and 1 with witness "Hebrew" (the deferred Immanuel record, Isaiah 8:8 and 8:10). No V, no untagged conflict. Every record carries `sides`; none has a side B on another book except the Immanuel record (Isaiah 8:10).

## Verse numbers

- KJV and Hebrew numbers agree throughout 1–29 at every verse quoted.
- **Brenton’s Greek follows the Septuagint order.** KJV 25:1–13 = Greek 25:1–13; KJV 25:15–38 = Greek 32:15–38; KJV 26 = Greek 33, KJV 27 = Greek 34, KJV 28 = Greek 35, KJV 29 = Greek 36. Verse numbers inside those chapters match the KJV at the verses quoted (26:7, 28:1, 29:2, 29:11, 29:24 = 33:7, 35:1, 36:2, 36:11, 36:24). Brenton’s pages are JER01–JER36 here (bolls.life book 24, packaged KJV index 23). Brenton’s chapter 34 (KJV 27) has no verses 1, 7, 13 or 17 (printed labels), so the numbers there do not line up one for one.
- Several chapters have written/read (ketiv/qere) marks in the Leningrad text (2:16, 19:2); the records quote only the words outside the marks, or say that a marked word is not quoted.

## Method and what was compared

- Every KJV verse of 1–29 was scored by word overlap against the same-numbered verse and the best-matching verse anywhere in Brenton’s English of Jeremiah 1–36 (threshold 0.30); 142 verses fell below it and were read in the KJV and Brenton’s English. Verses above the threshold were not read one by one. The chapters were not read in full.
- Each issued record: KJV, WLC words and Brenton’s Greek and English asserted by script at the verse, and every source address answered (47 addresses checked).
- Published and drafted records at Jeremiah 1–29 were not duplicated: 7:22–23, 7:12–14, 18:7, 22:11, 22:19, 24:1, 25:20.
- Jeremiah 52 and the 2 Kings 25 parallel were issued in the 2 Kings session; not re-issued.

## Left out (reasons, so they are not re-opened)

- Brenton’s Greek prints no counterpart to KJV 8:11–12, 10:6–8 and 10:10, 17:1–4, 25:23–25 and 25:35–38, 29:12–18, 29:25–29 (and others): omitted verses are not issued (the standing decision on omitted verses).
- 10:9 “blue and purple”: Brenton’s English prints “scarlet”, but the Greek word printed is the one for purple, so the difference is in the English only.
- 4:26 “fruitful place” / Carmel; 15:12 (“northern iron and the steel”); 29:22 “roasted” / “fried”: held back for a later pass; quotes read.
- 22:25 (the Greek has no “Nebuchadrezzar”), 25:26 (“Sheshach” has no Greek counterpart): name omissions inside a verse, not issued.
- 3:9, 5:31, 15:11, 18:14, 20:8: wording differences without a plainly different word or name.
- No V: no act in 1–29 was found that is described, matched to a law by more than a shared verb, and left unrebuked; the passages that name sins (7:9, 22:13) are themselves rebukes.
