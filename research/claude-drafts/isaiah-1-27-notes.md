# Isaiah 1–27 — notes (2026-10-13)

All records are drafts until Codex accepts them. 16 records, all tag T, all witness "Septuagint"; no V, no untagged conflict. Every record carries `sides` (two have a side B on a New Testament verse). The Q records are in `isaiah-1-27-q-records.json` (see `isaiah-1-27-q-notes.md`).

## Verse numbers

- KJV 9:1 = Hebrew and Greek 8:23, and KJV 9:n = 9:(n−1) there for n = 2–21. The 9:6 record quotes the Hebrew and Greek at 9:5 and says so in its scope. KJV 8 has 22 verses; the Hebrew and Greek 8 have 23.
- Every other verse quoted carries the same number in the KJV, the Hebrew and Brenton's Greek.
- Brenton's Greek of Isaiah is a free rendering; many verses differ in wording without a difference in meaning. Only verses where a plainly different word, name or statement is printed were issued.

## Records (one line each)

1. `a-very-small-remnant-or-a-seed-greek` — 1:9, "a very small remnant" / "a seed"; the Greek New Testament prints the Greek word at Romans 9:29 (side B).
2. `say-ye-to-the-righteous-or-let-us-bind-the-just-greek` — 3:10, opposite treatment of the righteous.
3. `women-rule-over-them-or-extortioners-rule-over-you-greek` — 3:12, "women" / "extortioners".
4. `the-branch-of-the-lord-or-god-shall-shine-gloriously-in-counsel-greek` — 4:2, different subject.
5. `hear-ye-indeed-but-understand-not-or-by-hearing-ye-shall-hear-greek` — 6:9–10, commands in the KJV, statements in the Greek; Acts 28:26–27 prints the Greek wording (side B, both verses).
6. `the-holy-seed-shall-be-the-substance-thereof-greek-has-no-holy-seed` — 6:13, the closing clause; asserted by script that Brenton's Greek and English of the verse hold no "seed" or "holy".
7. `wonderful-counsellor-the-mighty-god-or-messenger-of-great-counsel-greek` — 9:6 (Hebrew and Greek 9:5); one title against five; asserted by script that Brenton's Greek and English hold no "mighty", "Father", "Wonderful".
8. `because-of-the-anointing-or-his-fear-from-thee-greek` — 10:27; asserted that the verse holds no "anoint", "oil".
9. `my-sanctified-ones-and-my-mighty-ones-or-giants-are-coming-greek` — 13:3; asserted that the verse holds no "sanctif", "holy".
10. `satyrs-shall-dance-there-or-devils-shall-dance-there-greek` — 13:21.
11. `the-city-of-destruction-or-the-city-of-asedec-greek` — 19:18, a name.
12. `blessed-be-egypt-my-people-and-assyria-the-work-of-my-hands-or-my-people-in-egypt-greek` — 19:25.
13. `and-he-cried-a-lion-or-call-thou-urias-to-the-watch-tower-greek` — 21:8, a name where the KJV has "A lion".
14. `pass-ye-over-to-tarshish-or-depart-ye-to-carthage-greek` — 23:6, a place name; 23:1 has the same difference and is named in the scope.
15. `he-will-swallow-up-death-in-victory-or-death-having-prevailed-has-swallowed-men-up-greek` — 25:8; 1 Corinthians 15:54 is side B.
16. `they-are-deceased-they-shall-not-rise-or-neither-shall-physicians-raise-them-up-greek` — 26:14.

No record says what a Hebrew word means. Each states what each text prints and does not claim which is original. Brenton's English is quoted only to show how that edition renders its Greek.

## Verses compared

All 27 chapters (510 verses of the KJV) were compared by script with the whole of Brenton's English of Isaiah 1–27 (best-matching verse and verse at the same number, so reordered verses are caught). The 107 verses with a low overlap were read in the KJV and Brenton's English; those that looked like a difference in meaning were read in the Hebrew (WLC) and the Greek. The chapters were not read in full in the original languages.

## Considered and not issued

- 7:14 "virgin" / Greek "παρθένος": the Greek agrees with the KJV; the Hebrew word has a range of senses that the KJV's choice lies inside. Left out (logged in `owner-questions.md`).
- 8:8 and 8:10 "Immanuel" / "O God with us": the KJV's Matthew 1:23 gives the meaning, and the Greek agrees only in part. Left out.
- 14:12 "Lucifer": Brenton's English prints "Lucifer" too; the KJV's word carries the same sense as the Hebrew's "bright one". Left out.
- 7:8 "threescore and five years": Greek agrees with the KJV.
- 2:16 "ships of Tarshish" / "ship of the sea": named in the 23:6 scope, not issued separately.
- 3:18–23 list of ornaments, 5:10 measures, 10:28–32 place names, 19:6–10, 21:13–14, 22:6–8: lists of terms and wording differences within a list; not issued.
- 1:1, 13:1 titles: a name spelled differently (Esaias, Amos) is a difference of form alone.
- 9:3 "and not increased the joy": the Hebrew has a written/read form here (background only).
- 26:19 "Thy dead men shall live": wording differences with no clear difference in meaning.
- 27:1 (Leviathan) is already a published Q; 27:3–11 differences are many and none is a single clear word. Held back: 27:8, 27:10 (Greek differences, quotes not sourced).
- Isaiah 36–39 are not in this part (Isaiah 39:2 is published).
- V: no law case in these chapters meets the test (no act described that a law clearly forbids and no disapproval from the narrator).
- Untagged conflicts: none found.
