# Deuteronomy 1–34 — Q records, notes

Session 2026-10-13. File: `deuteronomy-1-34-q-records.json` (3 records). All are drafts until Codex accepts them. All take `mode: "questions"`, `tags: ["Q"]`, `status: "Context"` and carry `sides`.

## Records

1. `he-buried-him-who-buried-moses` (34:6) — the burial is told with an unnamed subject and an unknown grave; who buried him changes how the unknown grave is read. Jude 1:9 remarks on the body without saying who buried it.
2. `the-host-of-heaven-divided-unto-all-nations-for-what` (4:19) — the verse says the sun, moon and stars were “divided unto all nations” and does not say for what; the same Hebrew verb is used at KJV 29:26 of gods “not given” to Israel.
3. `the-blessing-of-the-tribes-why-is-simeon-not-named` (33:1) — the blessing of “the children of Israel” names the other tribes and not Simeon, and gives no reason.
