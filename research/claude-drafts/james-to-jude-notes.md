## Editions and verses compared

- Greek New Testament text is the Textus Receptus (TR) and Tischendorf eighth edition (TISCH) as served by bolls.life books 59–65 (`https://bolls.life/get-text/TR/{book}/{chapter}/`, `…/TISCH/{book}/{chapter}/`). All 21 chapters were fetched and every verse where the TR and TISCH word lists differ was read in a full scan (about 190 lines); only differences of meaning were issued.
