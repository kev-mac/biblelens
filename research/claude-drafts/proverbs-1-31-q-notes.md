## Considered and not issued as Q

- Who the “strange woman” is (2:16, 5:3, 7:5): the book describes her by conduct throughout; no unstated identity materially affects interpretation.
- Lemuel and his mother (31:1): the name is issued in a T record (the Greek prints no name); a Q on the same name would repeat the Agur question.
- “The men of Hezekiah … copied out” (25:1): which writings they copied from is an omitted detail, not a question the text raises.
- Which proverbs are Solomon’s (1:1, 10:1, 25:1): stated by the headings.
- The “virtuous woman” as a person or a figure (31:10–31): the poem does not say; left out as a literary reading and not a gap in the passage.
