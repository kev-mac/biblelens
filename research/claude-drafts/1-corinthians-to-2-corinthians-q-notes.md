# 1 Corinthians to 2 Corinthians: Q notes (2026-10-14)

File: `1-corinthians-to-2-corinthians-q-records.json` (3 Q, mode "questions", status Context, all carry `sides`). All are drafts until Codex accepts them.

1. `who-are-they-which-are-baptized-for-the-dead-1-corinthians-15-29`: who is baptized “for the dead”, and what that was; the verse does not say whether Paul approves. Side A 15:29, side B 15:30.
2. `what-was-the-thorn-in-the-flesh-2-corinthians-12-7`: what the thorn (Greek σκόλοψ, stake) was; 12:8–9 say only that he asked three times and was told grace was sufficient. Side A 12:7, side B 12:9.
3. `because-of-the-angels-what-is-meant-1-corinthians-11-10`: which angels, and what “power on her head” is. Side A 11:10, side B 11:5.

Each record states what the passage does not say and holds the readings as possibilities. Greek is Tischendorf (TISCH) with the TR text checked as carrying the same words.

Considered and left out: which letter 1 Corinthians 5:9 means (“I wrote unto you in an epistle”); the “third heaven” of 2 Corinthians 12:2 (the verse itself says “I cannot tell” and the record would only restate that); “the rock that followed them” (1 Corinthians 10:4).
