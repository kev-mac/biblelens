# Numbers 1–36 — records, notes

Session of 2026-10-13. File: `numbers-1-36-records.json` (15 records: 12 G, 1 H+G, 2 untagged, no V; 6 carry `sides`). Q records are in `numbers-1-36-q-records.json` with their own notes. All are drafts until Codex accepts them.

Numbers was already well covered: 45 published records cite its verses, and nearly every plain conflict I checked was there (the Levite total, the Peor count, Aaron’s death, Balaam’s permission, the ark’s place on the march, the spies, the rock, Korah’s household, the Levite tithe). So this batch is mostly Greek wording.

## Verse numbers

No differences between the KJV, the Hebrew (WLC) and Brenton’s Greek at any verse quoted. Brenton’s Numbers 26 orders the tribes differently (noted in the Genesis session); nothing here rests on chapter 26.

## Records — why each qualifies

1. `levites-serve-from-thirty-years-old-or-from-twenty-five-greek` — G at 4:3, 4:23, 4:30, 4:35, 4:39, 4:43, 4:47. The Greek has twenty-five in all seven verses; quoted and asserted at each.
2. `suburbs-of-the-levite-cities-a-thousand-cubits-or-two-thousand-greek` — G at 35:4. The Greek has two thousand where the KJV has a thousand.
3. `alarms-for-the-east-and-south-camps-greek-adds-a-third-and-a-fourth` — G at 10:6. The Greek adds two signals the KJV does not state anywhere in the passage.
4. `thou-mayest-be-to-us-instead-of-eyes-or-an-elder-among-us-greek` — G at 10:31. “Eyes” and knowing the camping places are not in the Greek.
5. `they-prophesied-and-did-not-cease-or-did-so-no-more-hebrew` — H and G at 11:25. The pointed Hebrew and the Greek both say “did not add”; the KJV says “did not cease”, the opposite duration. The KJV renders the same verb form “added no more” at Deuteronomy 5:22.
6. `the-similitude-of-the-lord-shall-he-behold-or-he-saw-the-glory-greek` — G at 12:8. “Form” in the KJV, “glory” in the Greek.
7. `i-have-not-taken-one-ass-from-them-or-any-desirable-thing-greek` — G at 16:15. No animal in the Greek.
8. `what-he-did-in-the-red-sea-or-a-war-of-the-lord-set-zoob-on-fire-greek` — G at 21:14. No sea, no book title, and a different sentence in the Greek.
9. `let-my-last-end-be-like-his-or-my-seed-as-their-seed-greek` — G at 23:10. “End” in the KJV, “seed” in the Greek.
10. `water-out-of-his-buckets-and-agag-or-a-man-out-of-his-seed-and-gog-greek` — G at 24:7. No water in the Greek; a man who rules nations; Gog for Agag.
11. `a-sceptre-shall-rise-out-of-israel-or-a-man-greek` — G at 24:17. “Sceptre” in the KJV, “a man” in the Greek.
12. `he-took-up-his-parable-greek-adds-having-seen-og` — G at 24:23. The Greek names Og; the KJV of 24:20–24 does not (checked by search).
13. `kadesh-in-the-wilderness-of-zin-or-of-paran-greek` — G at 33:36. The Greek adds a stage and puts Kadesh in Pharan; the KJV itself has Paran at 13:26 and Zin elsewhere.
14. `amalekites-and-canaanites-dwelt-in-the-valley-or-in-that-hill` — untagged. “Dwelt in the valley” (14:25) against “dwelt in that hill” (14:45), and “Amorites” in Deuteronomy 1:44 for the same defeat. Hebrew and Greek agree with the KJV.
15. `hormah-named-in-the-days-of-moses-or-by-judah-and-simeon` — untagged. Numbers 21:3 against Judges 1:17. Issued here because Numbers is the earlier book; no Judges record covers Judges 1:17.

## For Codex

- **Records 1 and 2 stand beside published records** (`numbers-levite-age-thresholds`, `numbers-levite-town-measurements`) that state the KJV’s own two figures without any Greek. I issued the Greek wording as separate G records, as with Exodus 24:10 beside the `seen` study, and did not rewrite the published text. If you would rather carry the G tag on the published IDs, the quotations here are what you need.
- **Record 6** cites a verse the built-in `seen` study also cites (Numbers 12:8). It adds only the Greek wording.
- **Record 5, H:** the scope states that the consonants without the points have been read as another verb. The pointed text and the Greek agree, which is the case the standing rule on vowel points covers. Deuteronomy 5:22 is context only and is not in `appliesTo`.
- **Record 10:** Brenton’s Greek prints `ἡ Γὼγ βασιλεία`. The bracket follows Brenton’s own English page (“the kingdom of Gog shall be exalted”). The scope says the relation of Gog to the kingdom is not decided.
- **Record 8:** no H. The Hebrew line has no word for “sea” and no verb “did”, but its two key words are of unsettled meaning, so no meaning difference is shown.
- **Record 15:** Brenton’s Greek translates the name Hormah (“Anathema”) in both verses. That is noted in the scope and not tagged: it is the same in both verses and does not bear on the conflict.
- **Typo in the packaged KJV:** Numbers 21:33 reads “og the king of bashan … at edrei” without capitals. Not quoted in any record.
- The G tags sit only on verses where the Greek differs; the other side of each comparison is in `refs` and `sides`.

## Considered and ruled out

- 3:22, 3:28, 3:34 against 3:39 (22,300 or 22,000): published. Brenton’s Greek has the same figures, so no G.
- 25:9 against 1 Corinthians 10:8; 20:28 against Deuteronomy 10:6; 22:20 against 22:22; 10:33 against 2:17; 16:32 against 26:11; 13:2 against Deuteronomy 1:22; 18:21 against Deuteronomy 14:23: all published.
- 5:21, 5:22, 5:27, “thigh to rot” where the Hebrew verb is “fall”: the Hebrew can carry the KJV’s sense of wasting away, so no H.
- 23:22 and 24:8, “unicorn”: published as a translation note; the Greek agrees with the KJV and the Hebrew animal is not identified here, so no H or G.
- 13:33, “giants” for the Hebrew nephilim: the Greek agrees with the KJV; the name is not made of ordinary words whose meaning is shown.
- 21:1, “the way of the spies” where the Hebrew and Greek have a name: meaning unsettled; held back.
- 11:8 against Exodus 16:31 (taste of fresh oil, or of wafers with honey): Numbers 11:8 itself says the manna was ground and baked, so the two do not clash.
- 14:23 Greek addition about the children: the KJV says the same at 14:31, in the same passage.
- 24:25 against 31:8 (Balaam went home, or was killed in Midian): nothing in the wording excludes a return.
- 8:24 against 1 Chronicles 23:24–27 (twenty-five, or twenty): Chronicles gives the change as David’s own order.
- 21:8–9 against Exodus 20:4: published (`numbers-serpent-making-and-worship`).
- V: nothing qualifies. The breaches told in Numbers are punished in the story (the sabbath-breaker, Korah, Peor, the rock) or are acts commanded in it (the serpent, Midian).
