# Luke — Q notes (2026-10-14)

All drafts until Codex accepts them. 3 Q records in `luke-1-24-q-records.json`, each with a side A on the key verse:

- `the-lord-commended-the-unjust-steward-which-lord-luke-16-8` — “the lord commended the unjust steward” — which lord?
- `two-swords-it-is-enough-what-is-enough-luke-22-38` — “Lord, behold, here are two swords. And he said unto them, It is enough.” — what is enough?
- `the-kingdom-of-god-is-within-you-who-and-where-luke-17-21` — “the kingdom of God is within you” — within whom, or where?

Each follows the Q contract: the passage leaves the point open, readings are named only as readings, and none is chosen. Absence claims are bounded to the verses quoted.

Considered and left out: Luke 13:1 (Pilate mingling the blood of the Galileans: an event outside the text, not a gap the passage’s own question needs); 22:43–44 (the angel and the sweat: depends on a textual question); 10:1 (the seventy: ordinary omitted detail); 24:18 (Cleopas’ companion: ordinary omitted detail); 11:41 (“such things as ye have”: the verse is understood from its own clause); 16:16 (“every man presseth into it”: depends on a reading of the verb).
