## Untagged conflicts (KJV only)

- `the-face-of-an-ox-or-the-first-face-the-face-of-a-cherub-ezekiel-1-10` — the four faces at 1:10 (man, lion, ox, eagle) against 10:14 (cherub, man, lion, eagle); 10:20 in refs. Reconciliation named in the scope (the word “cherub” may be a name for the creature or for the ox face); the text does not report it.
- `the-just-shall-surely-live-or-i-will-cut-off-the-righteous-and-the-wicked-ezekiel-21-3` — 18:9 against 21:3, with 9:6 as context. Reconciliations named; the verses do not say which holds.
- `statutes-which-if-a-man-do-he-shall-live-or-statutes-not-good-whereby-they-should-not-live-ezekiel-20-11` — 20:11 against 20:25 in one chapter; the “also” in 20:25 is the named reconciliation.

## V

None. Ezekiel 8 and 14 name idolatry, but each is rebuked in its own passage, so no act is “unchecked”; the commanded acts of chapters 4–5 are God’s commands, not violations.

## Verses compared

Every verse of chapters 1–24 was screened: the KJV verse against Brenton’s English (best match across the book, so reordered verses are caught), plus a number scan (number words) and a name scan (capitalised words) at the aligned verse. Only the verses flagged by those screens, listed above or below, were read in Hebrew and Greek. The chapters were not read in full.

## Considered and not issued (quotes read)

- 1:2 “Jehoiachin” against Brenton’s “Joakim”: Brenton’s own 2 Kings 24:6 prints the same form for the son, so the name is a form, not a different name.
- 1:16 “beryl” (Greek θαρσείς; Brenton’s English prints “beryl”): no difference in Brenton’s English.
- 3:15 “Telabib” (no place name in the Greek), 8:7 (“a hole in the wall” not in the Greek), 10:14, 11:11–12, 17:20 (“I will bring him to Babylon” not in the Greek), 7:16 (“doves” not in the Greek): omissions, not issued under the omissions instruction.
- 7:7, 7:11, 7:23, 16:27, 16:29–30, 20:26, 21:10, 24:17: Greek differs in wording but the Hebrew words are obscure (“sounding again of the mountains”, “make a chain”, “ordinary food”) or the difference is one of how the act is described; no plainly different word to quote.
- 16:10 “badgers’ skin” (Greek: “purple”): the Hebrew word is uncertain; left out.
- 20:46–47 “south”: Brenton prints the place names Darom and Nageb for words the KJV renders as directions; a transliteration, not a different meaning.
- 8:14 Tammuz (“Thammuz”), 14:14 and 14:20 Noah (“Noe”), 8:12, 23:23 Pekod, Shoa, Koa (Phacuc, Sue, Hychue), 23:4 (Aholah, Aholibah): spelling or form only.
- 12:13 with Jeremiah 34:3 and 52:11 (Zedekiah “shall not see” Babylon; blinded before he came there): the two accounts fit (2 Kings 25:7 says his eyes were put out and he was carried to Babylon), so there is no clash to record. The Jeremiah 30–52 note asked whether the referent could be shown: 12:13 and 17:20 share the formula “my net also will I spread upon him”, and 17:12–20 describe the king set up by Babylon, but the comparison fails on its own terms.
- 18:20 (the son shall not bear the iniquity of the father) is already published with Lamentations 5:7 (`exp-late-24-5-7`, `exp-late-25-18-19`). Not re-issued.
- 21:27 is quoted in the published Shiloh Q (Genesis 49:10). Not re-issued; no T on it.
