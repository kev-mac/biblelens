## Q records

1. `thou-hast-been-in-eden-the-anointed-cherub-who-is-the-king-of-tyrus-addressed-as-ezekiel-28-13` — 28:2 addresses “the prince of Tyrus” and 28:12 “the king of Tyrus”; 28:13–14 add Eden, a day of creation and “the anointed cherub”. The verses do not say how the one addressed relates to the garden and the cherub. Three readings are named, none chosen. Genesis 2–3 and Isaiah 14 are not examined (stated in the scope). The Greek wording of 28:13–16 is a separate T record.
2. `gog-of-the-land-of-magog-who-is-gog-and-when-is-the-latter-years-ezekiel-38-2` — 38:2 names Gog; 38:8 and 38:16 give “after many days”, “the latter years” and “the latter days” with no reckoning. The record says what the verses leave open; the Greek “Rhos” difference at 38:2 and 39:1 is a separate T record.
3. `shew-the-house-to-the-house-of-israel-is-the-house-to-be-built-ezekiel-43-10` — 40:4 and 43:10–11 tell the prophet to declare and write the house’s form so that it may be kept; the verses do not say whether, when or by whom it is to be built. Three readings named, none chosen.

## Considered and not issued as Q

- Ezekiel 34:23–24 (“my servant David”): 34:23 names the shepherd as “my servant David”; a question about a later “David” would rest on one reading of a name the verse gives plainly.
- Ezekiel 37:1–14 (the dry bones): 37:11 states what the bones are (“the whole house of Israel”); no unstated identity.
- Ezekiel 47:1–12 (the river from the house): the verses describe it and name where it goes (47:8); what the river is for is stated by its effects (47:9, 47:12).
- Ezekiel 44:1–3 (the shut east gate): the reason is given in 44:2 (“the Lord, the God of Israel, hath entered in by it”); who the prince is is stated (“the prince”) with no gap beyond a title.
- Ezekiel 36:25–27 (clean water, a new heart): the effect is stated in the verses; no unstated mechanism the passage needs.
- Ezekiel 33:7–9 (the watchman): the office is explained in the verses themselves.
- Ezekiel 39:25–29 (the return): no unstated identity or cause.
- Ezekiel 28:2 (the prince of Tyrus): covered inside Q 1 above; a second Q on 28:2 would repeat it.
