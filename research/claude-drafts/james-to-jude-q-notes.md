# James to Jude — Q notes (2026-10-14)

All drafts until Codex accepts them. 3 Q records in `james-to-jude-q-records.json`, each with a side A on the key verse:

- `preached-unto-the-spirits-in-prison-who-where-when-and-what-1-peter-3-19-20` — Who are “the spirits in prison”, and when, where and what did he preach (1 Peter 3:19–20)?
- `the-gospel-preached-also-to-them-that-are-dead-who-and-when-1-peter-4-6` — Who are “them that are dead” to whom the gospel “was preached” (1 Peter 4:6)?
- `there-is-a-sin-unto-death-which-sin-and-what-death-1-john-5-16-17` — Which is “a sin unto death”, and what is meant by death (1 John 5:16–17)?

Each follows the Q contract: the passage leaves the point open, readings are named only as readings, and none is chosen. Absence claims are bounded to the verses quoted; word searches are word searches of the packaged KJV (case-sensitive, whole phrase: “spirits in prison” occurs only at 1 Peter 3:19; “preached also” only at 1 Peter 4:6; “sin unto death” at Romans 6:16 and 1 John 5:16). The Greek quoted is Tischendorf (eighth edition) as served by bolls.life, checked against the Textus Receptus text served there.

Considered and left out: James 5:14–15 (anointing and the prayer of faith: the verse states who is to be called and what is to be done, and the question would be about practice); 1 Peter 3:21 (“the like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us”: a statement of a relation, not an unstated fact); 2 Peter 2:4 and Jude 1:6 (the angels that sinned: covered by the published Q on the sons of God and the daughters of men, which lists both verses); 2 Peter 3:10 (the elements and the earth burned up: covered by the published coverage record on the delay); Jude 1:9 (Michael and the body of Moses: covered by the published Q `he-buried-him-who-buried-moses`); Jude 1:14–15 (Enoch’s prophecy: the verse names the source as Enoch, and the passage cited is not in the Old Testament text compared; the record would be an argument from silence); 1 John 2:18 (“it is the last time”: a date given, not left open); 1 Peter 3:7 (“the weaker vessel”: a description, not a question).
