# John — notes (2026-10-14)

Unit: John 1–21 (21 chapters, 879 verses). **All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.**

Counts: 55 records in `john-1-21-records.json`: 49 T (all witness "Greek New Testament") and 6 untagged; no V; every record carries `sides`. 3 Q in `john-1-21-q-records.json` (separate notes file).

## Editions and numbering

- Greek New Testament text is the Textus Receptus (TR) and Tischendorf eighth edition (TISCH) as served by bolls.life book 43 (`https://bolls.life/get-text/TR/43/{chapter}/`, `…/TISCH/43/{chapter}/`). TR is served without accents; TISCH is quoted.
- **Verse numbering:** the TISCH page for John 1 divides verses 38–51 one verse away from the KJV and TR (its verse 38 ends at “saith unto them”; the question “What seek ye?” is in its 39; the naming of Simon is in its 43). The Simon-son-of-John record quotes the clause at the TISCH page’s 1:43 and says so. The TISCH page ends John at 21:24 and prints no 21:25.
- The TR text served is not always the text behind the KJV: John 8:6 (“as though he heard them not”) is in the KJV and in neither page served, so it is not issued as an edition difference.
- Where a TISCH quote could not be a substring of the TR text, the record asserts that by script; where the same words stand in both, it asserts that (a `ctx` context verse).
