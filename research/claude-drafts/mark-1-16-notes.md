# Mark — notes (2026-10-14)

Unit: Mark 1–16 (16 chapters, 678 verses). **All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.**

Counts: 47 records in `mark-1-16-records.json`: 36 T (all witness "Greek New Testament") and 11 untagged; no V; every record carries `sides`. 3 Q in `mark-1-16-q-records.json` (separate notes file).

## Editions and numbering

- Greek New Testament text is the Textus Receptus (TR) and Tischendorf eighth edition (TISCH) as served by bolls.life book 41 (`https://bolls.life/get-text/TR/41/{chapter}/`, `…/TISCH/41/{chapter}/`). Verse numbers agree with the KJV; no offset. The TR text is served without accents and is quoted as served. The SBLGNT page was not used.
- The KJV follows the TR in the edition records, so each compares the KJV with Tischendorf’s text; each says that the TR text served agrees with the KJV and that the record does not say which reading is original.
- The Tischendorf page served prints the verse number with **no text** at 7:16, 9:44, 9:46, 11:26 and 15:28. Four are issued (9:44 and 9:46 together; 11:26; 15:28); each states only what the one served page prints. 7:16 was not issued (batch size). This is the same pattern as Matthew 17:21, 18:11 and 23:14.
- The page served prints Mark 16:9–20 in full, and the two editions differ there only at 16:18 and 16:19 (spelling of verbs). No record on the ending.

Notes on particular T records:
- 7:3 (“oft”): the KJV’s English matches Tischendorf’s πυκνά, not the TR’s πυγμῇ. The record has its own scope and says it does not decide the sense of πυγμῇ. If Codex rules that a word whose sense is disputed should not carry T, this is the one to hold.
- 7:19 (“purging all meats”): one letter of the participle (neuter against masculine). The record does not say who or what the masculine form is about.
- 1:2 sits beside the draft Malachi 3:1 record (`prepare-the-way-before-me-or-prepare-thy-way-before-thee-malachi-3-1`); the two are about different things (the Hebrew and Greek of Malachi 3:1, and which prophet Mark names). Malachi 3:1 is side B here. This is the Malachi 3:1 against Mark 1:2 item deferred from the Nahum to Malachi session.
- 9:31 and 10:34 share one record with Mark 8:31 as context: the KJV of Mark has “after three days” at 8:31 and both editions agree there.
- 12:29–30 shares Mark 12:30 with the published `love-god-with-heart-soul-might-or-with-mind-soul-strength-greek`; the scope says this record is only about the labels “first of all the commandments” and “this is the first commandment”.

## Verses compared

All 678 verses of Mark were compared by script between the TR and Tischendorf texts (every verse where the word lists differ was listed and read; spelling, name-spelling, word-order, verb-tense and “immediately” spellings (εὐθέως / εὐθύς) were not issued). Where a difference touched meaning, the KJV was read against both. KJV-against-Greek translation differences where **both** editions agree were screened by reading and not by script; this is not an exhaustive word-by-word translation audit of the 16 chapters.
