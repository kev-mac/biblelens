# Isaiah second pass (workstream D), 2026-10-20

First-time reading of the 30 Isaiah chapters that had no published record: 2, 5, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 22, 24, 27, 33, 35, 38, 39, 43, 44, 46, 47, 50, 51, 52, 55, 58, 60, 62, 64, 65, 66. The KJV and Brenton's Greek were read side by side for every chapter except 52, 62 and 65, where I read only the verse named in a record. Hebrew (WLC) was fetched by script for all 30 and compared only at the verses that raised a question. All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## Issued (23 records, `isaiah-second-pass-records.json`)

**Untagged Context, KJV against KJV, Hebrew (and Greek NT) quoted (2)**
- `all-nations-shall-flow-...-isaiah-2-2-and-micah-4-1`: Isaiah 2:2-4 and Micah 4:1-3 swap "nations" and "people" (גּוֹיִם / עַמִּים). Per the standing rule it is issued with the earlier book (Isaiah). Micah 4:5 and Joel 3:10 already have their own records; this one does not overlap them.
- `for-him-that-waiteth-for-him-or-for-them-that-love-him-isaiah-64-4-and-1-corinthians-2-9`: Isaiah 64:4 against 1 Corinthians 2:9. Issued here by the same rule; nothing in the 1 Corinthians drafts covers Isaiah 64 (checked by search). No claim of misquotation; Paul's "as it is written" is not said to be verbatim.

**T, Hebrew and Septuagint (2)** — the owner's ruling on ordinary-word names (Immanuel rule)

- 65:11 "that troop" and "that number" for לַגַּד and לַמְנִי (Gad and Meni). The record does not say they were gods and notes that the KJV's printed margin was not examined.

## Considered and ruled out
- 2:6, 5:25, 5:17, 22:6 (Kir missing in the Greek), 22:15 (Shebna/Somnas, a transliteration), 22:23-25 (nail against ruler), 27:1 (Leviathan/dragon-serpent), 27:2 ("vineyard of red wine" against "beautiful vineyard"), 27:12 (stream of Egypt/Rhinocorura, same place), 33:7, 38:8 (dial of Ahaz/house of thy father: same person), 38:10, 38:19, 38:21-22 against 2 Kings 20:7-8 (Isaiah's verses sit after the psalm; order only), 39:2 (already covered by `hezekiah-hearkened-to-the-envoys-or-was-glad-of-them`), 43:3 (Seba/Soene), 43:14, 43:23-24, 43:27, 44:2 (Jesurun), 46:1, 47:13, 51:6 ("in like manner"/"like gnats": disputed sense, not a plain difference), 52:14, 58:13, 60:17-18, 62:5, 64:5-6.
- 14:12 "Lucifer" and 7:14 "virgin" are excluded by the owner's rulings and were not touched.
- 11:14 (shoulders of the Philistines against ships of foreigners) was drafted and dropped: `ἀλλόφυλοι` is the Septuagint's ordinary word for Philistines and the difference rests on one uncertain word.
- 14:29-31 "Palestina" is the KJV's own spelling of Philistia; not a finding.
