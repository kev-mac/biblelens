## Considered and ruled out

- Isaiah 2:2-4 beside Micah 4:1-3 (nations / peoples swapped, "rebuke many people" / "strong nations afar off"): already published as `exp-late-32-4-1` ("A peace oracle shared with Isaiah").
- Isaiah 39:2 beside 2 Kings 20:13 (glad of them / hearkened): already published (`hezekiah-hearkened-to-the-envoys-or-was-glad-of-them`).
- Isaiah 39:1 "Merodachbaladan" beside 2 Kings 20:12 "Berodachbaladan": two spellings of one king's name in the KJV; found in no draft or published record. Left out as a spelling difference only (the Hebrew differs by one consonant); Codex may want it as an untagged Context card.
- Isaiah 39:8 "peace and truth" (Greek "peace and righteousness"): small; 2 Kings 20:19 puts it as a question. Not issued.
- Isaiah 38:5-8 beside 2 Kings 20:5-11 (third day, "for my servant David's sake", choice of the shadow's direction, "afore Isaiah was gone out into the middle court"): omissions in Isaiah, no direct contradiction.
- Isaiah 15–16 beside Jeremiah 48: same Moab places and similar laments ("Kirhareseth / Kirheres", "baldness / bald"), spelling variants of one name; no contradiction.
- Isaiah 14:12 "Lucifer": the Greek has ἑωσφόρος and Brenton's English also renders it "Lucifer"; the Hebrew is הֵילֵל בֶּן־שָׁחַר ("shining one, son of the dawn"). The KJV word is Latin for "light-bringer", the same meaning, so it fails the test that H needs a difference in meaning. Left out; Codex may want a note for the debate on whether the word names a person.
- Isaiah 14:23 (bittern / hedgehog), 35:7-8 (dragons / birds; "fools" / "the dispersed"), 33:6-9 and 33:18, 43:3 (Seba / Syene), 43:14, 47:2, 51:6, 62:5 ("sons marry thee" / "dwell in thee"), 2:16 ("pleasant pictures" / "fine ships"), 2:20 ("moles" / "vanities"): real Greek differences, but each is of the same kind as the 16 issued; held to keep the batch small. They can be issued in a later unit if the owner wants a fuller set.
- Isaiah 2:22: the Greek of Brenton has no verse 22 ("Cease ye from man"); an omission, not a different statement. Not issued.
- Isaiah 64:4 beside 1 Corinthians 2:9: the KJV and the Greek differ, and Paul's wording differs from both; needs the Greek New Testament opened beside it. Not done in this unit.
- Isaiah 20:1 (Sargon): a date and a name; no archaeology without a primary publication opened. Not used.
- Chapters 12, 43 (other verses), 46, 47 (other verses), 50, 51, 55, 58, 62 (other verses), 64: poetry or oracle; no direct wording contrast and no Greek difference in meaning beyond what is listed.
