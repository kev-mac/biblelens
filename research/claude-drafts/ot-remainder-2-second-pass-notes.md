## Considered, not issued
- Ezra 7:1-5 lineage: KJV and Greek agree with each other; the gaps against 1 Chronicles 6 are a cross-book question for a later check, not a Greek difference. The Greek spelling Σαραίου etc. is transliteration only.
- Ezra 7:17 "bullocks" against "calves", 7:22 measures, 7:24 "toll, tribute, or custom" against "tribute / oppress": synonyms or the same sense.
- Ezra 7:23 "diligently" (אַדְרַזְדָּא) against "take heed lest any one make an attack": a real Greek expansion but the Aramaic adverb is rare, so the sense cannot be shown without a lexicon; held back.

- Nehemiah 5:15 "forty shekels" against "forty didrachms": a coin name in each; same sum.
- Nehemiah 5:7 "exact usury" against "demand": wording.
- Nehemiah 13:3 "mixed multitude" against "every alien", 13:4 "chamber" against "treasury", 13:30 "wards" against "courses": wording or synonyms.
- Nehemiah 13:28 "Sanballat the Horonite" against "Uranite": spelling of a place name. Brenton's English line reads as if Eliasib were the son-in-law, but the Greek (ἀπὸ υἱῶν Ἰωαδὰ τοῦ Ἐλισοὺβ) follows the Hebrew; no difference.
- Nehemiah 5:1-4, 6, 8-10, 12-13, 16-17, 19 and 13:1-15, 17-24, 26-27, 29, 31: agree in sense.
