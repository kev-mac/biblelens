## Checks made

- 45: Psalms 45:1, 45:2, 45:6, 45:7 and Hebrews 1:8 read in the packaged KJV. The psalm’s title is not in the packaged KJV and was not examined; the record says so. Hebrews 1:8 is stated as an application, not as the answer to who the king was for the psalm’s first singers.
- 55: Psalms 55:12–14 and 55:20–21 read in the packaged KJV; Hebrew and Greek quoted at 55:14 (Hebrew numbering). No other psalm or book was searched.
- 69: word search of the packaged KJV, asserted by script: “book of the living” occurs once (Psalms 69:28); “book of life” occurs in eight verses (Philippians 4:3; Revelation 3:5, 13:8, 17:8, 20:12, 20:15, 21:27, 22:19). Exodus 32:32–33 is the published `exodus-intercession-relenting` verse pair and is quoted as context only; the overlap is the verses, not the question (that record is about intercession and relenting).

## Considered and not issued as Q

- Psalm 51 (what the sin was): the Hebrew title of the psalm itself names the occasion, so the question is answered in the psalm’s own text.
- Psalm 72:1 and 72:20 (which king, and whose prayers): the Hebrew verse 1 opens with a prefixed form of “Solomon” whose force depends on the title; left out as a title question.
- Psalm 44 (what defeat is lamented): the psalm is a lament in general terms; left out as lyric rather than a material gap.
- Psalm 58:1 (to whom “O congregation” is addressed): the Hebrew word is unclear and would need a dictionary source.
- Psalm 49:15 (“he shall receive me”): too dependent on one interpretation.
- Psalm 51:11 (the “holy spirit”): left out as interpretation-dependent.
- Psalm 78:60 (Shiloh): already published; do not re-issue.
