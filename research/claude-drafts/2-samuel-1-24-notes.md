## Considered and ruled out

- 8:17 "Ahimelech the son of Abiathar" against 1 Samuel 22:20 "Abiathar, son of Ahimelech": 1 Chronicles 24:6 also has an Ahimelech son of Abiathar, so a grandson of the same name is stated elsewhere; no conflict shown.
- 12:30 "their king's crown": Brenton's Greek adds the name Molchom; I did not source what that name refers to. Dropped.
- 12:31 "put them under saws": the Hebrew sense is disputed and the issue is ethical.
- 15:7 "after forty years": Brenton's Greek also has forty.
- 14:26 two hundred shekels of hair: Brenton's Greek also has two hundred.
- 6:6 Nachon's threshingfloor against Chidon's (1 Chr 13:9); 3:3 Chileab against Daniel; 24:16 Araunah against Ornan; 2:8 Ishbosheth against Eshbaal: names only.
- 5:4–5 forty years against seven years six months plus thirty-three: rounding.
- 8:1 Methegammah against "Gath and her towns" (1 Chr 18:1): not a direct contrast without an argument about the word.
- 10:6 against 1 Chr 19:6–7 (hired troops): different groupings, not one figure against another.
- 23:39 "thirty and seven in all": the count depends on how the names are divided.
- 22 against Psalm 18: poetry.
- 21:1–9 (Saul's sons killed) against Deuteronomy 24:16: an assessment; and the story has God accept it.
- 8:7 Greek plus (Shishak later took the gold shields) and 14:27 Greek plus (Tamar married Rehoboam and bore Abijah): real additions that say something the KJV lacks. Held back to keep the batch small; quotes are sourced. Say if they are wanted.
- 13:39 Hebrew: its sense is not settled, so no H.
- 6:19 Hebrew word for the third gift: meaning not sourced, so no H.
- V candidates: David's adultery and Uriah (rebuked, chapter 12); Uzzah (punished); Amnon (the story calls it folly and he is killed); the census (punished); the hanging of Saul's sons left exposed (done by Gibeonites, who are not Israelites). David taking more wives (5:13) against Deuteronomy 17:17: "multiply" sets no number, 12:8 has God give him wives, and I cannot state a whole-Bible check. All dropped, none issued untagged.

## Question for the owner

`absalom-three-sons-or-no-son`: its scope says that if the three sons died young both statements are true, though the book never says they died. Codex held a 1 Samuel record whose scope conceded the two statements need not conflict.
