# Isaiah 40–66 — notes (2026-10-13)

All records are drafts until Codex accepts them. 16 records, all tag T, all witness "Septuagint"; no V, no untagged conflict. Every record carries `sides`; four have a side B on a New Testament verse (1 Peter 1:24, Romans 11:34, Romans 11:26, Luke 4:18), quoted in KJV only. The Q records are in `isaiah-40-66-q-records.json` (see `isaiah-40-66-q-notes.md`). This finishes Isaiah.

## Verse numbers

- Verse numbers agree in the KJV, the Hebrew (WLC) and Brenton's Greek at every verse quoted. Elsewhere in chapter 64 the Hebrew and Greek numbers run one behind the KJV: KJV 64:1 is the last clause of Hebrew and Greek 63:19, and KJV 64:n = 64:(n−1) for n = 2–12. Brenton's Greek prints no counterpart of KJV 56:12 or 64:12 (not issued; omitted-verse records are not to be issued).
- Brenton's pages are ISA40–ISA66; bolls.life book 23; packaged KJV index 22. Brenton's Isaiah is a free rendering: many verses differ in wording without a difference in meaning, and only plainly different words, names or statements were issued.

## Method and what was compared

- Every KJV verse of 40–66 was scored against the same-numbered and the best-matching verse of Brenton's English (word-overlap, threshold 0.25); 98 verses fell below it and were read in the three texts. Verses above the threshold were not read one by one, and chapters were not read in full. Several verses outside the flagged list were also opened because the New Testament quotes them (40:3, 40:6, 49:12, 53:9–11, 61:1).
- For each record the KJV, the WLC words and Brenton's Greek and English were asserted by script at the verse, and each source address answered.
- Isaiah 40:28 (Exodus session) and 45:1 (built-in study) were not re-issued. Published records at 40:14, 44:6, 45:7, 56:3–5, 59:7–8 and 63:10 were not duplicated.
