# Daniel to Hosea — Q notes (2026-10-13)

File: `daniel-to-hosea-q-records.json` (3 Q records, all drafts until Codex accepts them). Main records and notes: `daniel-to-hosea-records.json` and `daniel-to-hosea-notes.md`.

## Records

1. `the-form-of-the-fourth-is-like-the-son-of-god-who-is-the-fourth-man-in-the-furnace-daniel-3-25` — the king counts four men loose where three were bound (3:24–25) and describes the fourth by likeness; at 3:28 he blesses the God who “hath sent his angel”. The verses quoted do not say who the fourth is. Readings are named as readings only. No Bible-wide claim is made; the record says it does not examine other uses of “Son of God”.
2. `darius-the-mede-took-the-kingdom-who-is-darius-the-mede-and-how-does-he-stand-to-cyrus-daniel-5-31` — Darius the Median (5:31), son of Ahasuerus (9:1), “the reign of Darius, and … the reign of Cyrus the Persian” (6:28), with years of Cyrus at 1:21 and 10:1. The verses do not say how the two reigns relate. Daniel 9:1 is also a context reference of the published Ezra 4 question (`ahasuerus-and-artaxerxes-between-cyrus-and-darius-which-kings-are-meant`); this record does not examine that question and says so.
3. `sent-to-king-jareb-who-is-king-jareb-hosea-5-13` — “king Jareb” at Hosea 5:13 and 10:6. A script search of all 1,189 packaged KJV chapters (whole name, case-sensitive) found the name only at those two verses; the record says it is a word search of the packaged KJV.

## For Codex

- Daniel 11:1 (the Greek has Cyrus where the KJV has Darius the Mede) is a T record in the main batch. Record 2 does not rest on it and refers to it only as a separate record.
- Daniel 3:24–90 (the Greek addition) is a T record in the main batch; record 1 does not rest on it.

## Considered and not issued

- **The “one like the Son of man” (Daniel 7:13) and “the saints of the most High” (7:18, 7:27):** the identity is a live question, but the chapter itself gives an interpretation (7:17–27) and the Q would have to take a position on how far that interpretation answers it; left out.
- **The “watchers” and “holy ones” of Daniel 4:13, 4:17, 4:23:** the verses name them as a class and what they decree; the question would be a theological premise.
- **Daniel 9:24–27 (the seventy weeks):** the verses state the years and several events and leave the referents to interpretation; any record would have to name rival readings of a long debate, and the withheld referents are not one gap. Left out.
- **Who “Gomer” is and why the prophet is told to take her (Hosea 1:2–3):** the question is about a literal or symbolic act, which depends on a theological premise.
- **“The days of Gibeah” (Hosea 9:9, 10:9):** the verses do not say which event is meant; considered and held back because the packaged chapters of Judges 19–21 would need to be read for the answer and no whole-Bible check could be stated in this session.
- **Who “Shalman” is and what happened at “Betharbel” (Hosea 10:14):** a real gap, held back for this session because the T record on the Greek of the same verse already quotes the clause; a later Q session may take it.
- **Who is “Jareb”, again:** issued as record 3.

## Limits

- The books were not read in the Hebrew or Aramaic in full. Every verse of Daniel 1–12 and Hosea 1–14 was read in the KJV beside Brenton’s English page; the Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek were read at the verses where a difference showed.
- Q claims are limited to the verses quoted in each record.
