## Considered and ruled out
- 10:2 "returned out of Egypt" against 1 Kings 12:2 "dwelt in Egypt": already ruled out in 1-kings-1-22-notes.md (disputed Hebrew, written and read forms).
- 12:10-11: Brenton's Greek may make Susakim (Shishak) the one who appoints the guard, but the Greek subject is ambiguous and verse 11 is obscure. Not issued.
- 1:16-17 "linen yarn": the Hebrew is obscure (KJV margin "a drove"); Greek prints "the charge of the merchants for going". Not issued.
- 10:15 "the cause was of God" / "a change from God"; 10:17; 12:6 "princes" / "elders"; 12:3 Sukkiims / Trogodytes; 6:28, 6:32 stretched-out / high arm; 18:8 officer / eunuch; 18:33 joints of the harness; 19:2 ungodly / sinner; 23:9 spears, bucklers / swords; 26:10 loved husbandry / was a husbandman; 26:22 Isaiah / Jessias; 30:11; 30:18; Greek spellings of names in 11:6-10, 29:12-14, 31:13-15: wording or spelling only, no shown change of meaning.
- 25:8: the Greek and KJV read as the same warning on a conditional.
- 25:12 (ten thousand thrown from the rock) against 2 Kings 14:7 (Selah taken): Kings is silent on the throwing; no contradiction.
- 12:13, 29:1, 32:21 against 2 Kings 14:21, 18:2, 19:35: figures agree or the later book gives no figure. The Ahaz and Hezekiah age arithmetic is covered by the published `ahaz-twenty-years-old-or-twenty-five-greek-chronicles`.
- 11:20 and 12:2 (Maachah, Shishak) are covered by published records; 1, 6 and 18 (Gibeon, dedication, Micaiah) have no wording contrast beyond synonyms.
- 30 and 31 (Passover in the second month, tithes): no direct wording contrast with another passage found.
