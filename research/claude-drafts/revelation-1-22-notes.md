Unit: Revelation 1–22 (22 chapters). **All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.** This is the last unit of the plan.

Counts: 48 records in `revelation-1-22-records.json`, all T with witness "Greek New Testament" (46 edition differences where the KJV and the Textus Receptus text served agree and Tischendorf’s text differs; 1 word record, 16:5, where both editions print the same Greek and the KJV’s English is different); no untagged; no V; every record carries `sides`. 3 Q in `revelation-1-22-q-records.json` (separate notes file).

## Editions and verses compared

- Greek New Testament text is the Textus Receptus (TR) and Tischendorf eighth edition (TISCH) as served by bolls.life book 66 (`https://bolls.life/get-text/TR/66/{chapter}/`, `…/TISCH/66/{chapter}/`). All 22 chapters were fetched and every verse where the TR and TISCH word lists differ was read in a full scan (about 320 lines). Only differences of meaning were issued.
- Verses compared in detail (KJV, TR and TISCH side by side): 1:5, 1:6, 1:8, 1:9, 1:11, 1:13, 1:18, 2:1, 2:5, 2:8, 2:9, 2:13, 2:15, 2:21, 2:22, 3:12, 3:14, 4:11, 5:5, 5:9, 5:10, 5:14, 6:1, 6:3, 6:5, 6:7, 6:17, 7:17, 8:13, 10:11, 11:4, 11:8, 11:15, 11:17, 12:12, 12:18 with 13:1, 13:18, 14:1, 14:5, 14:8, 14:13, 15:2, 15:3, 16:5, 16:14, 17:4, 17:8, 17:16, 18:13, 18:14, 19:1, 19:13, 19:17, 20:5, 20:9, 20:12, 20:14, 21:2, 21:3, 21:6, 21:7, 21:24, 22:5, 22:6, 22:11, 22:14, 22:19, 22:21.
- The other differences in the scan (spelling forms, article and particle differences, verb tense and number, order) were read in the scan output only and are not claimed as compared in detail.
