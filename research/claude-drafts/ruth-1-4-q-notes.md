# Ruth 1–4 — Q records, notes

Session 2026-10-13. Q-only backlog session: H, G, V and untagged conflicts were not re-opened. File: `ruth-1-4-q-records.json` (3 records). All are drafts until Codex accepts them. All take `mode: "questions"`, `tags: ["Q"]`, `status: "Context"` and carry `sides`.

## Records

1. `naomi-selleth-elimelechs-land-how-was-it-hers-to-sell` (4:3) — Naomi “selleth” land that was Elimelech’s, and Boaz buys it “of the hand of Naomi” (4:9). The order of inheritance in Numbers 27:8–11 names son, daughter, brethren, father’s brethren and nearest kinsman. How the land came into Naomi’s hand, and what she was selling, is not said.
2. `redeeming-the-field-and-raising-the-name-of-the-dead-what-joined-them` (4:5) — the nearer kinsman will redeem the field (4:4) and withdraws when Ruth and “the name of the dead” are joined to it (4:5–6). Leviticus 25:25 speaks of land; Deuteronomy 25:5–7 speaks of a husband’s brother. What joined the two duties in a kinsman not called the dead man’s brother is not said.
3. `obed-born-to-raise-up-the-name-of-the-dead-in-whose-line-was-he-counted` (4:17) — the marriage is “to raise up the name of the dead” (4:5, 4:10); the women say “There is a son born to Naomi”; the list says “Boaz begat Obed” (4:21), as do 1 Chronicles 2:12, Matthew 1:5 and Luke 3:32. How the dead man’s name was raised, and in whose line the child was counted, is not said.
