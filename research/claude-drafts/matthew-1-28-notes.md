# Matthew — notes (2026-10-14)

Unit: Matthew 1–28 (28 chapters). **All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.**

Counts: 42 records in `matthew-1-28-records.json`: 27 T (all witness "Greek New Testament") and 15 untagged; no V; every record carries `sides`. 3 Q in `matthew-1-28-q-records.json` (separate notes file).

## Editions and numbering

- Greek New Testament text is the Textus Receptus (TR) and Tischendorf eighth edition (TISCH) as served by bolls.life book 40 (`https://bolls.life/get-text/TR/40/{chapter}/`, `…/TISCH/40/{chapter}/`). The verse numbers of both agree with the KJV; no offset. The TR text is served without accents; the quotations from it are given as served.
- The KJV follows the TR in the verses of the “edition” records below, so those records compare the KJV with Tischendorf’s text; each says that the TR text served agrees with the KJV and that the record does not say which reading is original.
- The Tischendorf page served prints the verse number with **no text** at 17:21, 18:11, 21:44 and 23:14. Three of the four are issued (17:21, 18:11, 23:14); each record states that only the one served page prints no text and does not claim anything about other editions or manuscripts. This is the same pattern as the Septuagint omissions the owner answered (the record says which page, and which verse numbers it prints).
- Not used: SBLGNT. The bolls.life SBLGNT text is served with morphology codes attached to each word and was used only as a cross-check while screening.

Notes on particular T records:
- 8:28 (Gergesenes / Gadarenes): two different place-names, not a spelling variant. The KJV’s own Mark 5:1 and Luke 8:26 print “Gadarenes”; the record does not say whether the names denote one place.
- 11:23 and the “hell” record share Matthew 11:23 on purpose: the first is about the clause “which art exalted / shall you be exalted”, the second about the Greek words behind “hell”. Codex may fold them.
- 13:35 (Isaiah): the packaged KJV of Isaiah contains no verse with “open my mouth” (script check, whole book). The record does not say who wrote Psalm 78.
- 19:9: the record sits beside the published `coverage-nt-divorce` (Matthew 19:9, Mark 10:11); the scope says it concerns only the wording the Greek editions print.
- 24:36 (“nor the Son”): the KJV of Mark 13:32 is side B. No doctrinal conclusion is drawn.
- “Strain at a gnat”: the gloss “strain out” is the researcher’s plain rendering of the participle with the prefix for “through”; the record says so and does not discuss what the 1611 phrase meant.
- “Hell” (Gehenna / Hades) and “world” (kosmos / aion) are accepted-by-pattern cases of one English word standing for two Greek words in the same Gospel. They are not the Judges 15:19 pattern (one Hebrew phrase rendered two ways).

## Verses compared

All 1,071 verses of Matthew were compared by script between the TR and Tischendorf texts (every verse where the word lists differ was listed and read; spelling-only, name-spelling, verb-form and word-order differences were not issued). Where a difference touched meaning, the KJV was read against both. KJV-against-Greek translation differences where **both** editions agree were screened by reading, not by script, and only the five issued were found clearly meaning-different; this is not an exhaustive word-by-word translation audit of the 28 chapters. The Old Testament quotations in Matthew were not compared verse by verse in this session (see “Not examined”).
