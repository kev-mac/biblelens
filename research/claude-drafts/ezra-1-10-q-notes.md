## Records

1. `ahasuerus-and-artaxerxes-between-cyrus-and-darius-which-kings-are-meant` (Ezra 4:6, 4:7) — the chapter names two kings between Cyrus and Darius in the order of telling and does not say who they are or whether the letters about the city come before Darius. The answer decides whether the letters caused the stoppage of 4:24.
2. `strange-wives-and-their-children-what-became-of-them` (Ezra 10:44) — the proposal covers wives and children; the verses report assent, an inquiry, a pledge by the priests of verse 18 and a list, but do not describe the sending away or say what became of the women and children.
3. `turned-the-heart-of-the-king-of-assyria-which-king` (Ezra 6:22) — the king whose change of heart is given as the cause of the joy is not named and bears a title the account does not use for Darius.
