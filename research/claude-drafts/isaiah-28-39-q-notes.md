## Considered and not issued

- 29:1–2 (what "Ariel" is): 29:8 speaks of the fight against "mount Zion" in the same passage; the gap is partly answered in the chapter.
- 37:36 (the angel and the 185,000): the verse states the event and the actor; how it happened is a detail the passage does not claim to give.
- 38:8 (the shadow on the dial of Ahaz): a matter of mechanism that the text reports as a sign; not a gap in explanation the text sets up.
- 38:1–6 (the sickness and the promise to deliver "out of the hand of the king of Assyria" after chapter 37): the order of chapters 36–39 against "In those days" (38:1) is loose; not a gap material to a reading.
- 33:14–16 (who shall dwell with the devouring fire): answered in 33:15–16.
- 34:16 ("the book of the Lord"): which book is meant is a detail; no consequence for the passage is stated.
- 36:10 (Rabshakeh: "the Lord said unto me, Go up against this land"): a speaker's claim; the text does not ask a question the record could frame.
- 39:7 (the eunuchs in Babylon): a prediction; no gap.
