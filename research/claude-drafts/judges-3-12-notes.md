## Considered and ruled out

- Balak (11:25): issued in the Joshua file as `balak-war-against-israel`.
- Gideon's fleece (6:39 against Deuteronomy 6:16): held for the owner's ruling, README question 1. The KJV's "tempt" and "prove" are synonyms, so no H either.
- Jephthah's vow (11:31, 39): held for the owner's ruling, README question 2. The Hebrew is as silent on the act as the KJV.
- Judges 3:30, Greek adds "Ehud judged them until he died": extra text, no contrast.
- Judges 4:11 as H: the Hebrew in-law word normally means father-in-law; the KJV is within its range.
- Judges 7:3 Mount Gilead; 11:24 Chemosh: no variant in Hebrew or Greek; Chemosh is already published.
- Judges' years against the 480 of 1 Kings 6:1; Havoth-jair town totals; 42,000 Ephraimites: need assumptions about overlap or date that the text does not state.
- Jabin and Hazor, iron chariots, camels, Shechem's temple, the Dead Sea Scroll at 6:7–10: no primary publication opened.
- Prose and song participant lists (Judges 4–5): lists not stated to be exclusive.

## Owner ruling, 2026-10-12

Neither Gideon's fleece nor Jephthah's vow is a V case (a shared verb is not enough; an undescribed act is not "clearly broken"). Both stay out of this file.
