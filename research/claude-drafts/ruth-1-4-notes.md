Researcher: Claude · 2026-10-12 · **All seven records are drafts until Codex accepts them.** The `status` field is the intended published value. Nothing outside `research/claude-drafts/` was changed.

Every Hebrew, Greek and KJV quotation was copied by script from the fetched page or `data/chapters.json` and checked as an exact run of characters in the stated verse (Greek after removing page markup and collapsing spaces). All 85 verses were fetched in WLC, HAC and Brenton Greek; I compared the verses listed below, not every verse line by line, so a verse not mentioned here has not been cleared.

The only published record touching Ruth is `early-ruth-shoe` (4:7–10, history mode). None of the new records overlaps it, so all seven are new IDs.

## Considered and ruled out

- Deuteronomy 23:3 (no Moabite in the congregation "to their tenth generation") against Ruth 4:17–22 (David, third generation from Ruth): a law case that fails V — no act of "entering" is narrated and the story blesses the marriage. Dropped, not issued untagged.
- Mahlon, Chilion and Boaz marrying Moabite women: Deuteronomy 7 names seven peoples, not Moab. Not clearly broken.
- Boaz and the shoe (4:7–8) against Deuteronomy 25:5–10: Boaz is not a brother of the dead man; a different case. Already covered as custom by `early-ruth-shoe`.
- Elimelech leaving the land in famine (1:1): no law.
- Nahshon to David in five "begats" (4:20–22) against the 480 years of 1 Kings 6:1: needs the list to be complete, which it does not say.
- "Salmon" in KJV 4:20 where the Hebrew spells Salmah, then Salmon in 4:21: spelling.
- 4:5 written and read forms of the verb ("I acquire" / "thou acquirest"): a scribal marking, not H. Quoted in the 4:5 record only for completeness.
- 4:3 Hebrew "sold" against KJV "selleth": the Hebrew can carry the KJV's sense.
- 2:1 "kinsman" against Greek "acquaintance": the verse itself goes on to state the kinship.
- 1:20–21 "the Almighty" against Greek ὁ ἱκανός: a title rendered two ways, no shown difference in meaning.
- 4:1 "such a one" against Greek κρύφιε: same sense.
- 1:13 "for them" against a possible "therefore": Hebrew unresolved.
- Smaller Septuagint differences not issued: 3:16 lacks "Who art thou"; 4:7 and 4:8 add that the shoe was given to the redeemer; 4:11 splits the speech between people and elders; 2:14 names Boaz and Ruth. See the question below.
- Ten years in Moab; six measures of barley; harvest seasons: no contrast and no primary source opened.
