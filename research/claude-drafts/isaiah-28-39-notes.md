# Isaiah 28–39 — notes (2026-10-13)

All records are drafts until Codex accepts them. 16 records, all tag T, all witness "Septuagint"; no V, no untagged conflict. Every record carries `sides` (one has a side B on a New Testament verse: Romans 9:33). The Q records are in `isaiah-28-39-q-records.json` (see `isaiah-28-39-q-notes.md`).

## Verse numbers

- In chapters 28–39 the KJV, the Hebrew (WLC) and Brenton's Greek carry the same verse numbers for every verse quoted. Brenton's Greek pages are ISA28–ISA39; bolls.life book 23; packaged KJV index 22.
- Brenton's Greek of Isaiah is a free rendering; many verses differ in wording without a difference in meaning. Only verses where a plainly different word, name or statement is printed were issued.

## Method and what was compared

- Every KJV verse of 28–39 was scored against the same-numbered and the best-matching verse of Brenton's English (a word-overlap scan, threshold 0.3); 115 verses fell below it and were read in the three texts. Verses above the threshold were not read one by one. This is a screen, not a reading of every chapter in full.
- For each issued record the KJV, the WLC words and Brenton's Greek and English were asserted by script at the verse.
- Isaiah 36–39 repeats 2 Kings 18–20. These chapters were **not** compared with 2 Kings 18–20 verse by verse (the README said the same of 36–38). Isaiah 39:2 is already published (`hezekiah-hearkened-to-the-envoys-or-was-glad-of-them`); 36:1–3 appear in the built-in study `exp-late-22-36-1`; neither was re-issued.

## Considered and not issued

- 36:7 (Hezekiah "hath taken away" the high places and altars): Brenton's Greek of the verse stops at "We trust in the Lord our God" and prints nothing of it. An omission, not a different word; left out to keep to printed differences. Quotes sourced if Codex wants to ask.
- 29:1 "let them kill sacrifices" / "ye shall eat with Moab": real difference; left out so the record stays on one point (the "dwelt" / "besieged" record covers 29:1).
- 28:26, 28:17, 30:10, 31:2, 32:5, 33:20, 36:9, 38:17, 38:19: differences of wording or sense that are close to rewordings, or whose weight rests on a Hebrew word's range of senses.
- 28:25, 28:27 (plant names), 34:11, 34:13, 34:15 (bird and animal names), 30:4 (place-names Zoan/Hanes and Tanes), 35:1 ("rose" / "lily"): names of species and places that differ without a shown difference in meaning; the Hebrew words have ranges of senses.
- 38:12, 38:15–16, 38:20: the Greek differs, but the Hebrew of the Hezekiah psalm is hard and the difference is of sense rather than of a plain word.
- 36:1–37:38 against 2 Kings 18–19: not compared (see above).
- V: 30:1–2 and 31:1 (going down to Egypt for help; compare Deuteronomy 17:16) — the passage itself says "Woe" (30:1, 31:1), so the act is checked in the passage. No V.
- Untagged conflicts: none found within 28–39. The order of the sickness (38) after the Assyrian defeat (37) against "In those days" (38:1) was read and not issued; it is loose, not a contradiction.
