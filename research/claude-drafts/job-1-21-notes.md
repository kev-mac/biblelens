# Job 1–21 — records, notes

File: `job-1-21-records.json` (12 records: 12 G, no H, no V, no untagged; all carry `sides`). All are drafts until Codex accepts them. Written 2026-10-13. The Q records are in `job-1-21-q-records.json`, with their own notes.

Job has 42 chapters and is done in two parts: 1–21 (the prologue and the first two cycles of speeches) and 22–42. This batch is part 1 only.

## Verse numbers

- In chapters 1–21 the Hebrew edition (WLC) and Brenton’s Greek print every KJV verse number; there is no offset.
- Brenton’s Greek prints lettered verses `9a`–`9e` after 2:9 and `4a` after 19:4. Find verses by the printed label. The script asserts the label sequence of chapter 2 (`1 … 9 9a 9b 9c 9d 9e 10 … 13`).
- One Hebrew quotation is from part 2: **KJV Job 41:1 is WLC 40:25**. The record on 3:8 quotes it from `https://bolls.life/get-text/WLC/18/40/` and says so.
- Brenton’s Greek Job is at `https://ebible.org/grcbrent/JOB{cc}.htm`. All 36 source addresses answered.

## Why each qualifies

- `sons-of-god-before-the-lord-or-angels-of-god-greek` (1:6, 2:1) — G: the Greek says “the angels of God” where the KJV and the Hebrew say “the sons of God”.
- `he-will-curse-thee-to-thy-face-or-bless-thee-greek` (1:11, 2:5) — G: the Greek prints the verb of blessing where the KJV has “curse”.
- `jobs-wife-curse-god-and-die-greek-has-a-longer-speech` (2:9) — G: five added lines in the Greek say things the KJV of 2:7–10 does not (time passed, her own condition).
- `jobs-three-friends-temanite-shuhite-naamathite-or-kings-greek` (2:11) — G: the Greek makes the three friends rulers.
- `sabeans-and-chaldeans-or-spoilers-and-horsemen-greek` (1:15, 1:17) — G: the Greek names neither people.
- `job-offered-burnt-offerings-greek-adds-one-calf-for-a-sin-offering` (1:5) — G: an added offering.
- `thou-movedst-me-against-him-or-thou-hast-said-to-destroy-his-substance-greek` (2:3) — G: who is acted on, and what is to be destroyed, differ.
- `ready-to-raise-up-their-mourning-or-the-great-sea-monster-greek` (3:8) — G: a creature in the Greek where the KJV has “their mourning”.
- `taste-in-the-white-of-an-egg-or-in-empty-words-greek` (6:6) — G: speech in the Greek where the KJV has a food.
- `though-he-slay-me-yet-will-i-trust-in-him-greek-has-no-trust` (13:15) — G: no statement of trust in the Greek.
- `children-of-mine-own-body-or-sons-of-my-concubines-greek` (19:17) — G: a different group in the Greek.
- `in-my-flesh-shall-i-see-god-or-to-raise-up-my-skin-greek` (19:25, 19:26) — G: no standing on the earth, no worms, and no seeing of God in the Greek.

- Absence claims are limited to the verses quoted. Three word searches of the packaged KJV are asserted by script and named as word searches: no “concubine” in Job; no “king” in Job chapter 2; the label checks above.

## Considered and not issued

- 14:14, “shall he live again?”: Brenton’s Greek can be read as the same question (his English prints it as one).
- 7:9 and 14:12 beside verses on rising from the grave: 1 Samuel 2:6 is in a book already finished; a later-book verse would be set against a speech in verse, and the published `exp-late-17-42-7` says the book corrects its speakers.
- “Perfect and upright” (1:1) beside Romans 3:23: not a direct wording contrast.
- V: no breach of a law is told in these chapters; Job’s own sacrifices are not said to be under the Law.
- “Satan” for the Hebrew word with the article: no dictionary source; stated in the Q record as a fact about the spelling only.
- 5:19 (six troubles, “in the seventh”): the same figure differently worded.
