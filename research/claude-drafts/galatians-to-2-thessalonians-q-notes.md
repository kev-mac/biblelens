# Galatians to 2 Thessalonians — Q notes (2026-10-14)

All drafts until Codex accepts them. 3 Q records in `galatians-to-2-thessalonians-q-records.json`, each with a side A on the key verse:

- `what-withholdeth-and-who-now-letteth-2-thessalonians-2-6-7` — What is it that “withholdeth”, and who is “he who now letteth” (2 Thessalonians 2:6–7)?
- `which-is-the-epistle-from-laodicea-colossians-4-16` — Which letter is “the epistle from Laodicea” (Colossians 4:16)?
- `ordained-by-angels-in-the-hand-of-a-mediator-galatians-3-19-20` — Who is “the mediator”, and what is the role of “angels” (Galatians 3:19–20)?

Each follows the Q contract: the passage leaves the point open, readings are named only as readings, and none is chosen. Absence claims are bounded to the verses quoted. The Greek quoted is Tischendorf (eighth edition) as served by bolls.life, checked against the Textus Receptus text served there.

Considered and left out: Ephesians 4:8–9 (“led captivity captive”, “the lower parts of the earth”: the Psalm 68:18 comparison is already published as a T record and the lower parts are a matter of reading, not an unstated fact); Colossians 1:24 (“that which is behind of the afflictions of Christ”: the passage states what Paul does and why); Philippians 4:3 (“true yokefellow”: covered by the published record on the book of life); 1 Thessalonians 5:23 (body, soul and spirit: a list the passage gives, not an unanswered question); Galatians 6:11 (“how large a letter”: ordinary detail).
