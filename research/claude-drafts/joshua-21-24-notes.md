## Considered and ruled out

- Joshua 24:26 "sanctuary" as an unauthorised altar: no altar is mentioned. Folded into the Shechem/Shiloh record as wording only.
- Joshua 24:32 "pieces of silver" against Genesis 33:19 "pieces of money": same Hebrew unit; English synonyms.
- Greek additions after Joshua 24:30 and 24:33 (stone knives; Eglon): extra text with nothing in the KJV to contrast.
- Joshua 24:30 Timnath-serah against Judges 2:9 Timnath-heres: spelling.
- Joshua 24:11 "the men of Jericho fought": resisting a siege can count as fighting.
- Joshua 24:19, 24:27, 22:10–11: agreed with Codex's deferrals.
- Date of the Levite town list; which Greek New Testament manuscripts carry each reading at Acts 7:16: no primary publication opened.
- H tag for Genesis 48:22 "portion": the Hebrew word can mean a share, so the KJV is within its range.
