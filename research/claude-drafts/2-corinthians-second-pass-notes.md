# 2 Corinthians second pass (workstream D10), 2026-10-18

Scope: the 5 chapters with no published record: 2, 3, 7, 9 and 10. Method: Textus Receptus and Tischendorf (8th ed.) texts fetched from bolls.life (book 47) by script; a script compared them word by word (accents, case and final-sigma stripped) for every verse of the five chapters, and the verses that differ were then read against the KJV. The KJV was read at those verses only, not in full. This matches the method of the earlier 2 Corinthians batch. All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## Issued (6 records, `2-corinthians-second-pass-records.json`), all T, witness Greek New Testament, status Context
1. `which-vail-is-done-away-in-christ-or-because-it-is-done-away-in-christ-2-corinthians-3-14-greek-new-testament` (3:14): ο τι against ὅτι, and "this very day".
2. `our-care-for-you-or-your-care-for-us-2-corinthians-7-12-greek-new-testament` (7:12): pronouns swapped.
3. `we-were-comforted-in-your-comfort-or-in-our-comfort-2-corinthians-7-13-greek-new-testament` (7:13): "your comfort" against "our comfort".
4. `whereof-ye-had-notice-before-or-promised-before-2-corinthians-9-5-greek-new-testament` (9:5): announced beforehand against promised beforehand.
5. `minister-bread-multiply-and-increase-or-will-supply-multiply-and-increase-2-corinthians-9-10-greek-new-testament` (9:10): wish forms against future forms.
6. `our-authority-which-the-lord-hath-given-us-or-which-the-lord-gave-2-corinthians-10-8-greek-new-testament` (10:8): "us" and the verb mood.

## Considered, not issued
- 3:9 ("be glory" / τῇ διακονίᾳ dative, and "exceed in glory" / δόξῃ without ἐν): the KJV supplies "be" in italics; the sense is close.
- 3:17 ("there is liberty": TR has ἐκεῖ, Tischendorf has none): one adverb, same sense.
- 9:4 (TR ends "of confidence/boasting" τῆς καυχήσεως; Tischendorf stops at "this confidence"): an omission, same sense.
- Spelling or form only: 2:5 ἀλλ/ἀλλά, 2:17 εἰλικρινείας/εἰλικρινίας, 3:2–3 ἐγγεγραμμένη/ἐνγεγραμμένη, 3:6 ἀποκτείνει/ἀποκτέννει, 3:7/3:13/3:15 Μωσῆς/Μωϋσῆς, 7:3 συζῆν, 7:12 εἵνεκεν/ἕνεκεν, 10:1 πραότητος/πραΰτητος, 10:4 στρατείας/στρατιάς, 10:12 ἐγκρίναι/ἐνκρίναι, 10:13 οὐχί/οὐκ.
