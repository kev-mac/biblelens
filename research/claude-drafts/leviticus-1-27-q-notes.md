## Records

1. `the-other-lot-for-the-scapegoat-for-what-or-for-whom` (Leviticus 16:8, 16:10, 16:26) — the second lot is “for the scapegoat” in the KJV and “for azazel” in the Hebrew, set beside “for the LORD”; the chapter never says what the word is. The whole rite of the live goat depends on it.
2. `the-morrow-after-the-sabbath-which-sabbath` (Leviticus 23:11, 23:15, 23:16) — the sheaf and the feast fifty days later are dated from “the sabbath”, and the passage does not say which. Every other feast in the chapter has a day of the month.
3. `the-jubile-fiftieth-year-how-does-it-stand-in-the-count-of-sevens` (Leviticus 25:10, 25:11) — forty-nine years are counted and the fiftieth is hallowed; the chapter does not say whether the fiftieth stands outside the sevens or begins the next one.
