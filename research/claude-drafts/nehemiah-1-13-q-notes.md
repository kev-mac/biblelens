# Nehemiah 1–13 — Q notes

2026-10-13. Three Q records in `nehemiah-1-13-q-records.json`. All are drafts until Codex accepts them. Mode `questions`, tag Q, status Context.

## Verse numbers (for checking quotations)

- Brenton’s Greek has no Nehemiah pages. Nehemiah chapter *c* is printed at `https://ebible.org/grcbrent/EZR{c+10}.htm` (Nehemiah 1 = `EZR11.htm`, 2 = `EZR12.htm`, 10 = `EZR20.htm`). The records cite those pages.
- KJV Nehemiah 10:34 is 10:35 in the Hebrew (WLC) and in Brenton’s Greek. The record quotes them at 10:35 and says so.
- No difference at 1:1, 1:3, 2:1 or 13:31.

## Records

1. `chisleu-and-nisan-both-in-the-twentieth-year-how-is-the-year-counted` (1:1, 2:1) — Chisleu and the following Nisan are both put in a twentieth year, and 1:1 names no king. The verses do not say how the year is counted. The month numbers come from Zechariah 7:1 and Esther 3:7. Sequence and reckoning unstated; it decides the length of the wait and the start of the twelve years of 5:14.
2. `wall-broken-down-and-gates-burned-which-event-is-reported-to-nehemiah` (1:3) — the report names no time and no agent. 2 Kings 25:10 and Ezra 4:12, 4:23 are quoted as the two events a reader might connect it with; neither is said to be the one meant.
3. `wood-offering-as-it-is-written-in-the-law-where-is-it-written` (10:34) — the verse ends “as it is written in the law” and does not say whether that covers the burning on the altar or the wood offering itself. Leviticus 6:12 and 1:7 are quoted for what they do say.

## Considered and not issued

- Shemaiah “who was shut up” (6:10): the reason is not given, but nothing turns on it.
- How long Nehemiah was away (“after certain days”, 13:6): ordinary missing detail.
- Ezra’s whereabouts between his seventh year and Nehemiah’s twentieth (8:1–9): a gap only if both books mean the same Artaxerxes, which the published record already treats as open.
- “The book of the chronicles” (12:23): a pointer to a source; similar questions are already published for other books.
- Which Darius is “Darius the Persian” (12:22): close to the published question on the kings of Ezra 4.
- The branches of 8:15 beside Leviticus 23:40: the lists differ and the verse says “as it is written”; left out because the two verses may describe different acts (taking branches, making booths).
