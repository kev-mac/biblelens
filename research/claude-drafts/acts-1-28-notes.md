# Acts 1–28 — notes (2026-10-14)

`acts-1-28-records.json`: 66 records, all drafts until Codex accepts them. 57 carry `tags: ["T"]` with witness `"Greek New Testament"`; 9 are untagged. No V. Every record carries `sides`. Every KJV quotation was asserted against the packaged KJV, every Greek quotation against the fetched page, and every source address was checked (95 addresses, none failed). The Q records are in `acts-1-28-q-records.json` with `acts-1-28-q-notes.md`.

**Method.** The Textus Receptus text (TR, as served by bolls.life without accents) and Tischendorf’s eighth edition (TISCH) were fetched for all 28 chapters, and every verse whose word lists differ between them was listed (about 650 lines). The KJV follows the TR text in every T record; each record states what each edition prints and says nothing about which reading is original. Only differences of meaning were issued: a different word, name or number, a clause or word present in one and absent from the other, and verses the TISCH page prints with no text. Verses compared: all of Acts 1–28 between the two editions by script; the KJV was read only at the verses quoted below and at the verses listed under “considered and left out”.
