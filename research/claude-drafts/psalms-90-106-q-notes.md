## Considered and not issued

- **Psalm 95:11, “my rest”**: Hebrews 3–4 gives its own application, so the question would have to say what that does and does not settle; left out to keep the batch small.
- **Psalm 91 (who speaks and who is addressed at 91:2, 91:9, 91:14)**: the shifts of speaker are real but the psalm’s subject is not in doubt; left out.
- **Psalm 102:24–27 (to whom is it addressed)**: the psalm states the addressee (“O my God”); Hebrews 1:10 applies it, but there is no gap in the psalm itself.
- **Psalm 106:28 (“the sacrifices of the dead”)**: the phrase is unexplained, but it is a short phrase and Numbers 25:2 speaks of sacrifices of their gods; left out as too slight.
- **Psalm 106:33 (what Moses said)**: answered at Numbers 20:10, so not a gap.
- **Psalm 90:1 (title “A Prayer of Moses”)**: the title is outside the packaged KJV; not used.
- **Psalm 105 plagues order (darkness first, 105:28)**: a poetic order, not a stated sequence; not issued.
