# Job second pass (workstream D), 2026-10-18

First-time reading of the 28 Job chapters that had no published record: 4, 5, 7–12, 14–18, 20–25, 27, 28, 30, 31, 34–37, 39. The KJV of each chapter was read in full, with Brenton's English beside it for chapters 4–9 (the poetry of the first two cycles). The Hebrew (WLC via bolls.life) and Brenton's Greek (ebible.org) were fetched by script for all 28 chapters and compared at the verses named below; they were **not** read in full. Ledger entries say so.

Scope notes the KJV prints the Hebrew רָהַב as "Rahab" at Psalm 89:10 and Isaiah 51:9 and "the proud" at 26:12 (all asserted from the packaged KJV and WLC); it does not call "proud" wrong. Sibling of the published 26:12–13 Greek record.
- `a-brother-to-dragons-...-job-30-29` T, Septuagint: "dragons"/"owls" against "sirens"/"ostriches". The KJV margin already reads "or, ostriches"; stated in the record.
- `if-i-covered-my-transgressions-as-adam-...-job-31-33` T, Septuagint: "as Adam" against "having sinned unintentionally". KJV margin: "after the manner of men". The record does not say which reading of the Hebrew is right.
- `he-will-not-afflict-or-dost-thou-not-think-that-he-listens-greek-job-37-23` T, Septuagint, last clause only. Scope mentions 42:11 as a reader's possible comparison but draws none.
- `the-wicked-prosper-and-the-wicked-perish-job-21-and-27` untagged, status Context: Job in chapter 21 (wicked live long, no rod of God on their houses) against Job in chapter 27 (the wicked man's portion is the sword, children hungry). Limits stated: 21:17 already qualifies chapter 21; chapter 27 may answer the friends' view; no claim of self-contradiction. KJV only.
