## Records

1. `the-king-of-sheshach-shall-drink-after-them-who-or-what-is-sheshach-jeremiah-25-26` — who or what Sheshach is (25:26, with 51:41). Check made: a whole-word, case-sensitive search of the packaged KJV for “Sheshach” returns these two verses only.
2. `neither-shall-it-come-to-mind-what-is-to-become-of-the-ark-jeremiah-3-16` — what the verse says about the ark of the covenant. 2 Chronicles 35:3 is a context ref only, beside the published `put-the-holy-ark-in-the-house-where-had-the-ark-been` (a different question, at a different verse); the tagged verse is Jeremiah 3:16.
3. `when-seventy-years-be-accomplished-from-what-event-are-the-seventy-years-counted-jeremiah-25-11-12` — the starting event of the seventy years (25:11–12, 29:10; 2 Chronicles 36:21 as context). The record does not compare the Hebrew or the Greek.

## Considered and left out

- The “righteous Branch” (23:5–6): a reading of who it names depends on how later books use the word; not issued in this unit.
- Aramaic at 10:11 (the verse is in Aramaic in the Hebrew): the KJV gives no sign of it, so there is no KJV gap to question.
- The journey to the Euphrates (13:4–7) and the potter’s house (18): the passages say what is done; no consequential unstated identity or cause.
- Jeremiah 7:12–14 (Shiloh) and 24:1 (Jeconiah’s captives): already published Q records.
