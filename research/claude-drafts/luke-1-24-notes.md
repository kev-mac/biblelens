# Luke — notes (2026-10-14)

Unit: Luke 1–24 (24 chapters, 1,151 verses). **All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.**

Counts: 47 records in `luke-1-24-records.json`: 40 T (all witness "Greek New Testament") and 7 untagged; no V; every record carries `sides`. 3 Q in `luke-1-24-q-records.json` (separate notes file).

## Editions and numbering

- Greek New Testament text is the Textus Receptus (TR) and Tischendorf eighth edition (TISCH) as served by bolls.life book 42 (`https://bolls.life/get-text/TR/42/{chapter}/`, `…/TISCH/42/{chapter}/`). Verse numbers agree with the KJV except at Luke 4:18–19, where the Tischendorf page places the clauses about the captives and the blind in 4:19 (the KJV has them in 4:18); the record says so. TR is served without accents.
- The KJV follows the TR in the edition records, so each compares the KJV with Tischendorf’s text; each says the TR text served agrees with the KJV and that the record does not say which reading is original.
- The Tischendorf page served prints the verse number with **no text** at Luke 17:36, 23:17, 24:12 and 24:40. All four are issued, each stating only what the one served page prints (the Proverbs 22:6 and Matthew 17:21 pattern). If Codex holds such records under “Needs owner”, they stay held.

## Verses compared

All 1,151 verses of Luke were compared by script between the TR and Tischendorf texts (every verse where the word lists differ, about 680 lines, was listed and read; spelling, name spelling, verb-ending, tense, word order, εὐθέως / εὐθύς, γεννήσει / γενέσει-type spelling differences and Moses / Elijah / David / Jerusalem forms were not issued). Where a difference touched meaning it was issued or listed below. The Luke 3:23–38 genealogy was compared name by name: the differences are name spellings, plus Jesus (3:29) for Jose and Admin and Arni (3:33) for Aram; these were not issued as a count difference (the KJV list and the editions are both of long chains).
