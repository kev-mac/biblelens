# Job 1–21 — Q records, notes

File: `job-1-21-q-records.json` (3 records, all `mode: "questions"`, `tags: ["Q"]`, `status: "Context"`). All are drafts until Codex accepts them. Written 2026-10-13. Job is done in two parts; this is part 1 (chapters 1–21). Part 2 (22–42) follows in a later session.

## Why each qualifies

- `satan-came-also-among-the-sons-of-god-what-is-his-standing` (1:6, 2:1) — an unstated identity: Satan comes with the sons of God, is questioned and is given leave, and the verses do not say whether he is one of them or by what standing he comes. It decides who is acting against Job.
- `a-man-in-the-land-of-uz-where-and-when-did-job-live` (1:1) — an unstated place and time: no king, year, father or people is given for the man the whole book is about.
- `i-know-that-my-redeemer-liveth-who-is-the-redeemer` (19:25) — an unstated identity in the book’s best-known sentence: the redeemer is not named, and the chapter has God both as the one who struck Job and as the one Job will see.

## Considered and not issued

- Who the sons of God are (1:6): already asked in the published Genesis record, which cites these verses.
- Why Job is never told of the scene in heaven: a question about the whole book whose answer, if any, is in part 2.
- Whether Job expects to live again (14:14 beside 19:26): a tension between two speeches in verse by one speaker, not a gap in a passage.
- The “daysman” (9:33): Brenton’s Greek words the verse as a wish; a wording difference, held back, not a Q.
- “Huz” (Genesis 22:21): another spelling in the KJV; not shown to be the same name, so not used.
