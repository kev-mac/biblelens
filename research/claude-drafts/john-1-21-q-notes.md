# John — Q notes (2026-10-14)

All drafts until Codex accepts them. 3 Q records in `john-1-21-q-records.json`, each with a side A on the key verse:

- `the-disciple-whom-jesus-loved-who-is-he-john-13-23` — “one of his disciples, whom Jesus loved” — who is he?
- `what-jesus-wrote-on-the-ground-john-8-6-john-8-8` — Jesus “wrote on the ground” — what did he write?
- `a-feast-of-the-jews-which-feast-john-5-1` — “a feast of the Jews” — which feast?

Each follows the Q contract: the passage leaves the point open, readings are named only as readings, and none is chosen. Absence claims are bounded to the verses quoted. The Greek quoted is Tischendorf (eighth edition) as served by bolls.life, checked against the Textus Receptus text served there.

Considered and left out: John 1:48 (the fig tree: what Jesus saw is not asked by the passage’s own question); 18:15–16 (“another disciple”, known to the high priest: ordinary omitted detail, and it depends on the identity question already issued); 21:22–23 (“If I will that he tarry till I come”: the next verse states how the saying was taken); 3:8 (the wind: no gap, a comparison); 4:54 (the second miracle: counted by the verse itself); 19:35 (“he that saw it”: the same identity question as 13:23, not issued twice).
