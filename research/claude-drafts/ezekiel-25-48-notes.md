# Ezekiel 25–48 — notes (2026-10-13)

All records are drafts until Codex accepts them. File: `ezekiel-25-48-records.json` (29 records: 25 T, 24 witness “Septuagint” and 1 witness “Hebrew and Septuagint”; 4 untagged; no V). Q is in `ezekiel-25-48-q-records.json` and `ezekiel-25-48-q-notes.md`. This finishes Ezekiel (plan of parts 1–24, 25–48). Next: Daniel to Hosea.

Every KJV quotation, Hebrew quotation (WLC, bolls.life book 26) and Greek quotation (Brenton, `grcbrent/EZK{nn}.htm`) was asserted by script against the packaged KJV or the fetched page; all 51 source addresses answered. Brenton’s own English (`eng-Brenton/EZK{nn}.htm`) is quoted beside each Greek quotation and nowhere replaced by a researcher rendering. Sides were validated with `scripts/verse-roles.mjs`. No record shares a reference with any published or drafted record.

## Verse numbers

- KJV, WLC and Brenton agree at every verse quoted. Brenton pages EZK25–EZK48 all answer.
- Brenton has no counterpart of 27:31, 32:19, 33:26 or 40:30 (not issued).
- **Brenton’s Greek and Brenton’s English differ on the same page in two places:** 32:1 (Greek prints “δεκάτῳ ἔτει”, “tenth year”; English prints “twelfth year”) and 38:3 (the Greek page has no name; the English page prints “Rhos prince of Mesoch and Thobel”). The T records quote the Greek and avoid the point at issue: the 32:1 record compares the month only (both pages say tenth), and the Rhos record uses 38:2 and 39:1, where the Greek page prints the name.
- The Greek of 40–48 differs from the Hebrew in order and detail much more than elsewhere in the book (the temple vision); only plain number, name and noun differences are issued.

## T records (witness “Septuagint” except one)

Each compares what the KJV, the Hebrew and Brenton’s Greek print. The Hebrew is quoted for completeness; the finding is the Greek differing from the KJV.

1. `moab-and-seir-the-house-of-judah-or-moab-the-house-of-israel-and-juda-ezekiel-25-8-greek` — 25:8 names Moab and Seir, and Judah; the Greek names Moab, and Israel and Juda. The question form in Brenton’s English is stated as not the finding.
2. `tarshish-was-thy-merchant-or-the-carthaginians-were-thy-merchants-ezekiel-27-12-greek` — 27:12 and 38:13. Same pattern as the accepted Isaiah record on Tarshish and Carthage; the scope says the record does not claim the same place.
3. `the-men-of-dedan-or-the-sons-of-the-rhodians-ezekiel-27-15-greek` — 27:15; 27:20 is context, where the Greek prints a form of Dedan.
4. `ethiopia-libya-lydia-and-chub-or-persians-cretans-lydians-and-libyans-ezekiel-30-5-greek` — 30:5; names the lists and says which items stand in both.
5. `these-cities-shall-go-into-captivity-or-the-women-shall-go-into-captivity-ezekiel-30-17-greek` — 30:17; “these cities” against “the women”, with the place names in the same verse.
6. `the-tenth-year-the-twelfth-day-or-the-twelfth-year-the-first-day-ezekiel-29-1-greek` — 29:1; year and day.
7. `in-the-twelfth-month-or-in-the-tenth-month-ezekiel-32-1-greek` — 32:1; month only (see the verse-number note).
8. `in-the-fifteenth-day-of-the-month-or-in-the-first-month-on-the-fifteenth-day-ezekiel-32-17-greek` — 32:17; the Greek adds a month. Neither the KJV nor the Hebrew names one.
9. `eden-the-garden-of-god-or-the-delight-of-the-paradise-of-god-ezekiel-28-13-greek` — 28:13 and 36:35; the Greek prints a noun (“delight”) where the KJV prints the name Eden. 31:9, 31:16, 31:18 not placed.
10. `thou-art-the-anointed-cherub-or-thou-wast-with-the-cherub-ezekiel-28-14-greek` — 28:14 and 28:16; the cherub is the addressee in the KJV and a second party in the Greek.
11. `the-assyrian-was-a-cedar-in-lebanon-or-the-assyrian-was-a-cypress-in-libanus-ezekiel-31-3-greek` — 31:3.
12. `there-is-edom-and-all-the-zidonians-or-the-princes-of-assur-ezekiel-32-29-greek` — 32:29 and 32:30.
13. `i-will-destroy-the-fat-and-the-strong-or-i-will-guard-the-strong-ezekiel-34-16-greek` — 34:16; the verb differs.
14. `a-covenant-of-peace-with-them-or-with-david-ezekiel-34-25-greek` — 34:25; the KJV names David at 34:23–24 and the record says so; the Greek makes the covenant party David himself.
15. `the-stick-of-joseph-or-the-tribe-of-joseph-ezekiel-37-19-greek` — 37:19.
16. `one-king-shall-be-king-to-them-all-or-they-shall-have-one-prince-ezekiel-37-22-greek` — 37:22 and 37:24; the scope says the record does not claim that “prince” excludes the office of a king.
17. `the-chief-prince-of-meshech-and-tubal-or-rhos-prince-of-mesoch-and-thobel-ezekiel-38-2-greek` — 38:2 and 39:1; the Greek prints the Hebrew word as a name. The record says it does not claim whether the Hebrew word is a name.
18. `the-valley-of-hamongog-or-the-burial-place-of-gog-ezekiel-39-11` — witness **“Hebrew and Septuagint”**; 39:11 and 39:16. The KJV leaves “Hamongog” and “Hamonah” untranslated while the same verse renders the Hebrew word המונה “multitude”; this is a name-made-of-ordinary-words record under the rule decided for Immanuel. The Greek prints a noun of a different meaning (“burial-place”). The Hebrew limit is stated. 39:15 (same name) is not placed.
19. `posts-of-threescore-cubits-or-twenty-cubits-ezekiel-40-14-greek` — 40:14; the figure only (the Greek words the thing measured differently).
20. `the-breadth-eleven-cubits-or-the-breadth-twelve-cubits-ezekiel-40-49-greek` — 40:49 (the Greek’s “ten steps” is not part of the record).
21. `the-length-twenty-cubits-or-the-length-of-the-doors-forty-cubits-ezekiel-41-4-greek` — 41:4.
22. `the-breadth-ten-thousand-or-the-breadth-twenty-thousand-ezekiel-45-1-greek` — 45:1, 45:3, 45:5.
23. `for-twenty-chambers-or-cities-to-dwell-in-ezekiel-45-5-greek` — 45:5 (a second record at the same verse, a different word).
24. `out-of-two-hundred-or-out-of-ten-ezekiel-45-15-greek` — 45:15.
25. `ten-thousand-in-breadth-or-twenty-five-thousand-in-breadth-ezekiel-48-9-greek` — 48:9.

## Untagged conflicts (KJV only; Ezekiel’s temple offerings beside Numbers 28)

Issued here because Numbers is already decided (the later-book exception). All four state the reconciliation the verses allow (a different ordinance, a different offerer) and say the text does not report it.

- `six-lambs-and-a-ram-on-the-sabbath-or-two-lambs-on-the-sabbath-ezekiel-46-4` — Ezekiel 46:4 against Numbers 28:9.
- `a-young-bullock-six-lambs-and-a-ram-or-two-young-bullocks-one-ram-seven-lambs-ezekiel-46-6` — Ezekiel 46:6 against Numbers 28:11.
- `a-lamb-every-morning-or-two-lambs-morning-and-even-ezekiel-46-13` — Ezekiel 46:13 (and 46:14–15) against Numbers 28:3–4. The scope says the silence about the evening in Ezekiel is not asserted as a denial; only the three verses were searched by eye.
- `seven-bullocks-and-seven-rams-daily-or-two-young-bullocks-one-ram-seven-lambs-ezekiel-45-23` — Ezekiel 45:23 (and 45:22, sin offering) against Numbers 28:19 and 28:22.

## V

None. Ezekiel 44:22 (priests and widows) beside Leviticus 21:14 is an allowance, not an act clearly broken; no act is described.

## Verses compared

Every verse of chapters 25–48 was screened: the KJV verse against Brenton’s English (best match across the book, so reordered verses are caught), plus a number scan (number words) and a name scan (capitalised words) at the aligned verse. Only flagged verses, listed above or below, were read in Hebrew and Greek. The chapters were not read in full. For the untagged records the verses of Ezekiel 44–46 and Numbers 28 quoted in the records were read in the KJV.

## Considered and not issued (quotes read; do not re-issue)

- 25:5 (“Rabbah” against “the city of Ammon”) and 25:16 (“Cherethims” against “Cretans”): a name given in another form; left out.
- 27:13 (Javan, Tubal, Meshech against “Greece … the whole world … the adjacent coasts”), 27:19 (“Dan also and Javan” against “Out of Asel”), 27:23: Greek differs but the Hebrew words are obscure or a list is rearranged; no single plain difference to quote.
- 28:12 (“king of Tyrus … sealest up the sum” against “prince of Tyre … a seal of resemblance”): obscure Hebrew; the king/prince pair is covered at 37:22.
- 29:10 and 30:6 (“tower of Syene” against “Magdol and Syene”): the Hebrew word for tower is also a place name; the KJV’s choice lies inside its range.
- 30:15 (“No” against “Memphis”), 30:13–16 (other Egyptian city names), 27:25: a city printed under another name; the pair at 30:17 is the stronger record.
- 31:11 (“the mighty one of the heathen” against “the prince of the nations”) and 38:12 (“midst of the land” against “navel of the earth”): within the range of the Hebrew word.
- 39:2 (“the sixth part”), 36:23–38, 40:6 (“stairs” against “seven steps”), 41:6, 42:3–5, 45:12 (the maneh): Greek differs but the Hebrew is obscure or the text is much reordered. 45:12 has a clear number difference (KJV 20, 25, 15; Greek 5, 15, 50) but the sums are not stated in either text, so no record.
- Omissions not issued (earlier instruction): 25:10 (the Ammonites left out), 47:13 (“Joseph shall have two portions”), 40:39, 41:26, 46:12, 47:14.
- 44:2 and 46:1–12 (the shut east gate and the prince’s gate): the two passages can name different gates; no wording contrast.
- Spelling or form only (not issued): Tyre (Sor), Zidon (Sidon), Nebuchadrezzar (Nabuchodonosor), Zadok (Sadduc), the tribe names in 48 (Asser, Nephthalim and others), Hamath (Emath), Togarmah (Thogarma), Chebar (Chobar).
