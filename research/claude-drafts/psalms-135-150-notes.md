## Records (one line each: why it qualifies)

1. `they-that-wasted-us-required-mirth-or-they-that-carried-us-away-asked-a-hymn-greek` (137:3) — Greek names the second group by a word of leading away and the thing asked as a hymn; KJV "wasted us" / "mirth".
2. `thou-hast-magnified-thy-word-above-all-thy-name-or-thou-hast-magnified-thy-holy-name-above-every-thing-greek` (138:2) — KJV magnifies "thy word"; the Greek clause has no such word and magnifies the holy name.
3. `the-lord-will-perfect-that-which-concerneth-me-or-o-lord-thou-shalt-recompense-on-my-behalf-greek` (138:8) — "perfect" against a verb of recompense.
4. `there-is-not-a-word-in-my-tongue-or-there-is-no-unrighteous-word-in-my-tongue-greek` (139:4) — every word against "unrighteous word".
5. `if-i-take-the-wings-of-the-morning-or-if-i-take-up-my-wings-straight-greek` (139:9) — "of the morning" against a phrase rendered "straight forward".
6. `my-substance-was-not-hid-from-thee-or-my-bone-was-not-hid-from-thee-greek` (139:15) — first subject "substance" against "bone", with "substance" named separately.
7. `in-thy-book-all-my-members-were-written-or-in-thy-book-all-shall-be-written-greek` (139:16) — "all my members" against a bare plural "all".
8. `how-precious-also-are-thy-thoughts-unto-me-or-thy-friends-are-greatly-honoured-greek` (139:17) — "thoughts … sum of them" against "friends … their rule".
9. `thine-enemies-take-thy-name-in-vain-or-they-shall-take-thy-cities-in-vain-greek` (139:20) — "thy name" against "thy cities"; the Hebrew as printed has no word spelled שם (asserted by script).
10. `try-me-and-know-my-thoughts-or-examine-me-and-know-my-paths-greek` (139:23) — "thoughts" against "paths".
11. `let-me-not-eat-of-their-dainties-or-let-me-not-unite-with-their-choice-ones-greek` (141:4) — verb of eating and a thing eaten against a verb rendered "unite with" and "choice ones".
12. `it-shall-be-an-excellent-oil-which-shall-not-break-my-head-or-let-not-the-oil-of-the-sinner-anoint-my-head-greek` (141:5) — the oil accepted against the oil of the sinner refused; "calamities" against "pleasures".
13. `as-when-one-cutteth-and-cleaveth-wood-upon-the-earth-or-as-a-lump-of-earth-is-crushed-greek` (141:7) — a man cutting wood against a lump of earth broken; the Hebrew as printed has no word spelled עץ (asserted by script).
14. `that-our-oxen-may-be-strong-to-labour-or-their-oxen-are-fat-greek` (144:14) — "strong to labour" against "fat"; only the description of the oxen is the finding.
15. `the-lord-is-good-to-all-or-the-lord-is-good-to-those-that-wait-greek` (145:9) — "all" against "those that wait"; the scope says the Greek's second clause keeps "all his works".
16. `thy-kingdom-is-an-everlasting-kingdom-greek-adds-the-lord-is-faithful-in-his-words` (145:13) — Greek verse 13a; its second clause overlaps KJV 145:17, its first ("faithful in his words") is not in KJV 145:13, 145:14 or 145:17; the WLC verses 145:13 and 145:14 do not contain the letters of "faithful" (asserted by script).
17. `the-lord-openeth-the-eyes-of-the-blind-or-the-lord-giveth-wisdom-to-the-blind-greek` (146:8) — "openeth the eyes" against a verb rendered "gives wisdom", no word for eyes.
18. `who-maketh-grass-to-grow-upon-the-mountains-greek-adds-and-herb-for-the-service-of-men` (147:8) — Greek plus; the same sentence is at KJV Psalm 104:14, which the scope states (a statement in another passage does not disqualify an addition).

Quotation checks: every KJV quotation and every side quote was asserted by script against the packaged text; every Hebrew run against the WLC verse at its number; every Greek and English quotation against the fetched Brenton page at its printed label (including 13a). All 29 source addresses answered. Greek quotations are at Greek psalm numbers and the source labels say "(Greek numbering)".

## Considered and not issued

- 138:1 "gods" / "angels": same ground as 82:1, 97:7, 138:1 — decided under the T catch-up; not re-issued. The "angels" at 137:1 (Greek) is the same word.
- 139:19 "Surely thou wilt slay" / "Oh that thou wouldest slay": would rest on the range of the Hebrew particle; left out.

- 137:9 "stones" / "the rock" (Hebrew singular): a difference of number only; left out.

- 144:2 "fortress / high tower" against "refuge / helper", 144:12 "corner stones … palace" against "beautiful … temple", 145:5 "glorious honour" against "glorious majesty of thy holiness", 145:14, 147:9 ("which cry" against a participle rendered "call upon him": the pronoun's referent is not shown), 147:17, 148:12, 150:1: ranges of senses or wording only.

- 136: the 26 repeats of the refrain were not compared one by one; no number or name differs in the scripted scans. "Red sea" (136:13, 15) is the published Exodus record's subject (the Greek prints "Red Sea" as two words; no new record).
- 143:2 with Romans 3:20 and Galatians 2:16 ("no man living" / "no flesh"): Paul's sentence adds "by works of the law"; not a quotation formula; left out.
- Psalm 108 / 144 repeats of Psalm 18: the conflict belongs to Part 1, not issued.
- Psalm 151: Brenton's Greek page answers (`https://ebible.org/grcbrent/PSA151.htm`, 7 verses). It is outside the KJV, so there is no KJV verse to place a T record on and nothing for the KJV's English to differ from; left out and logged in `owner-questions.md`.
