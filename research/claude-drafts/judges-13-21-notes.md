## Considered and ruled out

- Samson and the Nazirite rule on dead bodies (14:8–9, 14:19, 15:15): Numbers 6 is not applied to him by name, and touching is not narrated. Not clearly broken.
- Samson's Philistine wife (14:3): Deuteronomy 7 names seven peoples, not Philistines; 14:4 says it "was of the LORD".
- Samson at Gaza (16:1): no law clearly covers the act.
- The daughters of Shiloh (21:21–23) against Exodus 21:16: the law's wording and its fit to this case are not clear.
- Jabesh-gilead (21:10–12): no specific law clearly broken.
- Gibeah (19:22–28): punished by the war that follows, so not unchecked.
- "A Levite of the family of Judah" (17:7): he "sojourned there"; residence, not descent, is a fair reading.
- Laish (18:29) against Leshem (Joshua 19:47): spelling.
- 1,100 shekels dedicated, 200 used (17:3–4): the text does not say the rest was withheld.
- "Captivity of the land" (18:30) beside "house of God in Shiloh" (18:31): dating, with no primary publication opened.
- Two defeats after God says "go up" (20:18–25): no victory was promised.
- Three hundred foxes; the gates of Gaza carried to Hebron: no science claim without a primary source.
- "Foxes" against jackals (15:4): the Hebrew word covers both.

## Owner ruling, 2026-10-12

A narrator's general remark counts as a "check" for V. Judges 17:6 ("every man did that which was right in his own eyes") therefore checks Micah's image, and the record carries no V tag. It was first drafted as `micah-graven-image-unchecked` with V; the ID was changed before any hand-off.
