# Ecclesiastes second pass (workstream D8), 2026-10-18

Scope: the 5 Ecclesiastes chapters with no published record: 4, 5, 6, 7, 11. Method: KJV and Brenton's English read side by side for every verse of the five chapters; the Hebrew (bolls.life WLC) and Brenton's Greek (ebible.org) read at every verse that raised a question. All records are drafts until Codex accepts them.

## Issued (8 records, `ecclesiastes-second-pass-records.json`), all T, witness Septuagint, status Context
1. `before-the-angel-that-it-was-an-error-or-in-the-presence-of-god-it-was-ignorance-greek` (KJV 5:6): "before the angel" against "in the presence of God".
2. `god-answereth-him-in-the-joy-of-his-heart-or-god-troubles-him-in-the-joy-of-his-heart-greek` (KJV 5:20): "answereth" against περισπᾷ ("troubles" in Brenton's English).
3. `nor-known-any-thing-or-nor-known-rest-greek` (6:5): the object of "known".
4. `a-thousand-years-twice-told-or-the-returns-of-a-thousand-years-greek` (6:6): the doubling is not in the Greek (the figure candidate left open in the first Ecclesiastes notes).
5. `a-gift-destroyeth-the-heart-or-oppression-destroys-his-noble-heart-greek` (7:7).
6. `withdraw-not-thine-hand-or-defile-not-thine-hand-greek` (7:18).
7. `walk-in-the-sight-of-thine-eyes-or-walk-blameless-and-not-in-the-sight-of-thine-eyes-greek` (11:9): Greek adds "blameless" and negates the second phrase.
8. `childhood-and-youth-are-vanity-or-youth-and-folly-are-vanity-greek` (11:10).

- Hebrew from the fetched WLC verse, Greek and Brenton's English from the fetched ebible pages; the page footer text was stripped. Every `sides` quote was asserted against the KJV verse by script. IDs checked against data/evidence.json: none exist. `checked` 2026-10-18.
- Records are about printed wording only. None claims which reading is original, and none glosses a Hebrew word beyond the words quoted. In records 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 the Hebrew word is named but not defined.

## Considered, not issued
- 4:4 ("is envied of his neighbour" / "this is a man's envy from his neighbour"): the Hebrew construction is ambiguous about direction; no firm difference.
- 4:14 ("born in his kingdom" / "was in his kingdom"), 4:8 ("child" / "son"), 5:3 ("business" / "trial"), 6:8, 7:12, 7:16 ("destroy thyself" / "be confounded"; already ruled out in the first pass), 11:5 ("spirit" / "wind"; a word with a range of senses): not issued.
- 7:17 ("foolish" / "stubborn"): a word difference, but a lexicon would be needed to show the Hebrew means more than one of them; held.
- 5:17 (KJV "eateth in darkness"): Brenton's Greek 5:16 prints no verb for eating; left as a lead (omission), not read against other Greek editions.
- 7:29 and 4:17: Brenton's English numbering/placement differs from the Hebrew at chapter ends; no wording issue.
