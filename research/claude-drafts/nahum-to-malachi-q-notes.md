# Nahum to Malachi — Q notes (2026-10-14)

All drafts until Codex accepts them. 3 Q records in `nahum-to-malachi-q-records.json`, each with a side A on the key verse:

- `these-are-the-two-anointed-ones-who-are-the-two-anointed-ones-zechariah-4-14` — “the two anointed ones” — who are they?
- `this-is-a-woman-that-sitteth-in-the-midst-of-the-ephah-who-or-what-is-she-zechariah-5-7` — “a woman that sitteth in the midst of the ephah” — who or what is she?
- `the-lord-hath-prepared-a-sacrifice-he-hath-bid-his-guests-who-are-the-guests-zephaniah-1-7` — “he hath bid his guests” — who is the sacrifice and who are the guests?

Each follows the Q contract: the passage leaves the identity open, readings are named only as readings, and none is chosen. None repeats an existing Q (checked against `data/evidence.json` and the drafts; the existing Q records on Zechariah are at 1:12, 3:1–2 and 7:1).

Considered and left out: Malachi 4:5 (Elijah — the verse names him); Zechariah 6:11–12 (the Branch — Zechariah 3:8 names him; a Q would argue from silence); Haggai 2:7 (the desire of all nations: a question about reading, not an omission); Habakkuk 2:2–3 (the vision: the book’s opening names it).
