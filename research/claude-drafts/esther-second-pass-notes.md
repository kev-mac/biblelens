## Issued (5 records, `esther-second-pass-records.json`; all T, witness Septuagint, status Context, none V)

- `esther-drew-near-and-touched-the-sceptre-or-the-king-laid-it-on-her-neck-esther-5-2-greek` (5:2): who performs the gesture.
- `she-obtained-favour-in-his-sight-or-god-changed-the-spirit-of-the-king-esther-5-1-greek` (5:2, Greek 5:1e): God is the actor on the king. Extends the published `god-named-in-the-greek-of-esther-where-the-kjv-names-none` (2:20, 4:8, 6:1, 6:13), which does not cover 5:1e; Codex may merge the two.
- `i-had-held-my-tongue-or-i-disobeyed-the-adversary-is-not-worthy-of-the-court-esther-7-4-greek` (7:4): verb and reason differ; no lexicon opened, scope says so.
- `laid-a-tribute-upon-the-land-and-the-isles-or-wrote-upon-the-kingdom-of-land-and-sea-esther-10-1-greek` (10:1): earlier Esther notes listed this as a known difference but issued no record.
- `mordecai-next-unto-the-king-or-took-over-from-king-artaxerxes-esther-10-3-greek` (10:3): rank wording only; royal name left to the published Ahasuerus/Artaxerxes record.

## Considered and ruled out

- 5:3, 5:6, 7:2 "half of the kingdom": Greek agrees.
- 5:14 and 7:9 gallows fifty cubits: Greek agrees; 5:14 Zeresh/Zosara and 7:9 Harbonah/Bugathan are name spellings.
- 7:9 "Hang him thereon" against Greek σταυρωθήτω: verb range, no lexicon opened, no statement clash.
- 7:6 "adversary and enemy" against "man, an enemy": same sense.
- 7:8 "force the queen": Greek βιάζῃ agrees in sense.
- 5:9–13 Haman's fury and boast: Greek abridges, no clash.
- 10:3a–3l (Mordecai's dream interpretation, colophon): Greek-only addition with no KJV verse to set against; Ptolemy/Cleopatra colophon dating not compared with anything here.
