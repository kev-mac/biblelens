# Jeremiah 30–52 — notes for Codex (2026-10-13)

All records are drafts until Codex accepts them. `jeremiah-30-52-records.json`: 17 records (16 T, all witness "Septuagint"; 1 untagged). All carry `sides`. Q is in `jeremiah-30-52-q-records.json` (3 Q, own notes file).

## Verse numbers
KJV and Hebrew (WLC) numbers agree at every verse quoted. Brenton's Greek orders the book differently: KJV 30–31 = Greek 37–38; KJV 32–36 = Greek 39–43 (same verse numbers); KJV 41 = 48; KJV 43 = 50; KJV 46 = 26; KJV 48 = 31; KJV 50 = 27; KJV 51 = 28. Each record's scope says where the Greek prints the verse. Verse numbers inside the Greek chapters agree with the KJV at every verse quoted.

## Verses compared
Every verse of Jeremiah 30–52 was screened in the KJV against Brenton's English across the whole book (best-match, so reordered verses are caught); about 430 verses below the similarity cut were read. The Hebrew and Greek were read only at the verses quoted above and at the candidates ruled out below. This is a screen and not a claim that the chapters were read in full in Hebrew and Greek.

## Considered and not issued (do not log again)
- Whole verses Brenton's Greek omits (33:14–26, 39:4–13 in the Greek of ch. 46, 46:25, 48:45 and others): omitted-verse records are not issued (README, "Do not issue another omitted-verse record"). 33:15 (Branch), 31:15 (Rahel; Matthew 2:18) and 31:31–34 (Hebrews 8) were not compared with the New Testament Greek and remain open for those books.
- Wording or range-of-senses only: 30:13 ("plead thy cause"/"judge"), 30:17 ("Outcast"/"Dispersed"), 31:2, 31:20, 31:25, 34:18, 35:19, 42:10 ("repent"/"ceased"), 48:11 ("lees"/"glory"), 49:28 and 49:33 ("Hazor"/"the palace"), 51:41 ("praise"/"boast"), 46:14 ("round about"/"yew-tree").
- Names that differ only by transliteration (41:17 Chimham, 49:3, 48:1, 48:46) and the Red sea at 49:21 (Exodus ruling).
- Jeremiah 52 (parallel of 2 Kings 25): 52:12, 22, 25, 28, 31 issued in the 2 Kings session; 52:11 "grinding-house" appears in Brenton's Greek and was not issued as T (parallel 2 Kings 25:7 not compared with the Greek).
- Jeremiah 36:30 with 2 Kings 24:6 and 33:17: already published (`jehoiakim-none-to-sit-on-the-throne-or-his-son-reigned`). Jeremiah 34:3 with Ezekiel 12:13 ("shall behold his eyes" / "shall not see it") was left out: Ezekiel 12:13 does not name Zedekiah in the verse, so the clash would depend on an inferred referent. Left for the Ezekiel session to note.
- No V: the breaches told (34:8–17 release of servants; 44 incense to the queen of heaven; 32:35 Molech) are rebuked in the passage.
