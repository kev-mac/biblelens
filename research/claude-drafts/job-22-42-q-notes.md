# Job 22–42 — Q records, notes

File: `job-22-42-q-records.json` (3 records, all `mode: "questions"`, `tags: ["Q"]`, `status: "Context"`). All are drafts until Codex accepts them. Written 2026-10-13. Job is done in two parts; this is part 2 (chapters 22–42) and finishes the book.

## Why each qualifies

- `elihu-the-son-of-barachel-why-is-he-not-named-in-the-lords-verdict` (32:2, 42:7) — an unstated outcome: Elihu is introduced as angry with Job and with the three friends, is the only speaker introduced in chapters 32–37, and is in neither group of the verdict at 42:7. It decides how six chapters are to be read.
- `the-thing-that-is-right-as-my-servant-job-hath-which-words-of-job` (42:7) — an unstated referent: the Lord approves Job’s speech and names no speech, after challenging Job’s words at 38:2, 40:2 and 40:8.
- `behemoth-and-leviathan-what-creatures-are-they` (40:15, 41:1) — an unstated identity: two creatures carry the Lord’s second answer, and the verses quoted do not say what kind of creature either is.

## Points for Codex

- **Verse numbers.** KJV Job 41:1 is 40:25 in the Hebrew edition (WLC) and in Brenton’s Greek. The behemoth and leviathan record quotes the Hebrew of KJV 41:1 from `https://bolls.life/get-text/WLC/18/40/` and says so. Its `refs` and `sides` use KJV numbers.
- **The Elihu record quotes the Greek of 38:1**, which names Elihu where the KJV verse does not. The scope says so and says the Greek too has the Lord speak “to Job”, so no quoted text states a reading.
- **Two word-search claims are asserted by script**: “Elihu” stands in the KJV of Job at 32:2, 32:4, 32:5, 32:6, 34:1, 35:1 and 36:1 only; and in chapters 32–37 the only lines that introduce a speaker (“answered … and said”, “spake … and said”, “proceeded, and said”) are 32:6, 34:1, 35:1 and 36:1, all naming Elihu. “behemoth” stands at Job 40:15 only; “leviathan” at Job 41:1, Psalms 74:14, Psalms 104:26 and Isaiah 27:1.
- **Overlap.** Job 42:7 carries the published `exp-late-17-42-7` (“The book corrects its own speakers”). Neither Q repeats it: one asks about Elihu, the other asks which of Job’s words are approved. Both scopes say so. Job 41:1 is a context verse of the part 1 draft `ready-to-raise-up-their-mourning-or-the-great-sea-monster-greek`.
- The Elihu record has two side-A entries and places its tag on two verses (32:2 and 42:7), as accepted in earlier Q records.
- The behemoth and leviathan record sits beside two G records of this batch on the same verses (40:15 and 41:1). It asks a different question and says so.

## Considered and not issued

- Whom 38:2 addresses — folded into the Elihu record as one of its readings; 38:1 says the Lord answered Job.
- Why Zophar has no third speech, and who speaks chapter 28 — narrative brevity and a matter of how the poem is divided; neither is a stated gap in a passage.
- Job’s second set of children (42:13) beside the doubling of his goods (42:10) — no statement in the text is left unexplained; a number is simply not doubled.
- The “captivity” of Job (42:10) — a wording matter, not a gap.
- Satan’s absence from the epilogue — the part 1 question on Satan’s standing covers the same matter.
