## Records

1. `the-ethiopian-woman-whom-moses-had-married-who-was-she` — Numbers 12:1. The marriage is given as the occasion of the episode, but the woman is not named, the time of the marriage is not given and the objection is given no words.
2. `the-book-of-the-wars-of-the-lord-what-was-it` — Numbers 21:14. The narrative cites a named written source; the passage gives a title and a fragment and nothing else.
3. `the-thirty-and-two-persons-given-as-tribute-to-the-priest-what-became-of-them` — Numbers 31:40–41. Persons are levied and handed to the priest in the same words as the animals; the outcome is not stated.
