# Mark — Q notes (2026-10-14)

All drafts until Codex accepts them. 3 Q records in `mark-1-16-q-records.json`, each with a side A on the key verse:

- `a-certain-young-man-with-a-linen-cloth-who-was-he-mark-14-51` — “a certain young man, having a linen cloth cast about his naked body” — who was he?
- `let-him-that-readeth-understand-who-is-the-reader-mark-13-14` — “let him that readeth understand” — who is the reader, and what is he to understand?

Each follows the Q contract: the passage leaves the point open, readings are named only as readings, and none is chosen. Every absence claim is a whole-word search of the packaged KJV and says so (“young man” in Matthew–John; “readeth” in the whole Bible; “salted” in the whole Bible). None repeats an existing Q (checked against `data/evidence.json` and the drafts).

Considered and left out: Mark 4:11 (the mystery of the kingdom: the verse states its own distinction); 1:13 (the forty days: ordinary omitted detail); 14:41 (“it is enough”: a question of the word, not of the text); 6:48 (“would have passed by them”: depends on a reading of the verb); 16:9–20 (a textual matter, not a Q).
