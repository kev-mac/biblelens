# Explore card drafts

Drafts only. Source: `src/atlas-explore.js`, each card’s `copy` field. IDs are route IDs. Includes the deferred Prophecies and Method & Sources cards.

## places — Places

**Original:** Examine place references, proposed identifications and the geography of biblical texts.

**Draft:** Explore Bible place references, suggested matches and the land described in the texts.

**Meaning flag:** Suggested matches retains proposed identifications; it does not present a location match as certain.

## people — People

**Original:** Examine named figures, genealogies and relationships described in the texts.

**Draft:** Explore named people, family lines and relationships described in the texts.

**Meaning flag:** None.

## stories — Stories

**Original:** Analyze plot, literary structure and recurring themes across biblical narratives.

**Draft:** Study plots, how Bible stories are shaped, and themes that repeat across them.

**Meaning flag:** None.

## categories — Evidence

**Original:** Compare textual claims, interpretations and attributed historical or linguistic evidence.

**Draft:** Compare text claims, how they're read, and sourced evidence about history or language.

**Meaning flag:** Sourced preserves attribution without implying that the evidence is verified. How they are read means interpretations, not a single settled meaning.

## timeline — Timeline

**Original:** Compare narrative sequences and proposed dates, with disputed chronologies identified.

**Draft:** Compare story order and proposed dates, with disputed orders and dates marked.

**Meaning flag:** None.

## commandments — Commandments

**Original:** Examine the 613 traditionally enumerated commandments by subject and textual reference.

**Draft:** Explore the traditional list of 613 commandments by subject and where each appears in texts.

**Meaning flag:** None.

## prophecies — Prophecies

**Original:** Examine prophetic texts, later interpretations and proposed correspondences.

**Draft:** Study prophecy texts, later readings of their meaning, and suggested links.

**Meaning flag:** Suggested links preserves proposed correspondences without adding an event, fulfilment claim or level of certainty.

## method — Method & Sources

**Original:** Review source attribution, editorial decisions and gaps in coverage.

**Draft:** Review credited sources, choices made in editing and gaps in coverage.

**Meaning flag:** None.
