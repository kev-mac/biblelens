**Original:** Peter has a vision of unclean animals and is sent to the centurion Cornelius. The Spirit falls on the Gentile household, and the Jerusalem church accepts it.

**Draft:** Peter has a vision of animals unclean under ritual rules. He is sent to the centurion Cornelius, an army officer. The Spirit falls on the Gentile, or non-Jewish, household, and the Jerusalem church accepts it.

**Estimated FK grade:** 9.34 → 9.08

**Meaning flag:** Unclean is explained as ritual status, not dirt or illness. Gentile means non-Jewish; the acceptance retains the original “it.”
